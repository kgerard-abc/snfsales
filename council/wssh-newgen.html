WSSH / NewGen: applications to take over 3 Rhode Island nursing homes

Filed March 31, 2026. Pending completeness review.

Each applicant belongs to Cindat Tenant Holdings LLC, which belongs to WSSH LLC (manager: Daniel Katz). WSSH was formed to buy 175 nursing homes, mostly on the East Coast, in one deal. The application says its leadership comes from NewGen LLC, which runs homes in California, Washington, and Nevada, and that NewGen's Aaron Robin and Abe Tress set up and benefit from the two trusts named in it.
