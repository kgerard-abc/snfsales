Shelby trusts / TL Management families: applications to take over 2 Rhode Island nursing homes

Filed July 24, 2026. Pending completeness review.

The operating companies belong to family trusts of Jack Shelby. The buildings belong to the Lichtschein, Scheiner, Kohn, Meisner/Rodkin, and Stern families (TL Management). Skilled Nursing News reported in May 2026 that Shelby trusts were buying CommuniCare's 18 Maryland nursing homes, and that Shelby owns nursing homes in Florida and Texas.
