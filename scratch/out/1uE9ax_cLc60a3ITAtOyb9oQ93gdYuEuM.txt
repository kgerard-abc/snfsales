===== PAGE 3 =====
                        Background
• The Applicants seek CEC approval:
   – to transfer operations of Sunny View Nursing Home (“Sunny
      View”) and South County Nursing & Rehabilitation Center
      (“South County”) (collectively, the “Facilities”) from the current
      operators, 83 Corona Street Eden Operations LLC and South
      County Eden Operations LLC, respectively, to Sunny View Opco
      LLC and South County Opco LLC (the “New Operators”); and
   – to transfer the real estate housing Sunny View and South County
      from the current owner, 83 Corona Street Eden Realty LLC, and
      780 Oak Hill Road Eden Realty, LLC to Sunny View Propco
      LLC and to South County Propco LLC, respectively. (the “New
      Real Estate Owners”).
• Sunny View is a 57-bed skilled nursing facility located at 83 Corona
  Street, Warwick, RI.
• South County is a 120-bed skilled nursing facility located at 780 Oak
  Hill Road, North Kingstown, RI.
                                                                           3

===== PAGE 4 =====
                  The Transaction
• Pursuant to Operations Transfer Agreements and Asset
  Purchase Agreements, dated as of April 13, 2026:
   – The New Operators will acquire the operations at the
     Facilities; and
   – The New Real Estate Owners will acquire the Real Estate
     housing the Facilities.
• The New Operators will lease the real estate from the New
  Real Estate Owners pursuant to a lease agreement (Tab
  24).
• The purchase price for the operations and real estate
  housing the Facilities is $26,000,000.

===== PAGE 5 =====
               Facilities Post-Closing
• The Facilities offer a full range of nursing care and
  social services. They also provide specialty programs
  including rehabilitation therapy programs (physical,
  occupational and speech language), health management
  (nutrition, diabetes care, wound care), and support
  services.
   – The New Operators will continue to provide quality,
     rehabilitation and long-term nursing care to residents of the
     Facilities.
   – The New Operators intend to retain current leadership and
     staff, including the Administrators and Directors of
     Nursing.
• There will be no change in services at the Facilities.

===== PAGE 12 =====
                   The New Operators
• The New Operators’ sole member is RI2 Opco Holdco LLC (“RI2 Opco”), a
  Delaware LLC. The members of RI2 Opco are Chevra RI Opco Holdings
  LLC (“Chevra Opco”) (45%) and Givir RI2 Opco LLC (“Givir Opco”)
  (55%).
• Nosson Stein is the sole member of Givir Opco and 55% indirect owner of
  the New Operators. Mr. Stein is an experienced long term care operator
  who has owned and operated Ross Opco LLC d/b/a Ross Center for
  Nursing and Rehabilitation (“Ross Center”), a 135-bed skilled nursing
  facility in Brentwood, New York, since September 2022, following a
  period of operational leadership at the facility beginning December 2020.
  In his role at Ross Center, Mr. Stein is responsible for overseeing all
  aspects of facility operations, including compliance, financial
  performance, and quality assurance.
• Prior to his role at Ross Center, Mr. Stein had leadership roles with
  Champion Care operating nursing homes in Michigan, Wisconsin, and
  Minnesota and directed business operations for Personal Healthcare, a
  long-term care organization with facilities in New York and
  Massachusetts.
• The New Operators in the present transaction will provide the same
  dedication and high-quality care provided at Ross Center.
