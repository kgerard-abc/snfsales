===== SLIDE 5 =====
The Transaction
‹#›
Pursuant to an Asset Purchase Agreement and Amendment thereto, effective October 15, 2021, (the “APA”) by and between the current Health Concepts property owners and the Health Concepts operators, on one hand, and Tryko affiliated entities, on the other hand, the Health Concepts entities agreed to sell the real property for the ten Health Concepts Facilities to Tryko affiliated entities.
Pursuant to ten Operation Transfer Agreements effective July 8, 2021 (“OTAs”), between each Health Concepts operator and Tryko affiliated entity, the respective Health Concept operators agreed to transfer to the Tryko affiliated entities all right, title, and interest to the nursing home beds and accompanying operational rights at the time of closing. 
At closing, the Tryko entities that are party to OTAs will assign their interests in the OTA to the Applicants.
Following the closing, the new property owners will enter into lease agreements with the new operating entities, the Applicants, for each facility. 
In addition, the Applicants will enter into an Administrative Services Agreement with Marquis Health Consulting, LLC for assistance with administrative services and Clinical Care Consulting for assistance with clinical planning and strategy. 


PKR
===== SLIDE 6 =====
The Transaction
Following execution of the original APA and OTAs, Tryko identified Kadima Healthcare Group, Inc. (“Kadima”) to own and operate the remaining six Health Concepts facilities. 
Kadima is an experienced skilled nursing facility operator currently operating six facilities in Pennsylvania. 
As a result, Tryko and the Kadima affiliated entities entered into a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (“MIPA”), effective October 15, 2021, pursuant to which Kadima will acquire Tryko’s membership interest in the six Tryko operating entities at closing.
CEC applications for the six proposed Kadima facilities were filed contemporaneously with the CEC applications for the proposed Tryko facilities, in November, 2021.
As a result of the Transaction, the Applicants will operate 516 beds formerly operated by Health Concepts and the Kadima entities will operate 584 beds.

The Applicants
The Applicants’ affiliates have a proven history of providing high-quality long-term care services to their nursing facility residents. 
The Applicant and its affiliates are third-generation operators of skilled nursing and assisted living facilities.
The Applicants are part of a long-standing family of 55 nursing and assisted living facilities operating in 7 states: Rhode Island, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Florida and Virginia. 
In Rhode Island, the Applicants’ affiliates own and operate:
Elmhurst Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center, a 206 bed in Providence, Rhode Island; 
Lincolnwood Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center, a 220 bed SNF in North Providence, Rhode Island; and
Bayview Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center at Scalabrini, a 120 bed SNF in North Kingstown, Rhode Island
Harmony Hospice Care, a licensed Hospice provider. 
The affiliates are fully experienced with the Rhode Island market and maintain an exemplary record among the State’s long-term care providers.

===== SLIDE 17 =====
Character, Competence, Commitment, and Standing in the Community 
‹#›
The Applicants and their affiliates are third-generation operators; currently with 55 skilled nursing and assisted living facilities in 7 states.
Elmhurst Rehabilitation & Healthcare Center has been affiliated with the Applicant since 2016 and maintains a solid and consistent reputation for competence and commitment to the community. 
Elmhurst received a $1 million EOHHS Transformation grant for a vent unit. 
Lincolnwood and Bayview were recently acquired by the Applicant’s affiliate and under its operation, are providing quality care to the community. 
Bayview was recently selected as a high performing facility by U.S. News & World Report. 
Lincolnwood opened a subacute rehabilitation unit in 2021 designed to help patients recover from surgery or illness and return home.
Lincolnwood is in the process of developing an inhouse Dialysis center for its residents.
Harmony Hospice started serving Rhode Island residents in 2021 and continues to provide compassionate palliative and end of life care.
The Applicants and their affiliates are in good standing and are dedicated to providing quality services to residents across jurisdictions. 
Kathleen Heren, Rhode Island State Long Term Care Ombudsman: “My office would like to write a letter of support for Marquis in the purchase of the [Health Concepts Homes].”

===== SLIDE 18 =====
The Applicant Will Continue to Provide Safe and Adequate Treatment 
‹#›
The Applicants will continue to provide high quality care previously offered by Health Concepts. 
Approval of the CECs will allow for expansion of services currently provided, including significant renovations and necessary building upgrades.
The Applicants will maintain an ongoing facility-wide Quality Assurance and Performance Improvement (“QAPI”) Committee at the Facility to oversee the QAPI Plan (Tab 22). 
The QAPI Plan details the facility’s activities and objectives designed to monitor and evaluate the quality of resident care, pursue methods to improve care quality, and resolve identified problems.
