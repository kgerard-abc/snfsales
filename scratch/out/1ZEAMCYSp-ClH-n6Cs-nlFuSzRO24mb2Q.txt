
===== SLIDE 1 =====
Change in Effective Control
Con-V-Care Inc.
d/b/a Woonsocket Health & Rehabilitation Centre
===== SLIDE 2 =====
Background
WHRC has been owned exclusively by the Pezzelli family since it first opened in 1975.
Norma Pezzelli became sole owner in 1986.
WHRC provides long and short stay skilled nursing care, dementia care, and hospice. 
The organization has an established record of excellence; it has routinely earned CMS ratings of 4 stars or higher.
The facility is accredited by the Joint Commission.
WHRC has built a strong relationship with the City of Woonsocket through its many years of service to the community’s elderly as well as by employing thousands of its residents.
WHRC is active in the community through its support for education, the Arts, senior services and recreation leagues, the homeless, and many other activities. 
===== SLIDE 3 =====
Reason for Change in Effective Control
Norma Pezzelli died in 2018.
All shares of Con-V-Care were left in trust as part of her estate.
The Pezzelli children are beneficiaries of the trust.
In order for the beneficiaries to continue to operate the company, and for the estate to be closed, the shares must be distributed to them out of the trust.
The distribution of the shares to the beneficiaries constitutes the change of ownership.
===== SLIDE 4 =====
Effects of the Change in Effective Control
This change in effective control will not affect the day-to-day operations of the facility. 
The facility will continue to be owned and operated by the Pezzelli family.
WHRC will continue to provide all the same high-quality nursing and rehabilitative services to  patients and residents without
 
regard to race, ethnicity, or sexual orientation. Providing safe and adequate treatment to those entrusted to our care remains our highest priority.
There will be no change of administrators or key staff members.
N
o additional debt or equity commitments are required to make this change.
The financial condition of the facility will not be impacted or changed.
