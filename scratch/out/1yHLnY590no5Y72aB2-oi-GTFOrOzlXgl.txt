I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of Violet SNF
Holdco LLC [sole member, Violet SNF LLC] for a Change in Effective Control
(“CEC”) of Pawtucket Falls Healthcare Center [RI License LTC 00813] (“Pawtucket
Falls”), a licensed nursing facility located at 70 Gill Avenue in Pawtucket, Rhode Island,
be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

Violet SNF Holdco LLC is seeking CEC approval to acquire the operations of the 154-bed
nursing home located in Pawtucket.

As presented, Pawtucket Falls is currently under the management and supervision of Mark
Russo, Esq., solely in his capacity as the Court Appointed Receiver for Pawtucket SNF
Operator, LLC (the “Receiver”) pursuant to an order entered into by the Rhode Island Superior
Court on May 15, 2023, in the matter of Pawtucket SNF Operator, LLC d/b/a Pawtucket Falls
Health Care Center, PC -2023-02239 (the “Receivership Proceeding”). Pawtucket SNF
Operator LLC (“Pawtucket SNF Operator”) is the licensed operator of the Facility.

In tandem with a change in operations, Gill Street LLC is purchasing the Pawtucket Falls real
estate from current owner, Pawtucket SNF Real Estate, LLC.

On October 12, 2023, Rhode Island Superior Court Justice Brian P. Stern, entered an order in
the Receivership Proceeding, approving a petition to sell all of the assets which comprise
Pawtucket Falls, including the real estate and all improvements located at 70 Gill Avenue,
Pawtucket, Rhode Island, pursuant to an Operations Transfer Agreement by and between the
Receiver and Violet SNF Holdco LLC, effective October 12, 2023, subject to CEC approval
and Contract of Sale by and between Receiver and Violet SNF Holdco LLC.

The Applicant has paid a $500,000.00 operational deposit to the Receiver allowing the
Receivership Estate to offset operational losses prior to CEC approval in exchange for a first
security lien on Pawtucket Falls’ receivables.

At closing, Gill Street LLC and Violet SNF Holdco LLC will assume the obligations of
Pawtucket SNF Real Estate, LLC and Pawtucket SNF Operations, LLC under an existing
promissory note with the principal amount due and owing being $9,000,000.00.

The Receiver engaged Violet SNF Holdco LLC under an Interim Advisory Services Agreement
to provide advisory services.

Following CEC approval, the Receiver and Violet SNF Holdco LLC plan to present to the
Court a transition plan for the first six months of operations to be monitored by the Court.

III.    INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 23-17-14.3 and 23-17-14.4, titled "Licensing
of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed a CEC application of the subject-licensed facility.
This request is being made because the statute requires that any proposed change in owner,
operator, or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council and approved
by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the review would commence on November 18, 2023. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by December
2, 2023, when practicable. There was one (1) written public comment.

review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators, or directors of the health care facility.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that the request for a CEC application, be
approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to Pawtucket SNF Operator, LLC d/b/a Pawtucket
Falls Health Care Center and the issuance of a new nursing facility license to Violet SNF
Holdco LLC d/b/a Pawtucket Falls Healthcare Center.

VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the Applicant has paid a $500,000.00 operational deposit to the Receiver to
          assist the Receivership Estate with offsetting operational losses incurred prior to
          CEC approval in exchange for a first security lien on Pawtucket Falls’
          receivables. At closing, Gill Street LLC and Violet SNF Holdco LLC will
          assume the obligations of Pawtucket SNF Real Estate, LLC and Pawtucket SNF
          Operations, LLC under an existing promissory note with the principal amount
          due being $9,000,000.00;

       2. that all post-receivership-petition operational and other Court-approved
          administrative fees and expenses have been satisfied by and through an Order
          entered by the Superior Court;

       3. that the application be implemented as approved;

       4. that the Applicant shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
          employees prior to employment;

       5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization, and demographic
          resident information, be timely furnished to the state agency, upon request;

       6. that the Applicant provide services to all residents without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay; and

       7. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
          alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
          Further that the Applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge the
          patient while an application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid) is
          pending.
