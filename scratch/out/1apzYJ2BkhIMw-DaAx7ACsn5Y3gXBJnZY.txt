                        The Transaction
• The Operations Applicants will acquire the operations of the Facilities
  pursuant to an Operations Transfer Agreement by and between the
  Current Operators and Operations Applicants, dated as of August 7, 2025
  (Tab 14).
• The Real Estate Applicants will acquire the real estate of the Facilities
  pursuant to an Asset Purchase Agreement by and between the Current Real
  Estate Owners and the New Real Estate Owners dated as of Augst 7, 2025
  (Tab 14).
• The Operations Applicants will lease the real estate from the Real Estate
  Applicants following closing of the Proposed Transaction pursuant to lease
  agreements (Tab 24).
• The purchase price for the operations and real estate of the 5 Facilities is
  $75,000,000.

===== PAGE 15 =====
                        The Applicants
• The principals of the Applicants, Yosef Meystel, Ben Berkowitz, and
  David Berkowitz own and operate the licensed facilities AdviniaCare
  Pawtucket Pleasant Rehab Center, LLC and AdviniaCare Providence
  Dodge Rehab Center, LLC.
    – RIDOH approved the CEC applications for the operations of those
      facilities on January 31, 2025.
    – An affiliated entity of the Applicants began providing management
      services at those facilities pursuant to a RIDOH-approved
      management agreement since February of 2023.
    – Since their management of those facilities, the annual survey
      outcomes for the facilities significantly improved, the operators
      prioritized decreasing turnover in key positions, the operators were
      able to eliminate all agency staff and worked collaboratively with the
      unions in those facilities and have infused capital into the facilities.
• The Operations Applicants in the present transaction will provide the
  same dedication to the Facilities upon approval of the CEC Applications.
• Combined, Yosef Meystel, Ben Berkowitz, and David Berkowitz own
  and/or operate a total of 111 licensed facilities in 8 states.

===== PAGE 16 =====
               Facilities Post-Closing
• All current leadership, including the Administrators and
  Directors of Nursing will remain at the Facilities following
  closing.
• The Facilities each offer a full range of nursing care and
  social services. They also provide specialty programs
  including rehabilitation therapy programs (physical,
  occupational and speech language), health management
  (nutrition, diabetes care, wound care), recreational therapy,
  and support services.
   – The Operation Applicants will continue to provide quality, short-
     term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care to residents of the
     Facilities.
   – There will be no change in services.
• The Operations Applicants will continue to provide safe and
  adequate treatment.

   Character, Commitment, Competence, and Standing
                  in the Community
• The Applicant’s principals have vast experience in owning and
  operating skilled nursing facilities and have owned two Rhode Island

  to provide high-quality care to all of the residents at the five Athena
  affiliated facilities.
