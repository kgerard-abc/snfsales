Pursuant to the Purchase and Sale Agreement (“PSA”) executed as of July 7, 2025, by and between
Greenwood Propco LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Real Estate Seller”) and Greenwoo
d RI Propco LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Real Estate Buyer” or “Applicant”), Real
Estate Seller agreed to sell the property at 1139 Main Street, Warwick, Rhode Island 02886 (the “Pr
operty”) to the Applicant. The Property houses Greenwood Center, a 130 bed skilled nursing facility
(the “Facility”) operated by Harborside Rhode Island Limited Partnership, a Massachusetts limited
partnership (“Harborside” or the “Existing Operator”) pursuant to a Master Lease Agreem
ent between Real Estate Seller, as Landlord, and Harborside, as Tenant. Pursuant to an Operati
ons Transfer Agreement between Harborside, and Greenwood Operations RI LLC (“Gree
nwood Operations” or the “New Operator”) and Genesis Healthcare, Inc. solely for the purpo
ses of Section 5.8 regarding indemnification guarantees, the Existing Operator agreed to transf
er operations of the Facility to the New Operator. The Existing Operator is part of the pending Gene
sis Healthcare, Inc. Bankruptcy Proceeding in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Nort
hern District of Texas, case number 25-80185.

This CEC Application is filed on behalf of the Applicant seeking approval to acquire the real estate
that houses the Facility. The purchase price for the property is $7,250,000 with a $500,000 deposit.
The Applicant is owned by Jacob Taub (50%) and Menachem (“Pinny”) Kofman (50%). See the PSA
at Tab 14.

Mr. Taub is a managing partner of The Wellstone Group (“Wellstone”) and serves as the 50% owner
of the Applicant. In 2013, Mr. Taub, along with his partners, formed Wellstone. In 2018, Mr. Taub
and his partners recognized opportunities in the Skilled Nursing Facility arena and began focusing
on healthcare transactions. Since its first healthcare acquisition in 2018, Wellstone has continued to
gradually grow significantly in the healthcare space and currently owns and operates 40 facilities
consisting of over 4,500 Beds with an approximate Market Value over $600MM. Mr. Taub and his
team have largely focused operations in Arkansas (26 Facilities) and Michigan (12 Facilities) with
partnerships in Indiana (1 Facility). Under Mr. Taub’s leadership, Wellstone has built robust
regional management including back-office, clinical, education, training, and admissions/marketing
teams to support facility level staff and work to ensure positive patient outcomes while maintaining
efficient operations. With an incredibly detailed approach and knowledge of the industry, Mr. Taub
leads all management decisions across Wellstone’s portfolio.

In February 2020, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) approved the CEC
Application of Crestwood Healthcare, Inc. to acquire Crestwood Nursing & Rehabilitation Center in
Warren, Rhode Island. Jacob Taub had a 28.4% membership interest in the Applicant. Although
RIDOH approved the CEC Application, the seller chose not to close the transaction.

Mr. Kofman graduated from Touro College with an Advanced Accounting Degree. He has a decade
of finance, underwriting and operational experience and has worked in various roles on 500MM+
