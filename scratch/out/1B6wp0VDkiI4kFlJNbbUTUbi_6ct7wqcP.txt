The Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) accepts the recommendation of the Health
Services Council, adopts the facts contained in the HSC Report, and approves the application
subject to the conditions as set forth in the HSC Report along with the additional conditions of
approval as contained within this Decision.

In deciding to approve this application, RIDOH has carefully considered the entire record in light
of the purpose of the Health Care Facility Licensing Act of Rhode Island (R.I. Gen. Laws 23-17-
3) and the four statutory review criteria found in Rhode Island Gen. Laws Section 23-17-14.3.

Therefore, in consideration of the entire record, including the written and oral public comments,
and taken together with RIDOH’s interest in ensuring that all nursing homes are operated with the
highest possible degree of care and competency, it imposes the following conditions of approval,
in addition to the standard conditions of approval contained in the HSC Report:
