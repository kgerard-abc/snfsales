I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of CBC Rhode
Island FT, LLC (“Applicant”) for the Change in Effective Control of Hope Nursing Home
Care, LLC [License # HNC02327], a licensed home nursing care provider located at 1049
Park Avenue, Cranston, Rhode Island, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

The Applicant is a newly formed entity that was formed for purposes of the acquisition.
One hundred percent of the membership interests of the Applicant are held by Community
Based Care FT Acquisition, LLC, which in turn, is 100% owned by Community Based
Care Intermediate I, LP.

Community Based Care Intermediate I, LP is 99% owned by Community Based Care
Holdings, LP and 1% owned by Martis Partners III Community Aggregator, LP.

Community Based Care Holdings, LP is owned 4.90% collectively by individuals and
95.10% by Martis Partners III Community Aggregator, LP. Martis Partners III Community
Aggregator, L.P. is 20.09% owned by Martis Partners III Community Blocker, LP and
79.91% owned by Martis Partners III, LP. Martis Partners III Community Aggregator is
managed by Martis Partners III GP, LLC.

Martis Partners III Community Blocker, LP and Martis Partners III, LP are owned by
passive investors that commit capital to Martis but do not have involvement in or control
of investment decisions made by Martis or the day-to-day operations of the Applicant.
Community Based Care Intermediate I, LP also owns 100% of the stock of Community
Based Care Acquisition, Inc., which owns 100% of the membership interest of Community
Based Care, LLC, Community Based Care, LLC in turn owns interests in several home
health providers, and is currently operating in 3 states (Florida, North Carolina, and
Virginia). The organizational chart is contained in Tab 15 of the Change in Effective
Control application.

Upon approval of the instant application, acquisition of Hope Nursing Home Care, LLC
(“Hope Nursing”), the subject home nursing care provider, and through various activities
and transactions with affiliated entities as described in the application, the Applicant will
seek to be the licensee of Hope Nursing.

As presented in the application, the total acquisition cost is $18,589,000 to be funded with
100% equity.


III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island entitled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed an application for a change in
effective control of the subject-licensed facility. This request is made because the statute

requires that any proposed change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility
be reviewed by the Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and

wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by April 3,

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      A. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.

         According to the application and representations made by the Applicant at the
         meeting of April 26, 2022, there are no citations, enforcement actions, violations,
         charges, investigations, or similar types of actions involving the Applicant and/or
         its affiliates.

         The Council was not apprised of any concerns related to the Applicant and its
         affiliates.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that this request for a change in effective
control be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to Hope Nursing Home Care, LLC and the issuance
of a new nursing facility license to CBC Rhode Island FT, LLC.


VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total project cost not exceed $18,589,000;

       2. that the operating costs for the first full operating year not exceed $8,056,516;

       3. that the Applicant implement the project as approved;

4. that the Applicant attain and maintain an accreditation from a nationally
   recognized accrediting agency;

5. that the Applicant shall conduct national background checks on its employees;

6. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic

7. that the Applicant will provide services to all patients without discrimination
   including payment source and ability to pay; and

8. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
   alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.

   patient while an application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid)
   is pending.
