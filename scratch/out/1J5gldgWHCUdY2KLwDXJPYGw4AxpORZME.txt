I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of Greenwood
Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare, LLC and ESRI Holdco, LLC] (“Applicant
or Greenwood Center”) for a Change in Effective Control (“CEC”) of Harborside Rhode
Island Limited Partnership d/b/a Greenwood Center located in Warwick, RI be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

As presented in the application, Greenwood Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare,
LLC and ESRI Holdco, LLC seeks CEC approval to acquire Harborside Rhode Island Limited
Partnership d/b/a Greenwood Center.

Greenwood Center is a 130-bed skilled nursing facility located at 1139 Main Street, Warwick,
Rhode Island.

III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant file a CEC application of the subject-
licensed facility. This request is made because the statute requires that any proposed change in
owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council and
approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the reviews would commence on October 18, 2025. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by November
16, 2025, when practicable. A letter, dated November 14, 2025, was submitted by Katie
Gradowski, Special Assistant Attorney General, from the Rhode Island Office of the Attorney
General; a letter, dated November 17, 2025 was submitted by Maureen Maigret, Senior Agenda
Coalition of RI Policy Advisor; a letter, dated November 18, 2025, was submitted by Kathleen
Gerard, Director for Advocates for Better Care in Rhode Island; a letter, dated November 18,
2025 was submitted by Jesse Martin, Executive Vice President, SEIU Healthcare; a letter dated
November 23, 2025 was submitted by Lori Light, Rhode Island State Long Term Care
Ombudsman; and a letter dated November 24, 2025 was submitted by State Representative
David A. Bennett.

IV.       FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.
          As presented by the Applicant and its legal counsel:

             •   “The Applicant’s principals are experienced operators of skilled nursing
                 facilities and combined own five Rhode Island facilities.

             •   By way of example, prior to Mr. Swartz’s management of Respiratory and
                 Rehabilitation, Greenville, and Warren, the facilities were facing numerous
                 operational, quality and financial challenges.

                     o Following Mr. Swartz’s management and later ownership of
                       Respiratory and Rehabilitation, Greenville, and Warren, the
                       facilities’ annual survey outcomes have improved, turnover in key
                       positions has decreased, and agency staffing has been reduced.
                     o Warren, a five star facility, was cited by U.S. News as one of the top
                       100 skilled nursing facilities in the country.
                     o Respiratory and Rehabilitation self reported an incident resulting in
                       an October 28, 2025 survey. After submission of the plan of
                       correction alleging substantial compliance on November 24, 2025,
                       RIDOH accepted the allegation of compliance on November 17,
                       2025, and presumed achievement of substantial compliance on
                       November 24, 2025.
                     o Greenville, a one star facility, has a five star rating for staffing.

      •   The Applicant is committed to providing high quality of care and commitment to
          serving their residents and the community.

      •   The experience of the principals at their other facilities in Rhode Island and other
          states, many of which previously faced operational challenges and include diverse
          resident populations, has equipped them to provide high-quality care to the
          residents at the Facility.”

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that the request for the CEC application,
be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to Harborside Rhode Island Limited Partnership
d/b/a Greenwood Center, and the issuance of a skilled new nursing facility license to
Greenwood Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare, LLC and ESRI Holdco,
LLC].


VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total purchase price shall not exceed $10.00;

2. that the Applicant implement the project as approved;

3. that within two years the Applicant attain and maintain an accreditation from a
   nationally recognized accrediting agency;

4. that the Applicant shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
   employees prior to employment;

5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
   resident information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

6. that the Applicant will provide services to all residents without discrimination
   including payment source and ability to pay; and

7. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
   alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
   Further that the Applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge
   the resident while an application for an alternative payor source (such as
   Medicaid) is pending, and for Medicaid residents, maintain compliance with
   state regulation, 210-RICR-50-00-7, “Involuntary Discharge from a Long-
   Term Care Facility.”
