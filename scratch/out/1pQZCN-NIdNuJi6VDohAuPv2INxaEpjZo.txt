I. SYNOPSIS


The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the applications of (1) 198
Waterman Avenue LLC [Michael Bigney and related trusts, 70%; Kelly Arnold and
related trust, 10%; Christopher Ryan and related trust, 10%; Stephanie Ryan, 10%]
for a Change in Effective Control of Eastgate Nursing & Rehabilitation Center
[LTC00779], a 76-bed nursing facility located in East Providence, RI; (2) Allen’s Health
Center, Inc. [Michael Bigney and related trusts, 70%; Kelly Arnold and related trust,
10%; Christopher Ryan and related trust, 10%; Stephanie Ryan, 10%] for a Change in
Effective Control of South Kingstown Nursing & Rehabilitation Center [LTC00720], a
112-bed nursing facility located in West Kingston, RI; (3) Warwick Health Center, Inc.
[Michael Bigney and related trusts, 70%; Kelly Arnold and related trust, 10%;
Christopher Ryan and related trust, 10%; Stephanie Ryan, 10%] for a Change in
Effective Control of West Shore Health Center [LTC00719], a 149-bed nursing facility
located in Warwick, RI; (4) Burrillville Health Center Associates Limited [Michael
Bigney and related trusts, 70%; Kelly Arnold and related trust, 10%; Christopher
Ryan and related trust, 10%; Stephanie Ryan, 10%] for a Change in Effective Control
of Bayberry Commons [LTC00651], a 120-bed nursing facility located in Pascoag, RI; (5)
Village House Convalescent Home, Inc. [Michael Bigney and related trusts, 70%; Kelly
Arnold and related trust, 10%; Christopher Ryan and related trust, 10%; Stephanie
Ryan, 10%] for a Change in Effective Control of Village House Nursing & Rehabilitation
Center [LTC00589], a 95-bed nursing facility located in Newport, RI; and (6) Elmwood
Health Center, Inc. [Michael Bigney and related trusts, 70%; Kelly Arnold and related
trust, 10%; Christopher Ryan and related trust, 10%; Stephanie Ryan, 10%] for a
Change in Effective Control of Elmwood Nursing & Rehabilitation Center
[LTC0000611], a 70-bed nursing facility located in Providence, RI for a Change in Effective
Control, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

 The Applicants are seeking approval to acquire the ownership interest in the six facilities and
associated real estate:

    •   198 Waterman Avenue LLC d/b/a Eastgate, with 76 beds located in Providence;
    •   Allen’s Health Center Inc. d/b/a South Kingstown, with 112 beds located in West
        Kingstown;
    •   Warwick Health Center, Inc. d/b/a West Shore, with 149 beds located in Warwick;
    •   Burrillville Health Center Associates Limited d/b/a Bayberry, with 120 beds located in
        Pascoag;
    •   Village House Convalescent Home, Inc. d/b/a Village House, with 95 beds located in
        Newport; and
    •   Elmwood Health Center, Inc. d/b/a Elmwood, with 70 beds located in Providence.

Pursuant to the terms of the Transaction, on January 1, 2024, Michael Bigney purchased a 49%
interest in Health Concepts, Ltd., the management company for the facilities, and became its

Michael Bigney is the co-owner of two HNCPs and one Hospice Provider with over 41 years of

president of the Rhode Island Partnership for Home Care, a trade association representing
licensed home care, home nursing care and hospice agencies in Rhode Island.

Kelly Arnold has over 20 years of clinical and managerial experience in the long-term care
setting and was the Nursing Home Administrator in three Health Concepts facilities before
becoming COO in 2017. Ms. Arnold received her NHA license in 1998 and has been with
Health Concepts for approximately 25 years. She is the current Board Chair of RIHCA, a trade
association representing more than 80% of the nursing homes in Rhode Island.

Christopher Ryan has been with Health Concepts for approximately 21 years, first in the
Information Technology department before serving as a Nursing Home Administrator and

care settings. Ms. Ryan is a co-owner of Nursing Placement and has been with the company for

Palliative Care LLC.

III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 23-17-14.3 and 23-17-14.4, titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed six (6) CEC applications of the
subject-licensed facilities. This request is being made because the statute requires that any
proposed change in owner, operator, or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed
by the Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the applications, found them to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicants
and the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-
mail that the reviews would commence on September 25, 2024. The notice also advised that all
persons wishing to comment on the applications submit their comments to the state agency by
October 25, 2024, when practicable. There were no written public comments.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicants, the Council recommends that the request for six (6) CEC
applications, be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to Eastgate Nursing & Rehabilitation Center and the
issuance of a new nursing facility license to 198 Waterman Avenue, LLC, located in
Pawtucket.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility issued to South Kingstown Nursing & Rehabilitation Center and
the issuance of a new nursing facility license to Allen’s Health Center, Inc., located in South
Kingstown.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility issued to West Shore Health Center and the issuance of a new
nursing facility license to Warwick Health Centre, Inc., located in Warwick.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility issued to Bayberry Commons and the issuance of a new nursing
facility license to Burrillville Health Center Associates Limited, located in Pascoag.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility issued to Village House Nursing & Rehabilitation Center and the
issuance of a new nursing facility license to Village House Convalescent Home, Inc.,
located in Newport.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility issued to Elmwood Nursing & Rehabilitation Center

VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant applications shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the Applicants implement the applications as approved;

       2. that within two years the Applicants attain and maintain an accreditation from
          a nationally recognized accrediting agency;

       3. that the Applicants shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
          employees prior to employment;

       4. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization, and demographic
          resident information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

       5. that the Applicants will provide services to all residents without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay;

       6. that the applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
          alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
          Further that the applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge the
          patient while an application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid)
          is pending; and

       7. that the applicant provide, through administrative and operational policies and
          procedures individualized and resident-centered care, services, and
          accommodations.
