===== PAGE 3 =====
                     Background
• The Applicants seek CEC approval:
   – to transfer operations of Elderwood of Scallop Shell at
     Wakefield (the “Facility”) from the current operator, 981
     Kings Town Road Operating Company LLC, to
     AdviniaCare Scallop Shell, LLC (the “New Operator”);
     and
   – to transfer the real estate of the Facility from the
     current owner, 981 Kings Town Road, LLC, to 55
     Scallop Shell, LLC (the “New Real Estate Owner”).
• The Facility is an 80-bed skilled nursing facility located at
  55 Scallop Shell Way in South Kingstown.

                                                                  3

===== PAGE 4 =====
                  The Transaction
• Pursuant to a Purchase and Sales Agreement dated as of
  December 23, 2025:
   – The New Operator will acquire the operations of the
     Facility; and
   – The New Real Estate Owner will acquire the Real Estate of
     the Facility.
• The New Operator will lease the real estate from the New
  Real Estate Owner following closing of the Proposed
  Transaction pursuant to a lease agreement (Tab 24).
• The purchase price for the operations and real estate of
  the Facility is $8,500,000.
                                                                 4

===== PAGE 5 =====
                 Facility Post-Closing
• All current leadership, including the Administrator and
  Director of Nursing will remain at the Facility
  following closing.
• There will be no change in services.
• The Facility offers a full range of nursing care and
  social services. It also provides specialty programs
  including rehabilitation therapy programs (physical,
  occupational and speech language), health management
  (nutrition, diabetes care, wound care), recreational
  therapy, and support services.
   – The New Operator will continue to provide quality, short-
     term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care to residents
     of the Facilities.

                         The Applicants
• Yosef Meystel, Ben Berkowitz, and David Berkowitz, principals of the
  Applicants, combined, own and/or operate a total of 111 licensed facilities in
  8 states.
• The Applicants’ affiliated entities own and operate 7 licensed facilities in
  Rhode Island.
   1. AdviniaCare Pawtucket;
   2. AdviniaCare Providence;
   3. Heatherwood Rehabilitation & Health Care Center;
   4. Oakland Grove Health Care Center;
   5. Orchard View Manor Rehabilitation & Nursing Center;
   6. Summit Commons Rehabilitation & Health Care Center;
   7. Waterview Villa Rehabilitation.
• The New Operator in the present transaction will provide the same
  dedication and high quality care given at the 7 Rhode Island facilities.

===== PAGE 9 =====
                        The Applicants
• RIDOH approved the CEC applications for the operations of
  AdviniaCare Pawtucket and AdviniaCare Providence on January 31,
  2025, after an affiliated entity provided management services pursuant to
  a RIDOH-approved management agreement, beginning in February of
  2023.
    – Since their management of those facilities, the annual survey
      outcomes for the facilities significantly improved, the operators
      prioritized decreasing turnover in key positions, were able to eliminate
      all agency staff and worked collaboratively with the unions in those
      facilities and have infused capital into the facilities.
• RIDOH approved the CEC applications for the operations of the five
  Athena Healthcare-owned Rhode Island facilities on December 3,
  2025 and the transaction closed effective January 30, 2026.

         Financing and Financial Viability
• The Applicants will finance the $8,500,000 transaction with a $6,800,000
  conventional mortgage, $500,000 sellers’ note, and $1,200,000 equity.

  evidenced by their bank statements and CPA letter.
• The Applicants’ affiliates have been in the long term nursing facility industry for
  a number of years and have never experienced any defaults or failures.
    •   “We certify, in our professional opinion, that based on the history of the Applicants’
        principal’s successful ownership and operation of skilled nursing facilities, the

         • See Tab C3, Correspondence from CPA firm Roth & Co.
