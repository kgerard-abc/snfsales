===== SLIDE 3 =====
Background
Health Concepts affiliated entities currently own and operate ten skilled nursing facilities in Rhode Island.  The Applicants seek approval to acquire ownership and operations of six of the ten facilities:
Bayberry Commons is a 110-bed SNF located at 181 Davis Drive, Pascoag, RI.
Eastgate Nursing & Rehabilitation is a 68-bed SNF located at 198 Waterman Avenue, Providence, RI. 
Elmwood Nursing & Rehabilitation Center is a 70-bed SNF located at 225 Elmwood Avenue, Providence, RI.
South Kingstown Nursing & Rehabilitation Center is a 96-bed SNF located at 2115 South County Trail, West Kingstown, RI.
Village House Nursing & Rehabilitation Center is a 95-bed SNF located at 70 Harrison Avenue, Newport, RI.
West Shore Health Center  is a 145-bed SNF located at 109 West Shore Road, Warwick, RI.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
The Transaction
Pursuant to an Asset Purchase Agreement and Amendment thereto, effective October 15, 2021, (the “APA”) by and between the Health Concepts property owners and the current Health Concepts operators, on one hand, and Tryko affiliated entities, on the other hand, the Health Concepts entities agreed to sell the real property for the ten Health Concepts Facilities to Tryko affiliated entities.
Pursuant to ten Operation Transfer Agreements effective July 8, 2021 (“OTAs”), between each Health Concepts operator and a Tryko affiliated entity, the respective Health Concept operators agreed to transfer to the Tryko affiliated entities all right, title, and interest to the nursing home beds and accompanying operational rights at the time of closing. 
At
 closing, the Tryko entities that are party to OTAs will assign their interests in the OTA to the Applicants.



‹#›
===== SLIDE 6 =====
The Transaction
Following execution of the original APA and OTAs, Tryko identified Kadima to own and operate six of the ten Health Concepts facilities. 
Kadima is an experienced skilled nursing facility operator currently operating six facilities in Pennsylvania. 
At closing, the Kadima property companies will lease the facilities to the Kadima operating entities (the Applicants).
As a result, Tryko and the Kadima affiliated entities entered into a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (“MIPA”), effective October 15, 2021, in which Kadima will acquire Tryko’s membership interest in the six Tryko operating entities at closing
CEC applications for the six proposed Kadima facilities were filed contemporaneously with the CEC applications for the proposed Tryko facilities.
As a result of the Transaction, the Tryko affiliated entities will operate 516 beds formerly operated by Health Concepts and the Applicants will operate 584 beds.

The Applicant Will Continue to Provide Safe and Adequate Treatment

The Applicants will continue to provide high quality care previously offered by Health Concepts.
