===== SLIDE 3 =====
Background
Lincolnwood Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center (“Lincolnwood Center”) seeks approval of its acquisition of the Hopkins Manor skilled nursing facility located at 610 Smithfield Road in North Providence. 
Hopkins Manor has been under Receivership since April 2020; the owners of Lincolnwood Center were chosen by the Receiver as purchaser of the facility after a public, open-bid process, and the acquisition has been approved by Judge Brian Stern of the Rhode Island Superior Court. 
The facility is licensed to operate 200 beds and provides skilled nursing services, physical therapy, speech and occupational therapy. 
===== SLIDE 4 =====
Background
Lincolnwood Center is part of a longstanding family of 31 nursing facilities operating in six states owned by 
Tryko
 Holdings and its affiliates.
As owners of Elmhurst Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center in Providence, the operators of Lincolnwood Center are fully experienced with the Rhode Island market and maintain an exemplary record among the State’s long term care providers.
===== SLIDE 5 =====
Transaction Overview
Total investment of $19,500,000, including $14,500,000 for the purchase price for the facility and $5,000,000 in capital expenditures. 
Financed by:
$10,875,000 facility acquisition loan (75% of purchase price); 
$3,750,000 capital expenditure loan (75% of capital expenditure); and 
$4,875,000 equity contribution (25% of transaction total).
Capital expenditures will focus on upgrades and repairs to the building: complete renovation of patient room and common areas, replace windows, renovate gym, and upgrade memory care unit.
The facility’s real estate will be owned by Lincolnwood Property, LLC, an entity co-owned by the owners of Lincolnwood Center, and leased to the Center.

===== SLIDE 7 =====
Character, Commitment, Competence and Community Standing
The Applicant and its affiliates are a third-generation operator of nursing facilities; currently with 31 facilities in six states:  Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Virginia and Rhode Island.  Several have received four- and five-star ratings in the “Best Nursing Homes” listing.
The Applicant and its affiliates maintain an outstanding track record for regulatory compliance and providing quality services across their facilities.
Elmhurst Rehabilitation & Healthcare Center has been affiliated with the Applicant since 2016 and maintains a solid and consistent reputation for competence and commitment to the community.
===== SLIDE 8 =====
Provision of Safe and Adequate Care
The Applicant shall establish and maintain a Quality Assurance and Performance Improvement (QAPI) Committee at the facility to oversee the QAPI Plan.
The QAPI Plan details the facility’s activities and objectives to monitor and evaluate the quality and safety of resident care, pursue methods to improve care quality, and resolve identified problems.
The Applicant and its affiliates bring extensive experience to quality improvement activities through their management of multiple nursing facilities in multiple jurisdictions.
The Applicant is committed to maintaining the facility’s existing array of services and expanding the therapy gym for patients receiving rehabilitation services.
===== SLIDE 9 =====
Financial Viability and Strength
The facility has been in receivership since April 2020; its continued existence is dependent upon this transaction with the Applicant.
The Applicant brings extensive financial resources to the facility:
$19,500,000 in committed funds; including $5,000,000 for needed capital improvements
$1,400,000 in working capital funding
The Applicant and its affiliates have demonstrated decades of achieving consistent financial success in the long-term care industry – in six jurisdictions – and particularly with financially challenged facilities.
===== SLIDE 10 =====
Services to Underserved Populations
The Applicant is committed to providing access to underserved populations and does not discriminate on the basis of race, creed, color, religion, national origin, sex, gender, handicap, disability, blindness, source of sponsorship, service of payment, marital status, age, sexual preference, or genetic disposition in the admission, retention and care of residents and patients.
The Applicant intends to maintain the existing patient mix of approximately 78% of Medicaid and 10% of Medicare patients.
The Applicant will be implementing a special grant to provide memory care and behavioral health services to underserved populations.
