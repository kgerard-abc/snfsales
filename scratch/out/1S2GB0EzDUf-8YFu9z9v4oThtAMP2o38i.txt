Applications tomorrow.

I am writing in response to the April 18, 2025 letter from the Office of the Health Care Advocate
at the Rhode Island Office of the Attorney General (the "Letter").

We agree with the Letter’s statement that:

       Nursing homes play a critical role in Rhode Island’s health care ecosystem. As you know,
       skilled nursing facilities operate with limited financial resources and are primarily funded
       by public insurance, e.g. Medicaid and Medicare. The health and well-being of Rhode
       Island residents in skilled nursing facilities is often directly tethered to the financial
       health of these facilities.

The three facilities that are the subject of the CEC Applications include both Medicaid and
Medicare residents, with the majority being funded by Medicaid. The Applicants understand the
importance of the financial health of the facilities to ensure quality of care. Since the Applicants
have been providing management services pursuant to the RIDOH approved Management
Agreement, they have worked successfully to improve the financial condition of the facilities,
including decreasing turnover in key positions and reducing agency staffing costs. As a result,
the management team has successfully reduced agency staffing costs from over $4.1M under the
