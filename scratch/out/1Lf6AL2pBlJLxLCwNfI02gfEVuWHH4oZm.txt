                            Introduction
• Violet SNF Holdco LLC (the “New Operator” or “Applicant”)
  seeks change in effective control approval to acquire the operations
  of Pawtucket Falls Healthcare Center (“Pawtucket Falls” or
  “Facility”), a 154-bed nursing home located at 70 Gill Ave.,
  Pawtucket, Rhode Island.
• The Facility is currently under the management and supervision of
  Mark Russo, Esq., solely in his capacity as the Court Appointed
  Receiver for Pawtucket SNF Operator, LLC. (the “Receiver”)
  pursuant to an order entered by the Rhode Island Superior Court in
  the matter of Pawtucket SNF Operator, LLC d/b/a Pawtucket Falls
  Health Care Center (the “Receivership Proceeding”).
   – Pawtucket SNF Operator LLC (“Pawtucket SNF Operator”) is the licensed
     operator of the Facility.
• In tandem with a change in operations, Gill Street LLC (“PropCo”)
  is purchasing the Pawtucket Falls real estate from current owner,
  Pawtucket SNF Real Estate, LLC. (“Current PropCo”)

===== PAGE 7 =====
                      The Transaction
• On October 12, 2023, Rhode Island Superior Court Justice Brian P. Stern, entered
  an order in the Receivership Proceeding, approving a petition to sell all of the
  assets which comprise Pawtucket Falls, pursuant to an Operations Transfer
  Agreement (“OTA”) by and between the Receiver and the New Operator,
  effective October 12, 2023, subject to CEC approval and Contract of Sale by and
  between Receiver and Violet SNF Holdco LLC. See Tab 14.
• The Applicant has paid a $500,000.00 operational deposit to the Receiver allowing
  the Receivership Estate to offset operational losses prior to CEC approval in
  exchange for a first security lien on Facility receivables.
• At closing, the New Operator will assume the obligations of the Current PropCo
  and Pawtucket SNF Operations, LLC under an existing promissory note with the
  principal amount due and owing being $9,000,000.00. See Tab 1.
• The Receiver engaged the New Operator under an Interim Advisory Services
  Agreement to provide advisory services. See id.
• Following CEC approval, if granted, the Receiver and the New Operator plan to
  present to the Court a transition plan for the first six month of operations to be
  monitored by the Court.

       Character, Competence, Commitment, and
             Standing in the Community
• Giorgio Mayer and Leah Jacobwitz have a proven track record in owning and operating
  skilled nursing facilities.
• Mr. Mayer began his career in skilled nursing in 1996, when he purchased shares in the
  operating and realty entities of Gold Crest Care Center in the Bronx, New York.
• During the past 20 years, Mr. Mayer has acquired ownership interests in eleven skilled
  nursing facilities, all of which are in good standing:
      •   Gold Crest Care Center: 175 bed SNF, Bronx, New York – acquired 1996.
      •   Sunnyside Care Center: 80 bed SNF, East Syracuse, New York – acquired 1998.
      •   Wellsville Manor: 120 bed SNF, Wellsville, New York – acquired 1999.
      •   Valley View Manor: 82 bed SNF, Norwich, New York – acquired 2001.
      •   Bethany Operating Company: 100 bed SNF, Rome, New York – acquired 2002.
      •   Fieldston Lodge Care Center: 200 bed SNF, Bronx, New York – acquired 2004.
      •   Affinity Skilled Living & Rehabilitation: 280 bed SNF, Oakdale, New York – acquired 2008.
      •   Whitney Rehabilitation & Nursing Center: 150 bed SNF, Hamden, Connecticut – acquired
          2015.
      •   Elm Manor Nursing Home: 46 bed SNF, Canandaigua, New York – acquired 2017.
      •   Wedgewood Nursing Home: 29 bed SNF, Spencerport, New York – acquired 2017.
      •   The Baird Nursing Home: 28 bed SNF, Rochester, New York – acquired 2019.
      •   Pending application in Connecticut to acquire St. Jospeh Center, Trumbull, Connecticut.
•   Ms. Jacobwitz worked for eighteen years in the medical supply industry in New York City. Thereafter, she
    worked at a SNF in Brooklyn, New York where she was responsible for staffing and clinical needs of residents.
    Ms. Jacobwitz also has a 50% ownership interest in Whitney Rehabilitation & Nursing Center, Hamden,
    Connecticut.

===== PAGE 10 =====
  The Applicant Will Continue to Provide
      Safe and Adequate Treatment
• The New Operator will continue all current services including 24-hour
  nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech and language
  therapy, short-term rehabilitation, hospice and wound care, specialized
  diets, dementia care, and dental, podiatry and audiology services.
    • No services will be added, terminated, expanded, or reduced.
• The New Operator will continue to ensure quality improvement (see
  Quality Assurance Policy at Tab 22).
    • The Quality Assurance Policy is designed to identify the high risk,
       problem prone and high-volume areas to evaluate for improvement
       and identify, collect and use data relevant to the unique characteristics
       and needs of the resident.
• The New Operator will continue to provide resident-centered practices to
  ensure that each resident receives the highest level of care.
