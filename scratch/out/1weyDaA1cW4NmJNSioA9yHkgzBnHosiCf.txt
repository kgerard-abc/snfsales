                           Background
• AdviniaCare Providence Dodge Rehab Center, LLC (“AdviniaCare
  Providence”), AdviniaCare Pawtucket Pleasant Rehab Center, LLC
  (“AdviniaCare Pawtucket”) and AdviniaCare Kingston Rehab Center, LLC
  (“AdviniaCare Kingston”) (collectively the “Applicants”) seek change in
  effective control approval to acquire Bannister Center for Rehabilitation &
  Health Care (“Bannister”), Oak Hill Center for Rehabilitation and Health
  Care (“Oak Hill”) and Kingston Center for Rehabilitation and Health Care
  (“Kingston”), respectively.
• Bannister, a 161 bed nursing home located at 135 Dodge Street,
  Providence, Rhode Island, is licensed and operated by Bannister
  Operations Associates LLC (“Bannister Operator”).
• Oak Hill, a 129 bed nursing home located at 544 Pleasant Street,
  Pawtucket, Rhode Island, is licensed and operated by Oak Hill Operations
  Associates LLC (“Oak Hill Operator”).
• Kingston, a 55 bed nursing home located at 415 Gardiner Road, West
  Kingston, Rhode Island, is licensed and operated by Shady Acres
  Operations Associates LLC (“Kingston Operator”).

                       Background Continued
•   The Applicants’ sole member is Pointe RI OpCo LLC. The principals of Pointe RI
    OpCo LLC are Ben Berkowitz, David Berkowitz, and Yosef Meystel. Combined, the

===== PAGE 5 =====
                    Background Continued
•   Prior to the Applicants’ management, the facilities were facing numerous operational, quality and
    financial challenges. Compounding these challenges, the facilities had low star ratings and high
    turnover with agency staffing.
•   The population at the facilities is not composed of the “typical” geriatric residents. Instead, these
    facilities serve not only geriatric patients but also a younger population that often presents with
    complex, multifaceted conditions, which in addition to physical health challenges, may include
    behavioral health conditions.
•   Under the Applicants’ management, annual survey outcomes have greatly improved, with far less
    citations and decreased scope and severity levels.
      – All three facilities are in good standing with RIDOH.
      – The Applicants prioritized decreasing turnover in key positions and turnover is currently less
           than the average turnover percentage in the industry.
      – The Applicants eliminated the use of agency staff in all three facilities within three months of
           assuming management recognizing the need for educated, trained and experienced staff in each
           facility.
      – The Applicants work collaboratively with both the New England Healthcare Employees Union,
           District 1199/SEIU and Local 328 of the United Food & Commercial Workers Union.
      – The CMS Five-Star Rating System is a three year process of improvement, which the Applicants
           have begun with improved survey results, consistent staffing and a focus on quality
           improvement.
•   The Applicants will continue to provide quality short-term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care
    to the residents of the three facilities listed above.
•   The Applicants have infused capital into the facilities and funded their losses. The Applicants’
    principals have loaned over $636,000 to the facilities.                                             5

===== PAGE 7 =====
                          Examples
Aperion Care Wilmington
• A 171 skilled bed nursing facility located in Wilmington, IL. When
  the facility was first acquired in September 2013, it suffered from
  absentee operators, lack of funding and was on the verge of closure.
  Aperion was able to step in with the capital and management system
  necessary to improve the foundation and build the successful HUD
  facility Aperion Care Wilmington as it is known today.
• The facility underwent a variety of physical improvements including
  a new roof, facility entrance and lobby, new floors and all hallways,
  resident room renovations and new resident furniture. In addition to
  these upgrades, both the memory care/behavioral health units and
  therapy rooms were also renovated.
• The facility is run by a highly efficient team of individuals, many of
  whom have served the building and its residents since Aperion’s
  acquisition in 2013.

             Examples Continued
Aperion Care Dolton
• An 88 bed skilled nursing facility located in Dolton,
  IL. When the facility was originally acquired in
  2010, it was comprised of three bed units with a total
  of 80 beds. The facility was considered
  nonfunctioning and needed an extensive overhaul in
  all aspects of operations.
• Thorough changes in structure contributed to the
  facility’s vast improvement. Aperion Care Dolton is
  now considered a premier facility in the southern
  suburbs of Chicago and a cornerstone in compliance
  and management within the Aperion portfolio.

                    The Transactions
• Operations: Subject to CEC approval and pursuant to the three
  Operations Transfer Agreements (“OTAs”) entered into on December
  19, 2022, the Applicants agreed to acquire the operations and
  operational assets from Bannister Operator, Oak Hill Operator and
  Kingston Operator, respectively. See Tab 14.
• Real Estate: Subject to CEC approval and pursuant to a Master Lease
  Agreement entered into on December 19, 2022, Providence RI
  Properties Holdings, LLC, Pawtucket RI Properties Holdings, LLC and
  West Kingston RI Properties Holdings, LLC (collectively the
  “Sublessors”) will lease the Bannister property, Oak Hill property, and
  Kingston property, respectively (collectively, the “Properties”) from the
  current property owners, Bannister Realty Associates LLC, Oak Hill
  Realty Associates LLC and Shady Acres Realty Associates LLC,
  respectively and sublease to the Applicants. (Tab 24)

===== PAGE 18 =====
       Financing and Financial Viability
• The New Operators have paid a security deposit of $1,650,000 for all
  three facilities, with $165,000 (10%) allocated for Kingston, $742,500
  (45%) allocated for Oak Hill and $742,500 (45%) allocated for Bannister.
• There is no financing for the proposed transaction.
• The Applicants have sufficient funds for capital and operating needs as
  evidenced by its bank statements and CPA letter.
• The Applicants’ affiliates have been in the long term nursing facility
  industry for a number of years and have never experienced any defaults
  or failures.
   • See Tab 23, Correspondence from CPA firm Roth & Co.,
       demonstrating that the Applicants have the financial capability to
       consummate the purchase of and safely operate the facilities.
• Additionally, the Applicants plan to commit capital resources necessary
  to continue growth at the facilities.

RIHCA, John B. Gage, MBA, NHA, President & CEO:
       I am writing in support of the applications of AdviniaCare for changes
       in effective control of Kingston Center, Oak Center and Bannister
       Center. AdviniaCare has been managing these three facilities under a
       management agreement reviewed and approved by the Department of
       Health since July of 2023. In that period of 16 months or so,
       AdviniaCare has done a good job managing these facilities. In that time,
       there have been no deficiencies issued that were categorized as
       “substandard quality of care” or “Immediate Jeopardy”. These facilities
       are vital to the overall healthcare system in our state and to the nursing
       facility network. They provide care and services to some of the most
       challenging individuals in Rhode Island – individuals for whom hospitals
       struggle to transition when they no longer need an acute hospital stay.
       It is imperative that these facilities be allowed to have AdviniaCare assume
       ownership. They will continue to manage them and ensure their long-term
       viability. Just since the start of the pandemic, 7 nursing facilities in Rhode
       Island have closed and an eighth has just announced its imminent closure.
       Given the dramatic increase of our population projected over the next
       decade, our current nursing home capacity must be preserved and
       strengthened.
