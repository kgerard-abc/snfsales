Attached is the final Report of Health Services Council on the Application of Holiday Operator,
LLC and Holiday Property, LLC for a Change in Effective Control of Holiday Retirement
Home, Inc., Located in Manville, Rhode Island, be approved. (“HSC Report”).

The Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) accepts the recommendation of the Health
Services Council (“HSC”), adopts the facts contained in the HSC Report, and approves the
application subject to the conditions as set forth in the HSC Report along with the additional
condition of approval as contained within this Decision.

In deciding to approve this application, RIDOH has carefully considered the entire record in light
of the purpose of the Health Care Facility Licensing Act of Rhode Island (R.I. Gen. Laws 23-17-
3) and the four statutory review criteria found in Rhode Island Gen. Laws Section 23-17-14.3.

Therefore, in consideration of the entire record, including the written and oral public comments,
and taken together with RIDOH’s interest in ensuring that all nursing homes are operated with the
highest possible degree of care and competency, it imposes the following conditions of approval,
in addition to the standard conditions of approval contained in the HSC Report:

       9. Starting three (3) months upon the issuance of the license ( "grace period") to Holiday
       Operations, LLC (“Holiday"), Holiday shall be subject to a one-year evaluation period. If
       Holiday's Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (“CMS”) star rating is reduced by
       two (2) or more stars, beginning from the end of the grace period and for one year
       thereafter, RIDOH may require Holiday to hire, at Holiday’s expense, a third-party
       monitor. The monitor will remain in place until RIDOH determines that the facility has
       improved to an acceptable level. RIDOH may waive the requirement for a monitor if
       RIDOH determines that the specific deficienc(ies) that caused the rating decrease does
