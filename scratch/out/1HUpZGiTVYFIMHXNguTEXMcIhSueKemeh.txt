===== PAGE 3 =====
                        Background
• Cortland Place Rehab Center LLC (“Cortland Place” or “Operator”)
  and Cortland Place Realty LLC (the real estate owner) (collectively,
  the “Applicants”) seek change in effective control approval for Josh
  Gellis to buy out the other member’s interest in the Applicants.
• Cortland Place operates the 87-bed licensed nursing home doing
  business as Stillwater Assisted Living and Skilled Nursing
  Community, located at 20 Austin Avenue in Greenville, Rhode Island
  (“Stillwater”).
• Cortland Place Realty LLC owns the property where Stillwater is
  located.
• The Applicant will continue to provide quality short-term
  rehabilitation and long-term nursing care to Stillwater’s residents.

===== PAGE 6 =====
                     The Transaction
• The Application seeks approval for Josh Gellis to buy out WF
  Cortland LLC’s (“WF”) 75% interest in Cortland Place Holdings
  LLC (“CP Holdings”) (the direct owner of the Applicants).
• The Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (“MIPA”) dated as of
  July 25, 2025 (Tab 14) provides that WF will sell its membership
  interest in CP Holdings to Mr. Gellis.
   – As a result, Mr. Gellis will be the 100% owner of CP Holdings.
• CP Holdings will then assign its interests in the Applicants to Josh
  Gellis (Tab 14).
• Following the transaction, Josh Gellis will be the sole member of
  both Applicants.
• The Purchase Price for the Transfer of the membership interests is
  $6,910,000.

   The Applicant Will Continue to Provide
       Safe and Adequate Treatment
• The Applicant will continue all current services, physical and
  occupational therapy, speech therapy, respite care, respiratory therapy and
  sub-acute care.
    • No services will be added, terminated, expanded, or reduced.

  Assurance Policy at Tab 22).
    • The Quality Assurance Policy is designed to identify the high risk,

      and needs of the resident.

  ensure that each resident receives the highest level of care.
