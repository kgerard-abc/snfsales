                              Background
•   Greenwood Center, a 130 bed skilled nursing facility located at 1139 Main Street in Warwick,
    Rhode Island is currently operated by Genesis Healthcare, Inc. (“Genesis”) entities (the
    “Facility”).
•   On July 7, 2025 and September 25, 2025, the owner of the real estate and Genesis entity
    entered into a Purchase & Sale Agreement and Operations Transfer Agreement with
    Greenwood RI Propco LLC and Greenwood Operations RI LLC (the “Applicant”),
    respectively, for the sale of the real estate housing the Facility and transfer of operations.
•   On July 9, 2025 Genesis Healthcare filed a Chapter 11 Bankruptcy Petition in the United
    States Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Texas – Dallas Division.
•   On September 25, 2025 the Bankruptcy Court entered an Order approving the transfer of
    operations at the Facility.
•   The Applicant seeks change in effective control approval for the transfer of operations.
•   Pursuant to the Operations Transfer Agreement (Tab 14) by and among the licensed
    operators and the Applicant dated September 25, 2025, the Facility operations will be
    transferred to the Applicant.
•   The Applicant will lease the Facility from Greenwood RI Propco, LLC, subject to review and
    approval of Greenwood RI Propco, LLC’s CEC application filed August 20, 2025.
•   Closing is scheduled for December 8, 2025.

                              The Applicant
• The Applicant’s sole member is Greenwood Operations Holdings LLC, which is owned 55% by
  Mayflower Healthcare LLC (“Mayflower”). The principal and sole member of Mayflower is Zev
  Schwartz.
     • Mr. Schwartz has been involved in the Long-Term Health Care industry over the past 12
        years overseeing upwards of 18 facilities consisting of 1,000 beds in New York, New
        Jersey, Pennsylvania, and Rhode Island.
     • On May 8, 2025, RIDOH approved the CEC applications of Coventry RI Operations LLC
        (“Coventry”), Greenville Operations RI LLC (“Greenville”) and Warren Operations RI
        LLC (“Warren”) for operation of Respiratory and Rehabilitation Center of RI
        (“Respiratory and Rehabilitation”) (a 210-bed facility), Greenville Center (a 131-bed facility)
        and Warren Center (a 63-bed facility), respectively.
     • All these facilities are now owned and operated by Mayflower. Prior to the transfer of
        ownership which occurred on May 23, 2025, Mr. Schwartz served as the team leader
        managing the three facilities since January 2024 pursuant to a Management Agreement
        approved by RIDOH.
• The other indirect owner of the Applicant is ESRI Holdco LLC (“ESRI”) with a 45% ownership
  interest. ESRI’s members include Ephraim Nussbaum and Shlomo Heller, with 50/50%
  ownership interests.
     • On May 29, 2025 RIDOH approved the CEC applications of Cedar Haven Operations
        LLC d/b/a Lake Forest Health & Rehabilitation LLC and Cedar Haven Operations
        Holdings LLC d/b/a Valley View Health & Rehabilitation LLC, for which Mr. Nussbaum
        and Mr. Heller have 20% indirect ownership interests.

===== PAGE 7 =====
     Character, Commitment, Competence,
                 and Standing
              in the Community
• The Applicant’s principals are experienced operators of skilled nursing facilities and combined
  own five Rhode Island facilities.
• By way of example, prior to Mr. Swartz’s management of Respiratory and Rehabilitation,
  Greenville, and Warren, the facilities were facing numerous operational, quality and financial
  challenges.
    • Following Mr. Swartz’s management and later ownership of Respiratory and Rehabilitation,
       Greenville, and Warren, the facilities’ annual survey outcomes have improved, turnover in key
       positions has decreased, and agency staffing has been reduced.
    • Warren, a five star facility, was cited by U.S. News as one of the top 100 skilled nursing
       facilities in the country.
    • Respiratory and Rehabilitation self reported an incident resulting in an October 28, 2025
       survey. After submission of the plan of correction alleging substantial compliance on
       November 24, 2025, RIDOH accepted the allegation of compliance on November 17, 2025,
       and presumed achievement of substantial compliance on November 24, 2025.
    • Greenville, a one star facility, has a five star rating for staffing.
• The Applicant is committed to providing high quality of care and commitment to serving their
  residents and the community.
• The experience of the principals at their other facilities in Rhode Island and other states, many of
  which previously faced operational challenges and include diverse resident populations, has
  equipped them to provide high-quality care to the residents at the Facility.
