                                   CONSENT AGREEMENT


       WHEREAS, this matter is before the Rhode Island Department of Health

(“RIDOH”), pursuant to the authority conferred upon the Director of Health (the “Director”)

under the provisions of R.I. General Laws § 23-17-1, et seq., 216-RICR-40-10-1 et seq, the rules

and regulations Licensing of Nursing Facilities (collectively the “Regulations”); and

       WHEREAS, 740 Oak Hill Road Operations, LLC (“Oak Hill Operations”) and 740 Oak

Hill Road Property, LLC (“Oak Hill Property”), are the operator and owner of a licensed Nursing

Facility commonly known as South County Nursing & Rehabilitation (“South County”),

respectively, and Oak Hill Operations holds a Nursing Facility license issued by RIDOH,

LTC00746 (the “License”); and

       WHEREAS, Oak Hill Operations is a Genesis Healthcare, Inc. subsidiary; and

       WHEREAS, Oak Hill Property and Oak Hill Operations have agreed to sell the Facility

and transfer operations of the Facility to 780 Oak Hill Road SNF Real Estate LLC and South

County SNF Operations LLC, respectively (the “Transaction”); and

       WHEREAS, the Transaction described above requires change in effective control

(“CEC”) approval from the RIDOH for the Rhode Island licensed Nursing Facility; and

       WHEREAS, the closing of the Transaction is anticipated to occur on or around July 1,

2020. At the time of the closing, Genesis will also close on transactions across the country

involving nursing facilities in which it has an ownership or control interest; and

===== PAGE 2 =====
       WHEREAS, pursuant to R.I. General Laws § 23-17-14.4, Health Services Council

(“HSC”) review and RIDOH’s approval may take up to 90 days once the CEC Application has

been deemed complete by the RIDOH for initiation of review; and

       WHEREAS, South County SNF Operations LLC has filed a CEC Application with

RIDOH seeking approval of the Transaction (the “CEC Application”); and

       WHEREAS, to avoid disruption of the care to Rhode Island residents receiving services

provided by South County, RIDOH has agreed to maintain in full force and effect the License

from the date hereof through completion of the CEC approval process.

       NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises contained in this

Agreement, and for other good and value consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is

hereby acknowledged, the parties hereby agree as follows:

       1.      RIDOH shall maintain in full force and effect License LTC00746.

       2.      The Transaction may close on or around July 1, 2020.

       3.      RIDOH will process the CEC Application and South County SNF Operations

LLC will continue to seek review and approval by the HSC and RIDOH for the Transaction.

South County SNF Operations LLC and RIDOH will take all reasonable steps necessary to

complete the CEC approval process in a timely fashion. In the unlikely event that South County

SNF Operations LLC does not receive CEC approval for the change in ownership of South

County, after review by the HSC and decision by RIDOH and exhaustion of all appeals thereto,

South County SNF Operations LLC will either (i) discontinue providing services that require a

Nursing Facility license, or (ii) within one hundred eighty (180) days thereafter will enter into an

agreement to sell or otherwise dispose of, any asset related solely to the provision of services

under the respective Nursing Facility license, and will take all reasonable steps necessary to

assist the third party in completing the CEC approval process related thereto, if any.

        4.      The parties shall cooperate with each other as may be reasonably requested by

either party with regard to the foregoing.

        5.      This Agreement will terminate automatically and immediately, without any

further action on the part of the parties, upon the parties’ performance of their respective

obligations, as set forth herein.

        6.      Genesis requested and received contingency payments from the State pursuant to

Rhode Island General Laws section 40-8-6.1. The State has determined that as of July 1, 2020,

Genesis owes the State a total due and payable contingency payment balance of $180,753.58/100

(“Contingency Payment”). The State shall recoup the Contingency Payment in full through a

mass adjustment against the claims to be paid by the State to Genesis in the July 2020 Medicaid

payment cycle for June 2020 Medicaid claims or subsequent claims cycles. In addition, there is

an outstanding balance of $11,211 owed by Genesis to the State for contingency payments for

cases currently pending eligibility determination (“Pending Contingency Payment”). Payment of

this Pending Contingency Payment is due to the State once eligibility is approved. Upon

determination of eligibility the State would recoup in full any Pending Contingency Payment

through a mass adjustment against claims to be paid to Genesis. This repayment of the

Contingency Payment and the Pending Contingency Payment is applicable only to the CEC of

South County.
