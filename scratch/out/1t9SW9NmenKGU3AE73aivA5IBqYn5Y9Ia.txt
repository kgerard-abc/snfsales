===== SLIDE 3 =====
Introduction
Greentree Nursing and Rehab LLC (the “Applicant”) seeks change in effective control approval to acquire the skilled nursing facility duly licensed and operated by 
Saint Elizabeth Manor, East Bay (“Saint Elizabeth Manor”). 
Saint Elizabeth Manor has a long history of providing quality care in the East Bay. 
The facility is licensed for 133 beds. 
It offers short-term rehabilitation, long-term nursing care, specialized memory care and hospice care. 
It is located at 1 Dawn Hill Road, Bristol, Rhode Island.
The Applicant will continue to provide such quality services to the residents.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
Saint Elizabeth Manor COVID-19 
Response & Vaccine Status
5
Saint Elizabeth Manor has worked diligently to protect its residents from COVID-19.  
It had very few cases from March through the end of December.  
At the end of December, the same week the first dose of the vaccine was received, the facility had an outbreak.
The facility has been COVID free since the December outbreak other than a few occasional staff cases.
54.4% of the residents and 54% of the staff have been vaccinated.
A number of residents were unable to receive the vaccine because of the antibody treatment.  They will be eligible to receive the vaccine in April (90 days following the treatment).
Saint Elizabeth Manor has provided education and information to all staff regarding the vaccine.
Saint Elizabeth Manor has maintained appropriate PPE in supply at all times.
The Applicant will continue this COVID-19 protocol.
===== SLIDE 6 =====
The Transaction
6
Pursuant to the terms of the Operations Transfer Agreement (“OTA”) dated as of November 9, 2020 between Saint Elizabeth Manor and Green Tree Healthcare Management, LLC (“Green Tree”), Green Tree will acquire Saint Elizabeth Manor’s operations.
Pursuant to the terms of a Contract of Sale dated as of November 9, 2020 between Saint Elizabeth Manor and Green Tree, Green Tree will purchase Saint Elizabeth Manor’s real estate.
Immediately at closing: 
Green Tree will assign the operations of the Facility to the Applicant.
Green Tree will assign the Contract of Sale to 1 Dawn Hill Property LLC, which shares ownership with the Applicant. 
Following the closing: 
The facility will be named The Dawn Hill Home for Rehab and Healthcare. 
The Applicant and Dawn Tree Property will enter into a Lease in which the Applicant will lease the real estate from Dawn Hill Property.
The Applicant will enter into a management agreement with Green Tree.
===== SLIDE 7 =====
The Transaction
7
The total capital cost for the transaction upon closing is $15,600,000.
Purchase Price: $15,100,000. 
Capital expenditures: $500,000. 
The Applicant is funding of the purchase price with 21% equity and 79% from a conventional mortgage. 
The Rhode Island Attorney General has provided approval for the transaction.
