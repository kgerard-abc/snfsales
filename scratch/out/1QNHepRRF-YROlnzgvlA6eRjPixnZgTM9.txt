                                Background
•   Greenwood Center, a 130 bed skilled nursing facility located at 1139 Main Street in
    Warwick, Rhode Island is currently operated by Genesis Healthcare, Inc. (“Genesis”) entities
    (the “Facility”).
•   On July 7, 2025 and September 25, 2025, the owner of the property and Genesis entity
    entered into a Purchase & Sale Agreement and Operations Transfer Agreement with
    Greenwood RI Propco LLC (the “Applicant”) and Greenwood Operations RI LLC,
    respectively, for the sale of the real estate housing the Facility and transfer of operations.
•   On July 9, 2025 Genesis filed a Chapter 11 Bankruptcy Petition in the United States
    Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Texas – Dallas Division.
•   The Applicant seeks Change in Effective Control (“CEC”) approval to purchase the real
    estate that houses the Facility.
•   Pursuant to the Purchase and Sale Agreement (Tab 14) dated July 7, 2025, the Applicant will
    purchase the real estate from the Current Property Owner for $7,250,000.
•   Subject to CEC approval, the Applicant will lease the real estate that houses the Facility to
    Greenwood Operations RI, LLC.
•   Closing is scheduled for December 8, 2025.
