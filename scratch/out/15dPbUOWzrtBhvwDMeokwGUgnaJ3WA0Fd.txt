Please provide an executive summary describing the nature and scope of the
proposal. Additionally, please include the following: (1) identification of all parties,
(2) description of the applicant and it's licensure track record, (3) the type of

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Con V Care (d.b.a. Woonsocket Health Centre) is a family owned and operated business.
The purpose of this proposal is the orderly transfer of the ownership of the shares of Con
V Care Inc, currently held in trust in the estate of former sole owner Norma M. Pezzelli
(deceased), and into the possession of her three children named as recipients of the
shares. The transaction will not result in any changes to the organization or daily
management of the facility or any changes in the bank financing that currently exists for
the facility. Administrators and key personnel will all remain in place. The continued
efficient management of the facility and provision of resident care at its highest possible
level remain the organization’s first priority.

Identification of Parties
The three recipients of the corporation's shares (equal ownership):

Peter J, Pezzelli, NHA — 75 shares
Paul M. Pezzelli, NHA — 75 shares
Lisa A. Pezzelli Hall — 75 shares

Description of Applicant

Con V Care Inc, has operated Woonsocket Health Centre since its opening in 1975,
Owned and operated by the Pezzelli family, it provides long and short stay skilled
nursing care, dementia care, and hospice, The organization has an established record of
excellence; it has routinely earned CMS ratings of 4 stars or higher.

Type of Transaction

Distribution of all of the issued and outstanding 225 shares of stock held in The Norma
Pezzelli Trust-2014 to the named beneficiaries.

Summary of Transfer Documents

Transfer documents shall consist solely of assignments from ‘The Norma M. Pezzelli
Trust- 2014 to the respective beneficiaries, the cancellation of the existing stock
certificates and the issuance of new stock certificates in the amounts and to the
beneficiaries listed above. There are no other transfer documents.
