Attached is the final Report of Health Services Council on the Application of Coventry
Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare LLC - Sole Member Zev Schwartz] for a Change in
Effective Control of Respiratory and Rehab Center of Rhode Island [LTC00789], a 210-Bed
Skilled Nursing Facility, Located in Coventry; Greenville Operations RI LLC [Mayflower
Healthcare LLC - Sole Member Zev Schwartz] for a Change in Effective Control of Greenville
Skilled Nursing and Rehabilitation [LTC00792], a 131-Bed Skilled Nursing Facility, Located in
Greenville; and Warren Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare LLC - Sole Member Zev
Schwartz] for a Change in Effective Control of Warren Skilled Nursing and Rehabilitation
[L1TC00796], a 63-Bed Skilled Nursing Facility, Located in Warren.

The Rhode Island Department of Health accepts the recommendation of the Health Services
Council and hereby approves the application and adopts the attached report in its entirety.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the existing
nursing facility license issued to Respiratory and Rehab Center of RI and the issuance of a new
nursing facility license to Coventry Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare LLC].

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the existing
nursing facility license issued to Greenville Skilled Nursing and Rehabilitation and the issuance
of a new nursing facility license to Greenville Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare LLC].

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the existing
nursing facility license issued to Warren Skilled Nursing and Rehabilitation and the issuance of a
new nursing facility license to Warren Operations RI LLC [Mayflower Healthcare LLC].
