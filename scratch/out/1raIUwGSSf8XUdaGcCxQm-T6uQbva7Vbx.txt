===== PAGE 3 =====
                            Background
• Coventry Operations RI LLC (“Coventry Operations”), Greenville Operations
  RI LLC (“Greenville Operations”), and Warren Operations RI LLC (“Warren
  Operations”) (collectively the “Applicants”) seek change in effective control
  approval to acquire Respiratory and Rehab Center of Rhode Island
  (“Respiratory”), Greenville Skilled Nursing and Rehabilitation (“Greenville”),
  and Warren Skilled Nursing and Rehabilitation (“Warren”), respectively.
• Respiratory, Greenville, and Warren are currently operated by Genesis
  Healthcare.
• Respiratory, a 210 bed nursing home located at 10 Woodland Drive, Coventry,
  Rhode Island, is licensed and operated by 10 Woodland Drive Operations
  LLC (“Respiratory Operator”). Respiratory is the only respiratory and
  rehabilitation center in Rhode Island.
• Greenville, a 131 bed nursing home located at 735 Putnam Pike, Greenville,
  Rhode Island, is licensed and operated by 735 Putnam Pike Operations LLC
  (“Greenville Operator”).
• Warren, a 63 bed nursing home located at 642 Metacom Ave., Warren, Rhode
  Island, is licensed and operated by 642 Metacom Avenue Operations LLC
  (“Warren Operator”).

===== PAGE 5 =====
                     Background Continued
•   Prior to the Applicants’ management, the facilities were facing numerous operational, quality and
    financial challenges. Compounding these challenges, the facilities had low star ratings and high
    turnover with agency staffing.
•   Pursuant to the RIDOH approved management agreements, the Applicants’ management team
    focus has been strengthening existing relationships with local officials and hospitals, stabilizing costs,
    and decreasing the case mix index, all of which have improved the financial condition of the facilities.
•   By way of example, Mr. Schwartz led the management team and worked closely with RIDOH’s
    Center for Health Facilities Regulations (“CHFR”) ultimately resulting in CHFR’s determination
    that, based on positive feedback and the lack of high-level citations, Respiratory met the
    requirements of a prior Compliance Order and discontinuance of a third-party clinical monitor.

     “Based upon positive feedback from the clinical consultant regarding the facility’s efforts to come
     into and remain in compliance, and the lack of high-level citations since the CO went into effect,
     the RIDOH feels that the facility has met the enforcement remedies to its satisfaction.

     Due to the success achieved, with the additional support of a clinical consultant, the RIDOH is
     hopeful that the facility will continue that relationship on some level to support the facility and
     maintain compliance.”

December 14, 2024 letter from Diane T. Pelletier, MSW, Chief, CHFR

                Background Continued
• In addition, under the Applicants’ management, annual survey outcomes have
  greatly improved, with far less citations and decreased scope and severity levels.
   – All three facilities are in good standing with RIDOH.
   – The Applicants prioritized decreasing turnover in key positions and turnover

   – The Applicants’ management team has focused on reducing Agency staffing

• Mr. Schwartz and his team have helped to stabilize operations at these facilities
  and will continue to provide quality short-term rehabilitation and long-term
  nursing care to the residents of the three facilities.

                  The Transactions
• Operations: Pursuant to the three Operations Transfer
  Agreements (“OTAs”) effective October 27, 2023, the Applicants
  agreed to acquire the operations and operational assets from
  Respiratory Operator, Greenville Operator and Warren Operator,
  respectively. See Tab 14.
• Real Estate: The properties that house the three licensed facilities
  are owned by OHI Asset (CT) Lender, LLC (“Property Owner”).
  The Property Owner currently leases the properties to Genesis
  OMG Operations LLC which in turn subleases to the Existing
  Operators. Subject to CEC approval, at closing of the proposed
  transaction, the Property Owner will enter into a Master Lease
  with Gentech RGW RI LLC (‘Gentech”). (Tab 24(A)). In turn,
  Gentech will sublease the premises to the Applicants. (Tab 24(B)).

===== PAGE 11 =====
  The Applicants Will Continue to Provide Safe
           and Adequate Treatment
• Respiratory offers a full range of nursing care and social services. It is
  the only respiratory and rehabilitation center in Rhode Island and only
  center to provide access to respiratory therapists 24 hours a day. 32 beds
  are fully equipped with state of the art ventilators and related medical
  equipment, piped in oxygen and suctioning.
• Greenville offers a comprehensive range of services designed to meet
  the diverse needs of its residents, including, but not limited to, skilled
  nursing care, dietary planning, medication management, rehabilitation
  therapy, and personalized care places.
• Warren offers physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy,
  subacute care, complex medical care, including wound treatment and
  pain management, and cardiac therapies.
• The Applicants do not anticipate adding, terminating, expanding, or
  reducing any services at the facilities.
