I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application Greenwood RI
Propco LLC (“Greenwood” or “Applicant”) for a Change in Effective Control of
Greenwood Propco LLC, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

As presented in the application Greenwood RI Propco LLC is seeking CEC approval to
purchase the real estate housing Greenwood Center, a 130-bed skilled nursing facility
operated by Harborside Rhode Island Limited Partnership. The property will then be
leased to Greenwood Operations RI LLC. The agreed purchase price for the property is
$7,250,000 with a $500,000 deposit. The applicant is owned by Jacob Taub, 50% and
Menachem (“Pinny”) Kofman, 50%.

III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicants filed two CEC applications of the subject-
licensed facility. This request is made because the statute requires that any proposed change in
owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council and
approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found its to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on RIDOH’s website and e-mail that the reviews would commence
on October 18, 2025. The notice also advised that all persons wishing to comment on the
applications submit their comments to the state agency by November 16, 2025, when practicable.
A letter, dated November 14, 2025, was submitted by Julia Harvey, Special Assistant Attorney
General and Katie Gradowski, Special Assistant Attorney General. The letter may be accessed

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.
         As presented by the Applicant and its legal counsel:

            •   “The principals of the Applicant have experience in owning and
                operating skilled nursing facilities.

            •   Jacob Taub, a 50% owner of the Applicant, is a managing partner of The
                Wellstone Group, which currently owns and operates 40 facilities in 3 states,

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that the request for the CEC application,
be approved.

Approval and implementation of the application will result in the acquisition of the real
estate housing Greenwood Center, a 130-bed skilled nursing facility.



VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total project costs for the real estate purchase shall not exceed
          $7,250,000; and

       2. that the Applicant implement the projects as approved.
