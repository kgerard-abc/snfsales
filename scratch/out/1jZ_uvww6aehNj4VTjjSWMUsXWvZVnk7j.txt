I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of Hope Nursing
Home Care, LLC [Community Based Care, LLC; Martis Partners III PV, LP] (the
“Applicant”) for a Change in Effective Control (“CEC”) of Home Care Services of Rhode
Island, Inc., a Home Nursing Care Provider agency located in Woonsocket, Rhode Island, be
approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

Hope Nursing Home Care, LLC is requesting Change in Effective Control approval of the
planned acquisition of the assets and business of Home Care Services of Rhode Island, Inc.
("HCS"), a Rhode Island corporation held by Vincent C. Ward. HCS is a licensed home
nursing care provider with a main office located at 68 Cumberland Street, Suite 206,
Woonsocket, Rhode Island 02895. The Applicant is part of the Community Based Care
family of home health care providers. In its application, Community Based Care states that
it has a main office located in Morrisville, North Carolina and operates home based care
providers in North Carolina, Virginia, Florida, and Rhode Island.

As stated in its application, HCS will be acquired via an Asset Purchase Agreement dated
September 22, 2022, which involves the Applicant acquiring certain assets and the
business of HCS for an aggregate purchase price of $540,000 subject to adjustment at
closing. The acquisition will be funded 100% through equity. Following the acquisition,
the assets and the business of HCS, including the current HCS location in Woonsocket,
will added to the operations of the Applicant.

The Applicant states in its application that one hundred percent (100%) of the membership
interests of the Applicant are held by CBC Rhode Island FT, LLC, which in turn is 100%
owned by Community Based Care FT Acquisition, LLC which is 100% owned by
Community Based Care Intermediate I, LP. Community Based Care Intermediate I, LP is
99% owned by Community Based Care Holdings, LP and 1% owned by Martis Partners III
Community Aggregator, LP. Community Based Care Holdings, LP is owned 14.89%
collectively by individuals, 12.48% collectively by co-investors, and 72.63% by Martis
Partners III Community Aggregator, LP. Martis Partners III Community Aggregator, L.P.
is 18.58% owned by Martis Partners III Community Blocker, LP, 1.51% owned by Martis
Partners III Community Blocker, LLC, and 79.91% owned by Martis Partners III, LP.
Martis Partners III Community Aggregator, LP is managed by Martis Partners III GP,
LLC.

Martis Partners III Community Blocker, LP and Martis Partners III Community Blocker,
LLC are each 100% owned by Martis Partners III PV, LP. Martis Partners III, LP and
Martis Partners III PV, LP are owned by passive investors that commit capital to Martis
but do not have involvement in or control of investment decisions made by Martis or the
day-to-day operations of Applicant. Community Based Care Intermediate I, LP also owns
100% of the stock of Community Based Care Acquisition, Inc., which owns 100% of the
membership interest of Community Based Care, LLC, Community Based Care, LLC in
turn owns interests in several home health providers and is currently operating in four (4)
states.

III.      INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed an application for a CEC of the
subject-licensed facility. This request is made because the statute requires that any proposed
change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council
and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the review would commence on February 11, 2023. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by March 13,
2023, when practicable. No public or written comments were received.

review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a CEC. The Council's comments and
findings on each of the criteria follow:

          the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.

          Carolina, Virginia, Florida, and Rhode Island and following the acquisition, the
          assets and the business of HCS, including the current HCS location in Woonsocket,
          will added to the operations of the Applicant.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that this request for a CEC be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing home nursing care provider license issued to Home Care Services of Rhode Island,
Inc., and the issuance of a new home nursing care provider license to Hope Nursing Home
Care, LLC, whose ownership is depicted in the post-closing chart contained within this
report.


VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the acquisition price not exceed $540,000 and to be financed with 100%
          equity;

       2. that the Applicant implement the project as approved;

       3. that the Applicant shall conduct national background checks on its employees;

       4. that the Applicant attain and maintain accreditation from a nationally recognized
          accrediting agency;

       5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization, and demographic
          patient information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

       6. that the Applicant will provide services to all patients without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay;

       7. that the Applicant establish and put into effect formal agreements for referral of
          charity care cases with a minimum of one 501(c)(3) licensed community health
          center in the Applicant’s service area within sixty (60) days of approval; and

       8. that the Applicant cooperate with 501(c)(3) providers who refer clients for charity
          care services to the Applicant’s facility to pre-certify such referred individuals to
          qualify for charity care services at the Applicant’s facility.
