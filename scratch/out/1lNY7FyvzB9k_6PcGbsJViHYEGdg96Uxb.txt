===== SLIDE 3 =====
Introduction
4000 Post Road Eden Operations, LLC (the “Applicant”) seeks change in effective control approval to acquire the operations of Brentwood Nursing Home Inc. (Brentwood/Facility), a 96 bed skilled nursing facility offering short-term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care.
Brentwood is located at 4000 Post Road, Warwick, Rhode Island.
The Applicant will continue to provide quality short-term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care to Brentwood’s residents.

===== SLIDE 6 =====
The Transaction
6
Pursuant to the terms of an Asset Purchase and Sales Agreement (“APA”) and Operations Transfer Agreement (“OTA”) entered into on August 4, 2021, and amended November 3, 2021, 4000 Post Road Eden Realty, LLC (“PropCo Buyer”) and the Applicant agreed to acquire Brentwood’s real estate and operations, respectively. 
The PropCo Buyer closed on the Facility real estate on December 31, 2021. On December 30, 2021, the Applicant and the current operator entered into a management agreement effective January 1, 2022.  Pursuant to the management agreement, the Applicant is managing Brentwood, under the direction of the current operator, pending receipt of CEC approval.

===== SLIDE 8 =====
The Applicant Will Continue to Provide Safe and Adequate Treatment 
8
The Applicant will continue all current services including skilled nursing services, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech and language therapy, post-surgical care, medication management and monitoring, IV therapy, and diabetes care. 
The Applicant will continue to ensure quality improvement (see Quality Assurance Policy at Tab 22). 
The Quality Assurance Policy is designed to identify the high risk, problem prone and high-volume areas to evaluate for improvement and identify, collect and use data relevant to the unique characteristics and needs of the resident. 
The Applicant will continue to provide resident-centered practices to ensure that each resident receives the highest level of care.
