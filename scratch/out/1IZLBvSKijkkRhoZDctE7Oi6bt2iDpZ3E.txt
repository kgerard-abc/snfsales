Hope Nursing Home Care is a licensed home nursing care provider in Cranston.
Hope Nursing is part of the Community Based Care family of providers.
Home Care Services of Rhode Island, Inc. is a home nursing care provider with a main office in Woonsocket, Rhode Island.
Hope Nursing plans to acquire substantially all of the of the assets and business of Hope Nursing Home Care and continue to provide services at the current Woonsocket location as a Hope Nursing location.
$540,000 purchase price.
Hope Nursing will continue to operate in Rhode Island out of its existing Cranston main office and the new Woonsocket office location as part of the Community Based Care family of healthcare providers and will continue to operate as a home nursing care provider.
Community Based Care currently operates out of 111 locations in North Carolina, Virginia, Florida and Rhode Island.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
Hope Nursing Home Care provides home nursing care services to Rhode Island patients who require assistance with everyday living, including health education and supportive services to those in need.
Home Care Services of Rhode Island include personal care, homemaking and companion care.
Hope Nursing intends to continue with the mission established by Home Care Services of Rhode Island to provide home nursing care services to support patients and their families. 
Hope Nursing does not anticipate that any services will be added, terminated, expanded or reduced from those currently offered and intends to provide services within the scope of its license.
Using its patient centric local approach to care that is supported by the resources and experience of the larger Community Based Care organization, Hope Nursing plans to improve efficiency of care in a cost effective manner. 
Hope Nursing believes that it has the experience to effectively manage costs without adversely affecting care delivery and is well positioned to respond to rapidly evolving health care reform efforts.
