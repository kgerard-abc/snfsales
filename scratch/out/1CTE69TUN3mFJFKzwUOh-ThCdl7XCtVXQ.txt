===== PAGE 3 =====
                               Background
•   Under the leadership of David Ryan, Health Concepts, Ltd. and affiliated entities have
    provided high quality nursing facility services for decades at the following skilled nursing
    facilities:
     – Heritage Hills Nursing & Rehabilitation Center (“Heritage Hills”), Smithfield, Rhode
          Island
     – Morgan Health Center (“Morgan”), Johnston, Rhode Island
     – Riverview Healthcare Community (“Riverview”), Cumberland, Rhode Island
     – Westerly Health Center (“Westerly”), Westerly, Rhode Island
     – Eastgate Nursing & Rehabilitation (“Eastgate”), Cumberland, Rhode Island
     – South Kingstown Nursing & Rehabilitation (“South Kingstown”), West Kingstown,
          Rhode Island
     – West Shore Health Center (“West Shore”), Warwick, Rhode Island
     – Bayberry Commons (“Bayberry”), Pascoag, Rhode Island
     – Village House Nursing & Rehabilitation Center (“Village House”), Newport, Rhode
          Island
     – Elmwood Nursing & Rehabilitation Center (“Elmwood”), Providence, Rhode Island
•   On September 2, 2022, RIDOH issued Change in Effective Control approval to Tryko
    affiliated entities and Kadima Healthcare Group (“Kadima”) for the sale of (1) Heritage
    Hills, Morgan, Riverview and Westerly, and (2) Eastgate, South Kingstown, West Shore,
    Bayberry, Village House and Elmwood, respectively.
     – The sale of the 4 facilities to Tryko affiliated entities was consummated on November
          1, 2022.
     – The sale of the 6 facilities to Kadima was not consummated.

===== PAGE 4 =====
                 Background Continued
•   Michael Bigney, Kelly Arnold, Christopher Ryan, Stephanie Ryan and related trusts are
    seeking approval to acquire the ownership interest in the six facilities and associated real
    estate:
     – 198 Waterman Avenue LLC d/b/a Eastgate, with 76 beds located in Providence.
     – Allen’s Health Center Inc. d/b/a South Kingstown, with 112 beds located in West
         Kingstown.
     – Warwick Health Center, Inc. d/b/a West Shore, with 149 beds located in Warwick.
     – Burrillville Health Center Associates Limited d/b/a Bayberry, with 120 beds located
         in Pascoag.
     – Village House Convalescent Home, Inc. d/b/a Village House, with 95 beds located in
         Newport.
     – Elmwood Health Center, Inc. d/b/a Elmwood, with 70 beds located in Providence
         (collectively, the “Applicants”).
•   Pursuant to the terms of the Agreement, Michael Bigney, Kelly Arnold, Christopher Ryan,
    Stephanie Ryan and related trusts will acquire 100% ownership interest in the Applicants,
    associated real estate, as well as Health Concepts, Ltd., the management company for the
    Facilities (the “Transaction”).
•   Pursuant to the terms of the Transaction, on January 1, 2024, Michael Bigney purchased a
    49% interest in Health Concepts, Ltd., and became its CFO.
•   All current skilled nursing care provided at the Facilities will remain following the
    Transaction.

===== PAGE 5 =====
                              The Applicants
•   The ownership group for each of the six facilities will be:
     – Michael Bigney and related trusts, 70%
     – Kelly Arnold and related trust 10%
     – Christopher Ryan and related trust 10%
     – Stephanie Ryan 10%
•   Michael Bigney is the co-owner of two HNCPs and one Hospice Provider with over 41 years of
    experience. He is also the co-owner of ProCare, a physical therapy practice in Cranston, Rhode Island
    and serves as the CFO of Health Concepts, Ltd. Mr. Bigney is the immediate past president of the
    Rhode Island Partnership for Home Care, a trade association representing licensed home care, home
    nursing care and hospice agencies in Rhode Island.
•   Kelly Arnold has over 20 years of clinical and managerial experience in the long-term care setting and
    was the Nursing Home Administrator in 3 Health Concepts facilities before becoming COO in 2017.
    Ms. Arnold received her NHA license in 1998 and has been with Health Concepts for approximately
    25 years. She is the current Board Chair of RIHCA, a trade association representing more than 80%
    of the nursing homes in Rhode Island.
•   Christopher Ryan has been with Health Concepts for approximately 21 years, first in the Information
    Technology department before serving as a Nursing Home Administrator and Director of Strategic
    Planning, receiving his NHA license in 2015.
•   Stephanie Ryan has more than 30 years of clinical and managerial experience in various health care
    settings. Ms. Ryan is a co-owner of Nursing Placement and has been with the company for
    approximately 15 years and is also a co-owner of ProCare and Nursing Placement Hospice and
    Palliative Care LLC.

===== PAGE 6 =====
                   The Transaction
• Pursuant to the Agreement by and between Health Concepts,
  Ltd., David Ryan as Trustee of various trusts currently owning
  the Facilities (“Current Operators”) and respective property
  owners (collectively, “Sellers”) and Michael Bigney and nominees
  (the “Buyer”) the Applicants are proposing to acquire full
  ownership interest in the 6 Facilities currently managed by Health
  Concepts, Ltd. and related real estate, as well as acquisition of
  Health Concepts, Ltd. See Tab B.
• The total purchase price for the six Facilities, related real estate
  and Health Concepts, Ltd. is $10,000,000.
• All employees of Health Concepts, Ltd. and the Facilities will be
  retained to ensure continued oversight for optimal compliance
  with state and federal regulators.

===== PAGE 19 =====
                Character, Competence, Commitment, and
                      Standing in the Community
•   The ownership group of the Applicants combined has 100 years of experience in the healthcare industry, with 61
    years of combined long term care in the Health Concepts umbrella.
•   David Ryan, seeking responsible, resident focused successors, is confident the new ownership group will continue the
    high quality care his family has provided to residents for 50 years.
•   The current Health Concepts leadership will remain with the new ownership group after the Transaction closes and
    each Administrator at the six Facilities will be staying on after the Transaction closes.
•   Each of the six Facilities offers high quality care, as evidenced below, which the new ownership group intends to
    continue.
      • Each of the six Facilities has been ranked as one of Americas Greatest Workplaces by Newsweek in 2023.
      • Eastgate has a 5 Star Rating from CMS and joined the Health Concepts family in 2012.
      • South Kingstown has a 5 Star Rating from CMS and joined the Health Concepts family in 2000.
      • West Shore has a 4 Star Rating from CMS and joined the Health Concepts family in 1999. It was also awarded
           the American Health Care Association (“ACHA”) Silver Achievement in Quality Award.
      • Bayberry joined the Health Concepts family in 1985 and has a 3 Star Rating from CMS. It has been awarded the
           following awards:
              • Bronze AHCA Quality Award 2009
              • Rhode Island Healthcare Quality Award 2010
              • Silver AHCA Quality Award 2012
              • Rhode Island Health Care Social Worker of the Year – 2015
              • Rhode Island Health Care Administrator of the Year – 2015
      • Village House joined the Health Concepts family in 1974 and has a 5 Star Rating from CMS and was awarded
           the AHCA Silver Achievement in Quality Award.
      • Elmwood has been part of the Health Concepts family since 1975 and has a 5 Star Rating from CMS. It is also
           the recipient of the AHCA Silver Achievement in Quality Award.

===== PAGE 20 =====
 The Applicants Will Continue to Provide
      Safe and Adequate Treatment
• The Applicants will continue to provide high quality care currently
  offered by Health Concepts and the Facilities.
• There will be no change in services provided at the Facilities
  following the Transaction.
• The Applicants will maintain an ongoing facility-wide Quality
  Assurance and Performance Improvement (“QAPI”) Committee at
  the Facilities to oversee the QAPI Plan (see Tab 22).
    • The QAPI Plan details the facilities activities and objectives
       designed to monitor and evaluate the quality of resident care,
       pursue methods to improve care quality, and resolve identified
       problems.

   Financing and Financial Viability
• The total purchase price for the Transaction is $10,000,000.
• The Applicants have already funded the Transaction with a $2,550,000 cash infusion,
  representing a 25.5% equity position.
• The Applicants have sufficient funds for capital and operating needs and have 30 days of
  operating expenses available.
• As evidenced in the pro forma provided in Appendix A of the respective Applications,
  there are adequate business resources to support these acquisitions. In addition, Michael
  Bigney will provide any additional finances required. See Tab H evidence of sufficient
  personal finances.

===== PAGE 22 =====
Access to Underserved Populations
•   The Applicants are committed to providing access to underserved populations and do not
    discriminate on the basis of race, creed, color, religion, national origin, sex, gender, handicap,
    disability, blindness, source of sponsorship, service of payment, marital status, age, sexual
    preference, or genetic disposition in the admission, retention and care of residents and
    patients.
•   The Applicants will continue to provide high level care to traditionally underserved
    populations in accordance with a robust charity care policy. See Charity Care Policy at Tab D.
•   The Applicants will continue serving the Medicaid population at the Facilities, which are
    approximately:
     •   Bayberry – 57%
     •   Eastgate – 61%
     •   Elmwood – 61%
     •   South Kingstown – 43%
     •   Village House – 34%
     •   West Shore – 63%
•   The Applicants will ensure that access to services is maintained for all existing patients without
    interruption or dislocation.
