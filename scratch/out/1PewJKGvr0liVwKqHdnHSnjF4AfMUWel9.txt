I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of South County
SNF Operations LLC for the Change in Effective Control of South County Nursing and
Rehabilitation Center, Skilled Nursing Facility located at 740 Oak Hill Road, North
Kingstown, Rhode Island, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

As presented in the application, South County Nursing & Rehabilitation Center is a licensed
skilled nursing facility located at 740 Oak Hill Road, North Kingstown. The facility is
licensed to operate 120 beds and offers short-term rehabilitative care, long-term care,
memory care, and hospice care.

On June 30, 2020, the South County SNF Operations LLC (the “Applicant”) and the Rhode
Island Department of Health entered into a consent agreement allowing the transaction to close
and operate the Facility while maintaining the Facility license in full force and effect pending
CEC approval.

South County SNF Consent Agreement

As presented, the Applicant agreed to assume operations of South County Nursing and
Rehabilitation from 740 Oak Hill Road Operations, LLC, a Genesis Healthcare, Inc.
Subsidiary and pursuant to the terms of the Asset Purchase Agreement effective February
28, 2020, 780 Oak Hill Road SNF Real Estate LLC agreed to purchase the facility real
estate from Oak Hill Road Property, a subsidiary of Genesis HealthCare, Inc.

As presented, the Applicant is a newly formed entity. South County Operations Holdings
LLC (“SCOH”), a Delaware limited liability company, is the sole member of the New
Operator. SCOH is owned by Deborah Bennet (4% membership interest); Sabrina 1818
Holdings LLC (43.5% membership interest; Shimon Idels, Manager), Lion 26 Holdings
LLC (47.5% membership interest; Steven Schwartz, Manager), and Delilah 2626 Holdings
LLC (5% membership interest; Joseph Lieberman, Manager). Cathy Donnelly is the
President and Administrator of the Applicant. Shimon Idels and Steven Schwartz are the
Managers of SCOH. The Applicant plans on engaging a management company to provide
management services to the Facility. The management company will be HVH South
County Management LLC, a Rhode Island limited liability company, owned by Hill
Valley Healthcare, LLC (“Hill Valley”) a Delaware limited liability company.

The applicant states it is dedicated to serving the traditionally underserved populations and
will continue to ensure the access to services is maintained for all patients without
interruption or dislocation.

As presented in the application, the total project cost $12,360,000 to be financed with
$2,472,000 (20%) equity and $9,888,000 (80%) debt.

III.      INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed an application for a change in
effective control of the subject-licensed facility. This request is made because the statute
requires that any proposed change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility
be reviewed by the Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to
implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant
and the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and
e-mail that the review would commence on June 9, 2021. The notice also advised that all
persons wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by
July 10, 2021, when practicable.

       A. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
          the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that this request for a change in effective
control be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to South County Nursing & Rehabilitation Center,
and the issuance of a new nursing facility license to South County SNF Operations LLC.


VI.    CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total project cost not exceed $12,360,000 and that debt not exceed
          $9,888,000;

       2. that the Applicant shall comply with the rules and regulations, Licensing of
          Nursing Facilities (216-RICR-40-10-1);

       3. that the Applicant maintains an accreditation from a nationally recognized
          accrediting agency;

       4. that the Applicant shall conduct national background checks on its employees;

       5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
          patient information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request; and

       6. that the Applicant will provide services to all patients without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay.

       7. that the Applicant will provide, through administrative and operational policies
          and procedures individualized and resident-centered care, services and
          accommodations; and
