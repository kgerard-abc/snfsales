===== SLIDE 3 =====
The Transaction
Bayview Operator, LLC d/b/a Bayview Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center at Scalabrini (the “Applicant”) seeks approval to purchase Scalabrini Villa, Inc. (“Scalabrini”)
Scalabrini is a licensed skilled nursing facility, located 
at 860 North Quidnessett Road, North Kingstown.
The facility is licensed to operate 120 beds and offers short-term rehabilitative care, long-term care, and Alzheimer’s and dementia programs. 
3
===== SLIDE 4 =====
The Transaction 
Pursuant to the terms of the Asset Purchase Agreement and Operations Transfer Agreement, both effective January 18, 2021:
Bayview Property, LLC (an affiliated entity of the Applicant) will purchase the facility property from The Pious Society of the Missionaries of Charles Boromeo (“The Pious Society”). 
The Applicant will acquire the operational assets from Scalabrini. 
Contemporaneous with the closing, Bayview Property will lease the facility space to the Applicant. 
Total investment of $12,000,000, including $8,000,000 for the purchase price of the facility property and $4,000,000 in capital expenditures. 
The purchase price will be funded entirely with equity.
At closing, Bayview Property will pay The Pious Society $6,500,000.
Beginning on the first anniversary of the Closing Date and the subsequent four (4) anniversaries, Bayview Property will pay $300,000 towards the Purchase Price until the remaining $1,500,000 is paid.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
Bayview Operator, LLC
The Applicant and its affiliates have a proven history of providing high-quality long-term care services to their nursing facility residents. 
The Applicant is part of a long-standing family of nursing facilities operating 35 skilled nursing facilities in 7 states: Rhode Island, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Florida and Virginia. 
The Applicant’s affiliated entities recently received CEC approval for Lincolnwood Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center located at 610 Smithfield Road in North Providence, Rhode Island (f/k/a Hopkins Manor) and, since 2016, own and operate Elmhurst Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center located at 50 Maude Street in Providence, Rhode Island. 
The affiliates are fully experienced with the Rhode Island market and maintain an exemplary record among the State’s long-term care providers.

===== SLIDE 6 =====
COVID-19 Preparation & Response
Implemented COVID-19 Task Force to review guidance and provide facility support.
Conducted weekly COVID-19 education calls with facilities.
Held weekly Medical Director calls led by Chief Medical Officer.
Partnered with infectious disease physicians for guidance.
Hired a Director of Infection Prevention for facility support.
Ensured PPE supply and developed policies for PPE optimization.
Established policies, procedures, systems, and processes surrounding resident/staff screening, testing, reporting, cohorting, cleaning/disinfecting, communication and notification, and visitation – virtual and in-person.

===== SLIDE 7 =====
COVID-19 Post Pandemic Response 
A physician-driven advisory board has been developed to support and guide the facilities.
Dr. Greg Allen has been added as Regional Chief Medical Director for additional support to facilities’.
Increased focus on post-pandemic infections and antibiotic stewardship.
Continued development of regional infection prevention and control program to provide additional facility oversight.
Increased focus on COVID-19 vaccination acceptance for residents and employees.
Continued focus on guidance changes to ensure compliance.
Focus on the development of in-house specialty program to meet community and provider needs, i.e., hemodialysis, behavior health/memory care, and ventilator-dependent.

===== SLIDE 10 =====
Character, Commitment, Competence and Standing in the Community
The Applicant and its affiliates are third-generation operators; currently with 35 skilled nursing facilities in 7 states.
Several nursing facility affiliates have received four-and five-star ratings in the “Best Nursing Homes” listing. 
Elmhurst Rehabilitation & Healthcare Center has been affiliated with the Applicant since 2016 and maintains a solid and consistent reputation for competence and commitment to the community. 
Lincolnwood was recently acquired by the Applicant’s affiliate and under its operation, is providing quality care to the community. 
The Applicant and its affiliates are in good standing and are dedicated to providing quality services to residents across jurisdictions.

===== SLIDE 11 =====
The Applicant Will Continue to Provide Safe and Adequate Treatment
The Applicant will establish and maintain an ongoing facility-wide Quality Assurance and Performance Improvement (“QAPI”) Committee at the Facility to oversee the QAPI Plan. 
The QAPI Plan details the facility’s activities and objectives designed to monitor and evaluate the quality of resident care, pursue methods to improve care quality, and resolve identified problems.
The Applicant and its affiliates bring extensive experience to quality improvement activities through their management of multiple nursing homes across its facilities. 
The Applicant is committed to maintaining the facility’s existing array of services and making long-term improvements, such as significantly updating the therapy gym. 
The Applicant anticipates pursuing Joint Commission accreditation following the purchase.

===== SLIDE 12 =====
Financing and Financial Viability
The Applicant brings extensive financial resources to the Facility. 
The $12,000,000 investment is being funded entirely with equity. 
The purchase price is $8,000,000.
The Applicant will invest an additional $4,000,000 of its own funds for capital improvements to the Facility. 
The Applicant and its affiliates have demonstrated decades of achieving consistent financial success in the long-term care industry in both Rhode Island and other jurisdictions.

===== SLIDE 13 =====
Access to Underserved Populations
The Applicant is committed to providing access to underserved populations and does not discriminate on the basis of race, creed, color, religion, national origin, sex, gender, handicap, disability, blindness, source of sponsorship, service of payment, marital status, age, sexual preference, or genetic disposition in the admission, retention and care of residents and patients. 
The Applicant intends to serve a patient mix of approximately 60% Medicaid and 15% Medicare at the Facility. 
The Applicant will ensure that access to services is maintained for all existing patients without interruption or dislocation.
