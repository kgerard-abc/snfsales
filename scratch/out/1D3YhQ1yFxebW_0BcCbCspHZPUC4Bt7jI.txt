===== SLIDE 2 =====
Overview of Presentation	
We were engaged to help the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) understand the transaction contemplated by Health Concepts to sell and transfer ownership of 10 nursing facilities to Tryko and Kadima (“Transaction”) 
This presentation reflects our understanding of the Transaction, as memorialized in the Transaction documents and the supplemental documentation we received from the transacting parties
Capitalized terms used herein are either from the Transaction documents directly, or were based on defined terms in the Transaction documents but were renamed for the ease of explaining the Transaction in the context of this presentation
2
===== SLIDE 3 =====
Proposed Deal Summary
Health Concepts currently owns and operates 10 nursing facilities in Rhode Island
It desires to sell all 10 facilities
4 of the facilities would be sold directly to Tryko, which currently operates other nursing facilities in Rhode Island
6 of the facilities would be sold indirectly to Kadima, which currently owns and operates 6 nursing facilities in Pennsylvania, but none in Rhode Island
The purchase price for the overall deal is: $86M 
3
===== SLIDE 4 =====
Transaction History
July 2021:
Health Concepts entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement (“APA”) with Tryko to sell the real estate/facility assets of its 10 nursing facilities to Tryko
Health Concepts entered into 10 Operations Transfer Agreements (“OTAs”) with Tryko to transition operations of the 10 nursing facilities to Tryko
October 2021:
Tryko entered into a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (“MIPA”) with Kadima to transfer Tryko’s ownership interests in various entities that are parties to the APA and OTAs with Health Concepts 
As a result, Kadima will become the ultimate owner of those entities, which are acquiring real estate/facility assets or are parties to OTAs with respect to 6 nursing facilities 
4
===== SLIDE 5 =====
Complicated Structure
The Transaction is complicated because:
The number of legal entities involved
There are 10 nursing facilities, each of which will have New Real Estate Owners and New Licensees/Operators, and some of which will have Interim Licensees/Operators 
The agreements are entered into by named parties “or [their] designees” 
E.g., The Interim Licensees/Operators are Tryko designees, and serve as place holder entities
The number of assignments to other entities
Because there was an APA, and then a MIPA, the transaction contemplates Tryko assigning certain rights and designating certain new entities to step in its shoes as the parties to particular Transaction documents
Some of the entities have very complicated ownership structures
This is particularly true with respect to the ownership structures of the Tryko entities

===== SLIDE 7 =====
The Asset Purchase Agreement (“APA”)
The APA was executed in July 2021 between Health Concepts and Tryko
Under the APA, Tryko will acquire the real estate/facility assets of the 10 nursing facilities from Health Concepts
Tryko (through its 
“Purchaser” 
RSO Investments LLC) will buy the real estate and physical facility assets of all 10 nursing facilities
Tryko will simultaneously assign Purchaser’s right under the APA to buy:
4 of the 10 nursing facilities to 4 other Tryko owned entities: “
Tryko Facility Purchaser Entities
”
6 of the 10 nursing facilities to 6 “
Single Purpose Entities
” that Tryko also owns 
All 10 Tryko Facility Purchaser Entities and Single Purpose Entities will be under common ownership with the Purchaser, 
RSO Investments 
LLC, and ultimately owned by Tryko through Rainwood Holdings LLC
7
===== SLIDE 8 =====
The Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (“MIPA”)
The MIPA was executed in October 2021 between Tryko and Kadima
Under the MIPA, Tryko will transfer its membership interests (i.e., ownership) in the 6 Single Purpose Entities to Kadima
Under the APA, the 6 Single Purpose Entities will acquire real estate/facility assets of 6 nursing facilities 
Thus, by transferring the ownership of the 6 Single Purpose Entities, under the MIPA, Kadima will become the real estate/facility asset owner of 6 nursing facilities
The MIPA does not impact the other 4 nursing 
facilities 
Tryko is retaining the ownership interests in the 4 Tryko Facility Purchaser Entities
Tryko will become the new real estate owner of 4 nursing facilities

===== SLIDE 10 =====
The 10 Operations Transfer Agreements (“OTAs”)
The 10 OTAs were executed in July 2021 between the current Health Concepts operators and Tryko
Under the OTAs, the operations of each facility will transfer from the current operators to 10 “
Interim Operators
” (one per nursing facility) 
The Interim Operators will all be owned by Tryko, through Rainwood Holdings, LLC
The OTAs (and all assignments to be described) will become effective simultaneously at Closing
The Interim Operators will exercise no ownership or control over the facilities
10
===== SLIDE 11 =====
4 Operations Transfer Agreements (“OTAs”)
Interim Operators are defined to include Tryko designees
For 4 nursing facilities, Tryko will assign the 4 OTAs from Interim Operators to other Tryko affiliates
Tryko, through the other Tryko affiliates, will become the “
New Licensee/Operator
” of 4 nursing facilities
11
===== SLIDE 12 =====
6 Operations Transfer Agreements (“OTAs”)
For 6 facilities, Tryko will assign the OTAs from the Interim Operators to the respective 
“Ultimate Licensee/Operator”
The Interim Operators are owned by Tryko
The Ultimate Licensee/Operators will initially be owned by Tryko
The Ultimate Licensee/Operators will be parties to the respective 6 OTAs
Under the MIPA, Tryko will transfer its membership interests (i.e., ownership) in the 6 Ultimate Licensee/Operators to Kadima
Kadima will become a party to the 6 OTAs, and the owner of the Ultimate Licensee/Operators of 6 nursing facilities
