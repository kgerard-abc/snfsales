===== PAGE 3 =====
                                      Background
•   Pascoag Health Center Inc. and Pascoag Properties LLC, whose principals include Michael
    Bigney, Kelly Arnold, Christopher Ryan, Stephanie Ryan and related trusts (collectively, the
    “Applicants’ Principals”) are seeking approval to acquire the ownership interest in the
    operations and real estate of Crystal Lake Rehabilitation and Care Center (“Crystal Lake” or
    “Facility”).
•   Crystal Lake, a 71-bed skilled nursing facility located at 999 South Main Street, Pascoag, Rhode
    Island, provides residents with short term nursing and rehabilitation services as well as long
    term care services in a home like environment.
•   All current skilled nursing care provided at the Facility will remain following the Transaction.
•   On December 12, 2024, RIDOH approved the sale of six Rhode Island skilled nursing facilities
    to Applicants’ Principals.
     –   Eastgate Nursing & Rehabilitation (“Eastgate”), East Providence, Rhode Island
     –   South Kingstown Nursing & Rehabilitation (“South Kingstown”), West Kingstown, Rhode Island
     –   West Shore Health Center (“West Shore”), Warwick, Rhode Island
     –   Bayberry Commons (“Bayberry”), Pascoag, Rhode Island
     –   Village House Nursing & Rehabilitation Center (“Village House”), Newport, Rhode Island
     –   Elmwood Nursing & Rehabilitation Center (“Elmwood”), Providence, Rhode Island
•   The Applicants will continue to provide quality, short-term rehabilitation and long-
    term nursing care to the residents at Crystal Lake.

===== PAGE 5 =====
                        The Transaction
• Pursuant to the Operations Transfer Agreement by and between CL Operating
  LLC (“Old Opco”) and Pascoag Health Center, Inc. (“New Opco”), New Opco
  will acquire full ownership interest in Facility. See Tab B.
• Likewise, pursuant to the Contract of Sale, by and between RI Equity Group
  LLC (“Old Propco”) and Pascoag Properties LLC, (“New Propco”), New Propco
  will acquire the property where the Facility resides. Id.
• The total purchase price for the Facility and related real estate is $5,500,000.
• The New Propco will assume the outstanding balance of the existing HUD
  insured mortgage loan on the property.
• At closing, the New Opco will enter into a lease agreement with New Propco at
  fair market value.
• All employees at the Facility will be retained to ensure continued oversight for
  optimal compliance with state and federal regulators.
