I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of Holiday
Operator, LLC and Holiday Property, LLC (“Applicant”) for a Change in Effective Control
(“CEC”) of Holiday Retirement Home, Inc., located in Manville, Rhode Island, be
approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

As presented in the application holiday operator, LLC is seeking CEC approval for the
skilled nursing facility. The current owner and seller of the facility is the holiday
retirement home, inc.
Under the proposed transaction, Holiday Property, LLC (“Holiday Property”), which
shares common ownership with the Applicant, will acquire the real estate of the skilled
nursing facility. Following the acquisition, Holiday Property will lease the facility to the
Applicant for ongoing operations.
The facility is a 170-bed nursing facility providing short-term rehabilitation and long-term
care services. Services offered include rehabilitation, memory care, and skilled nursing
care.
The total purchase price is sixteen million one hundred fifty thousand dollars
($16,150,000.00). Following the acquisition, Holiday Property will lease the facility to its
affiliated entity, Holiday Operator LLC.
Also, after closing, the Applicant, through its affiliated entities, plans to invest
approximately five million one hundred thousand dollars ($5,100,000.00) in capital
improvements to the facility. These improvements will address necessary upgrades and
repairs, including the HVAC system, building exterior, resident rooms, lobby/entrance,
nursing stations, corridors, lounges, and other common areas, with the goal of creating a
more modern, home-like environment for residents and enhancing the therapy gym,
rehabilitation capabilities, and related equipment.
The Applicant, through its affiliates, collectively owns and operates 81 skilled nursing and
assisted living facilities across eight states, including Maryland, Massachusetts, New
Hampshire, New Jersey, Rhode Island, Pennsylvania, Florida, and Virginia, seven of
which are located in Rhode Island.
The Rhode Island facilities include:
   •   Bayview Operator LLC d/b/a Bayview Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center at

III.       INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island, titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed a CEC application of the subject-
licensed facility. This request is made because the statute requires that any proposed change in
owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council and
approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.
Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the review would commence on February 18, 2026. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by March 18,
2026, when practicable.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that the request for the CEC application,
be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing skilled nursing facility license (LTC00588) issued Holiday Retirement Home,
Inc., and the issuance of a new skilled nursing facility license to Holiday Operator, LLC.
Holiday Property, LLC will own the real estate.


VI.    CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total purchase price shall not exceed $22,289,225.

       2. that the Applicant implement the project as approved;

       3. that within two years the Applicant attain and maintain an accreditation from a
          nationally recognized accrediting agency;

       4. that the Applicant shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
          employees prior to employment;

       5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
          resident information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

       6. that the Applicant will provide services to all residents without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay;

       7. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
          alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
          Further that the Applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge the
          patient while an application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid) is
          pending; and

       8. that the Applicant provide, through administrative and operational policies and
          procedures individualized and resident-centered care, services, and
          accommodations.
