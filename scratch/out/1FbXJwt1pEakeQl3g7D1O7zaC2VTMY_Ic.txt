The Facilities, prior to the Applicants’ management, were facing numerous operational, quality,
and financial challenges. Compounding these challenges, the Facilities had low star ratings and
high turnover and agency use. The population at the facilities is not composed of the “typical”
geriatric SNF residents. Instead, these facilities serve not only geriatric patients, but also a
younger population that often presents with complex, multifaceted conditions, which, in addition
to physical health challenges, may include behavioral health conditions.

The Applicants’ principals’ experience at their other facilities that faced operational challenges,
often with diverse resident populations, has equipped them to provide high quality care to all of
the residents at the Facilities. In particular, first, since the Applicants began managing the
Facilities, the annual survey outcomes for the Facilities have significantly improved with less
citations and decreased scope and severity for the citations that were issued. Second, the
Applicants have prioritized decreasing turnover in key positions and it is currently less than the
average turnover percentage in the industry. Third, recognizing the need for educated, trained,
and experienced staff in the Facilities, the Applicants were able to eliminate all agency staff in
all three facilities within three months of becoming managers. As part of this elimination of
agency staff, the Applicants have worked collaboratively with New England Healthcare
Employees Union District 1199/SEIU and Local 328 of the United Food & Commercial Workers
Union. Finally, the Applicants have infused capital into the Facilities and funded their losses
since becoming managers. In total, the Applicants’ principals have loaned over $636,000 to the
Facilities to date. The Applicants’ proven track record at the Facilities as managers demonstrates
their dedication to the Facilities’ quality of care and sustainability. The CMS five star rating
system is a three year process of improvement which the Applicants have begun with improved
survey results, consistent staffing and a focus on quality improvement.

The Applicants have a proven track record of providing high quality care to the residents at the

quality care. There is already a statutory process in place for Facilities Regulations in
conjunction with CMS, to address actions that may be taken as a result of deficiency findings

occur here.
