I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the applications Cedar Haven
Operations LLC d/b/a Lake Forest Health & Rehabilitation LLC, and Lake Forest
Health & Rehabilitation Propco LLC, [Lake Valley Operations Holdco LLC], for a
Change in Effective Control of Hebert Health Center [LTC00767] located in Smithfield;
and Valley View Health & Rehabilitation LLC, and Valley View Health &
Rehabilitation Propco LLC, [Lake Valley Operations Holdco LLC], for a Change in
Effective Control of Trinity Health and Rehab Center [LTC00766] located in Woonsocket,
Rhode Island be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

Cedar Haven Operations LLC d/b/a Lake Forest Health & Rehabilitation LLC (“Cedar Haven”)
and Valley View Health & Rehabilitation LLC (“Valley View”) (collectively the “Applicants”)
seek change in effective control approval to acquire Hebert Health Center (“Hebert”) and
Trinity Health & Rehabilitation Center (“Trinity”), respectively.

Hebert, a 133-bed nursing home located at 180 Log Road, Smithfield, Rhode Island, is licensed
and operated by ASLC Opco RI II, LLC (“Hebert Operator”).

Trinity, a 185-bed nursing home located at 4 St. Joseph St., Woonsocket, Rhode Island, is
licensed and operated by ASLC Opco RI I, LLC (“Trinity Operator”).

Hebert and Trinity are in receivership with Jeremy B. Savage, Esq., as the Court Appointed
Receiver (“Receiver”) pursuant to an Order of the Superior Court dated June 21, 2023 (the
“Receivership Action”) and on June 23, 2023, the Receiver entered into a Management

Managing Partner of Allaire. The Management Agreement was approved by the Superior
Court.


III.   INTRODUCTION

On April 9, 2025, pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode
Island titled "Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicants filed two CEC applications
of the subject-licensed facilities followed by resubmissions on April 22, 2025 and April 25,
2025 These requests are made because the statute requires that any proposed change in owner,
operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council and approved
by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

At the request of the applicant and based on commentary by the Court overseeing the
Receivership, RIDOH staff undertook an expedited review of the foregoing applications. Staff
reviewed the applications, found them to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicants and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the reviews would commence on April 30, 2025. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the applications submit their comments to the state agency by May 14,
2025, when practicable. A written comment, dated May 14, 2025, was submitted by Kathleen

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.
         As presented, Jay has extensive experience with nursing home operations in Ohio,
         Alabama, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Tennessee, many of which were
         underperforming facilities that were turned around and made profitable and
         experience at other facilities, many of which faced operational challenges and
         include diverse resident populations, has equipped him to provide quality care to all
         of the residents at Hebert and Trinity. As confirmed by the services provided under
         the Management Agreements since July, 2023, the Applicants have a proven track
         record of providing quality care at Hebert and Trinity will continue to do so as
         operators of the facilities.

         Linda Wheeler-Omiunu stated the following during the presentation “…So both
         facilities are long-term and short-term rehab facilities. We both have subacute units,

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicants, the Council recommends that the request for three CEC
applications, be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to ASLC RI II, LLC d/b/a Hebert Health Center and
the issuance of a new nursing facility license to Cedar Haven Operations LLC d/b/a Lake
Forest Health & Rehabilitation LLC

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to ASLC Opco RI I, LLC d/b/a Trinity Health &
Rehab Center and the issuance of a new nursing facility license to Valley View Health &
Rehabilitation LLC.

VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant applications shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the Applicants implement the projects as approved;

       2. that within two years the Applicants attain and maintain an accreditation from
          a nationally recognized accrediting agency;

       3. that the Applicants shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
          employees prior to employment;

       4. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
          resident information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

       5. that the Applicants will provide services to all residents without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay; and

       6. that the Applicants will work in good faith with residents to establish an
          alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
          Further that the Applicants will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge
          the resident while an application for an alternative payor source (such as
          Medicaid) is pending, and for Medicaid residents, maintain compliance with
          state regulation, 210-RICR-50-00-7, “Involuntary Discharge from a Long-
          Term Care Facility.”
