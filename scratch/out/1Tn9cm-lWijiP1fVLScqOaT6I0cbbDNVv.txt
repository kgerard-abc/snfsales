                                Background
•   Cedar Haven Operations LLC d/b/a Lake Forest Health & Rehabilitation LLC (“Cedar
    Haven”) and Valley View Health & Rehabilitation (“Valley View”) (collectively the
    “Applicants”) seek change in effective control approval to acquire Hebert Health Center
    (“Hebert”) and Trinity Health & Rehabilitation Center (“Trinity”), respectively.
•   Hebert, a 133 bed nursing home located at 180 Log Road, Smithfield, Rhode Island, is
    licensed and operated by ASLC Opco RI II, LLC (“ Hebert Operator”).
•   Trinity, a 185 bed nursing home located at 4 St. Joseph St., Woonsocket, Rhode Island,
    is licensed and operated by ASLC Opco RI I, LLC (“Trinity Operator”).
•   Hebert and Trinity are in receivership with Jeremy B. Savage, Esq., as the Court
    Appointed Receiver (“Receiver”) pursuant to an Order of the Superior Court dated June
    21, 2023. (the “Receivership Action”)
•   On June 23, 2023, the Receiver entered into a Management Agreement with Allaire
    Healthcare, Inc., effective July 18, 2023 with Jay Yarmush as the Managing Partner of
    Allaire. The Management Agreement was approved by the Superior Court.
•   The Applications seek approval for the acquisition of the operations and real estate of
    Hebert and Trinity, as approved by the Superior Court on November 4, 2024.

===== PAGE 4 =====
                     Background Continued
• Prior to the new management in July, 2023, the Facilities were facing numerous
  operational, quality and financial challenges resulting in receivership.
• Jay Yarmush, has led the management team at both facilities following the
  Superior Court’s approval of the Management Agreement effective July 18,
  2023.
• Mr. Yarmush has extensive experience with nursing home operations in Ohio,
  Alabama, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Tennessee, many of which were
  underperforming facilities that were turned around and made profitable.
• Under Mr. Yarmush’s leadership the census at both Facilities has increased, the
  financial situation at both Facilities has improved greatly, agency staff has been
  reduced and the provider teams are focused on ensuring quality care to all
  residents.
• On November 4, 2024, the Superior Court entered an order in the
  Receivership Action approving the Receiver’s petition to (i) sell all of the assets
  which comprise Hebert and Trinity, including the real estate and all
  improvements, pursuant to the terms of the Agreements for Purchase and Sale
  and (ii) transfer operations pursuant to the Asset Purchase Agreement.
