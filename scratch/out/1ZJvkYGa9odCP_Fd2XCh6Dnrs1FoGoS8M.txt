===== SLIDE 3 =====
The Transaction Travel
On March 2, 2020, South County SNF Operations LLC (the “Applicant”) filed a Change in Effective Control (“CEC”) Application for the following transaction:
Pursuant to the terms of the Operations Transfer Agreement entered into as of February 28, 2020, South County SNF Operations LLC (the “Applicant”) agreed to assume operations of South County Nursing and Rehabilitation (the “Facility”) from 740 Oak Hill Road Operations, LLC, a Genesis Healthcare, Inc. subsidiary.
Pursuant to the terms of the Asset Purchase Agreement effective February 28, 2020, 780 Oak Hill Road SNF Real Estate LLC (the “Buyer”) agreed to purchase the Facility real estate from Oak Hill Road Property, a subsidiary of Genesis HealthCare, Inc.
South County Nursing & Rehabilitation Center is a licensed skilled nursing facility located at 740 Oak Hill Road, North Kingstown.
The Facility is licensed to operate 120 beds and offers short-term rehabilitative care, long-term care, memory care, and hospice care. 
The current census is 73, increased from 50 at the time of the acquisition.
On June 30, 2020, the Applicant and RIDOH entered into a consent agreement allowing the transaction to close and operate the Facility while maintaining the Facility license in full force and effect pending CEC approval. 
The Transaction closed on July 6, 2020.

===== SLIDE 4 =====
The Transaction 
The purchase price for the acquisition of operations and real estate was $11,369,728 including $360,000 in closing costs. 
The Applicant funded the purchase price as follows:
$2,269,728(20%) with equity; and 
$9,100,000(80%) with a five-year term loan. 
The purchase price was reduced at closing from $12,000,000 due to the low census at the time of closing.
Contemporaneous with the closing, the Applicant entered into a lease agreement with Oak Hill Real Estate.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
The Applicant’s Commitment to the Facility and its Residents
The Applicant has operated the Facility for over a year and has demonstrated its commitment to offering high quality care to its residents. 
The Facility is currently a Medicare Three Star nursing home.  
Due to the Applicant’s efforts, the Facility increased from a two star (at the time of closing) to a three star home. 
The Facility has not been subject to any federal or state fines or civil monetary penalties under the Applicant’s ownership.
The Applicant has made extensive efforts to invest in the Facility’s residents, building, and employees.
Following closing, all of the Facility’s employees continued in their positions.  
The Applicant has made extensive efforts to recruit staff, including, but not limited to, signing bonuses.
The Applicant has made extensive capital improvements, including, but not limited to, replacing flooring and lighting, adding a new dishwasher, and, currently, installing a new roof.
The Applicant has also maintained all services and expanded the services offered, including adding a secured dementia unit with state approval.
The Applicant provides CNA training.

===== SLIDE 12 =====
The Applicant Will Continue to Provide Safe and Adequate Treatment
The Applicant has established and maintained an ongoing facility-wide Quality Assurance and Performance Improvement (“QAPI”) Program. 
The QAPI is overseen by a multi-disciplinary group, including the Executive Director and the members of the Governing Body. 
The QAPI Program details the facility’s activities and objectives designed to monitor and evaluate the quality of resident care, pursue methods to improve care quality, and resolve identified problems.
The Applicant offers and will continue to offer the current services and a high quality level of care to all residents.
The Applicant has maintained and expanded the services offered at the Facility, including adding a secured dementia unit with state approval.
