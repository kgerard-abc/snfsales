Dear Ben Berkowitz:

Attached is the final Report of Health Services Council on the Application of AdviniaCare
Pawtucket Pleasant Rehab Center, LLC [Pointe RI OpCo LLC], Change in Effective Control of
Oak Hill Center for Rehabilitation and Health Care, a 129-Bed Skilled Nursing Currently
Operated by Oak Hill Operations Associates LLC Located at 544 Pleasant Street, Pawtucket,
Rhode Island.

The Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) accepts the recommendation of the Health
Services Council and hereby approves the application and adopts the attached report in its entirety.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the existing
nursing facility issued to Oak Hill Center for Rehabilitation and Health Care and the issuance of
a new nursing facility license to AdviniaCare Pawtucket Pleasant Rehab Center, located in
Pawtucket.

Please be advised that section 1.6 D of the rules and regulations for the Licensing of Nursing
Facilities (216-RICR-40-10-1) requires RIDOH’s prior review and approval of any acquisition of
real property that is being operated as a nursing facility.

Section 1.6 D: In accordance with R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17-4(a), a person that conducts or
maintains a nursing facility in a building owned by another person is acting severally or
jointly with the building owner to do so. Accordingly, real property that is being operated
as a nursing facility may not be acquired or purchased by a person prior to the
Department's review and approval of such transaction. The provisions of this section shall
apply to any real property transactions that close on and after October 2, 2023.
