                     Introduction
• South County Eden Operations, LLC (the “Applicant”)
  seeks change in effective control approval to acquire the
  operations of South County Nursing & Rehabilitation
  Center (South County/Facility), a 120-bed skilled nursing
  facility offering short-term rehabilitation and long-term
  nursing care.
• The Facility is located at 740 Oak Hill Road, North
  Kingstown, Rhode Island.
• The Applicant will continue to provide quality short-term
  rehabilitation and long-term nursing care to the Facility’s
  residents.

===== PAGE 6 =====
                The Transaction
• Pursuant to the terms of an Asset Purchase and Sales
  Agreement (“APA”) entered into on January 6, 2022, and
  an Operations Transfer Agreement (“OTA”) entered into
  on December 20, 2021, 780 Oak Hill Road Eden Realty
  LLC (“PropCo Buyer”) and the Applicant agreed to
  acquire the Facility’s real estate and operations,
  respectively.
   o The PropCo Buyer closed on the Facility real estate on
     March 2, 2022. The current operator continues to operate
     the Facility. Upon CEC approval, the Applicant will
     assume operations.

===== PAGE 8 =====
   The Applicant Will Continue to Provide
       Safe and Adequate Treatment
• The Applicant will continue all current services including skilled nursing
  services, physical therapy, speech and language therapy, neurological care
  and rehabilitation, medication management and monitoring, pain
  management, orthopedic rehabilitation, and wound care.
• The Applicant will continue to ensure quality improvement (see Quality
  Assurance Policy at Tab 22).
        ▪ The Quality Assurance Policy is designed to identify the high
            risk, problem prone and high-volume areas to evaluate for
            improvement and identify, collect and use data relevant to the
            unique characteristics and needs of the resident.
• The Applicant will continue to provide resident-centered practices to
  ensure that each resident receives the highest level of care.
