Associates Limited [Michael Bigney and Related Trusts, 70%; Kelly Arnold and Related Trust, 10%;

The Rhode Island Department of Health accepts the recommendation of the Health Services
Council and hereby approves the applications and adopts the attached report in its entirety.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the existing nursing

facility license to 198 Waterman Avenue, LLC, located in Pawtucket.
