I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of South County
Eden Operations, LLC (“Applicant”) for the Change in Effective Control of South County
Nursing and Rehabilitation, [License # LTC00746], a licensed 120-bed nursing home located
at 740 Oak Hill Road, North Kingstown, Rhode Island, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

South County Eden Operations, LLC seeks Change in Effective Control to acquire the
operations of South County Nursing & Rehabilitation Center, a 120-bed skilled nursing
facility offering short-term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care, wholly owned by Mr.
Louis “Josh” Gellis.

As presented, the Applicant and 780 Oak Hill Road Eden Realty, LLC (“PropCo”) entered
into an Asset Purchase and Sales Agreement on January 6, 2022, and Operations Transfer
Agreement on December 20, 2021, to acquire South County Nursing & Rehabilitation
Center’s real estate and operations.

The applicant states in its application that it will continue the quality services currently
provided at the Facility and continue to make any needed upgrades.

As presented in the application, the total acquisition cost is $14,000,000 plus $360,000 in
closing costs to be funded with $2,720,000, 20% equity and $11,280,000, 80% debt.


III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed an application for a change in
effective control of the subject-licensed facility. This request is made because the statute
requires that any proposed change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility
be reviewed by the Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to
implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the review would commence on June 3, 2022. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by July 3,
2022, when practicable. No written comments were received.

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      A. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.

         According to the application and representations made by the Applicant at the
         meeting of July 12, 2022, Josh Gellis owns and operates skilled nursing and
         assisted living facilities in Rhode Island and out of state.

         Mr. Gellis has an ownership and operating interest in:

            o West View Nursing & Rehabilitation Center, a 120-bed skilled nursing
              facility in West Warwick, RI;
            o Cortland Place Rehab Center, LLC d/b/a Stillwater Assisted Living and
              Skilled Nursing Community, an 80-bed skilled nursing facility and an 87-
              bed assisted living facility in Greenville, RI;
            o Overlook Nursing and Rehabilitation, a 100-bed skilled nursing facility in
              Pascoag, RI;
            o Brentwood Health Center, a 96-bed skilled nursing facility in Warwick, RI;
            o Brentwood by the Bay, a 68-bed assisted living facility in Warwick, RI;
            o Sunny View Nursing Home, Inc, a 57-bed skilled nursing facility in
              Warwick, RI;
            o Delmar Nursing & Rehabilitation, a 109-bed skilled nursing facility in
              Delmar, Delaware; and

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that this request for a change in effective
control be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to South County Nursing & Rehabilitation Center,
Inc., and the issuance of a new nursing facility license to South County Eden Operations,
LLC.


VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total project cost not exceed $14,000,000, to be financed with a minimum
          equity contribution of $2,720,000 (i.e., 20%) and debt not to exceed $11,280,000 (i.e.,
           80%).

       2. that the Applicant implement the project as approved;

       3. that within two years the Applicant attain and maintain an accreditation from a
          nationally recognized accrediting agency;

       4. that the Applicant shall conduct national background checks on its employees;

       5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
          patient information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;
