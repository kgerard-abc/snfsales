EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Pezzelli Nursing Home, Inc (d.b.a. Golden Crest Nursing Centre) is a family owned and operated
business, The purpose of this proposal is the orderly transfer of the ownership of the shares of
Pezzelli Nursing Home Ine, currently held in two trusts in the estate of former sole owner Norma
M. Pezzelli (deceased), into the possession of or for the benefit of her four surviving children
and two grandchildren named as beneficiaries of the shares, The two trusts are identified as
follows: (i) The Norma M. Pezzelli Family Trust- 2012 (“2012 Trust”) and (ii) The Norma M.
Pezzelli Trust-2014 (2014 Trust”). Prior to the proposed change of effective control, the issued
and outstanding shares of Pezzelli Nursing Home, Inc, consisted of 100 shares of voting stock
and 9,900 shares of non-voting stock of which the 100 shares of voting stock and 4,900 shares of
non-voting stock was held in the name of the 2014 Trust and 5,000 shares of non-voting stock
was held in the name of the 2012 Trust. As indicated below, the voting shares will be held in
equal amounts by three (3) of Norma Pezzelli’s children. The transaction will not result in any
changes to the organization or daily management of the facility. Administrators and key
personnel will all remain in place. The continued efficient management of the facility and
provision of resident care at its highest possible level remain the organizationl]s first priority.

Description of Applicant

Pezzelli Nursing Home, Inc, d/b/a , Golden Crest Nursing Centre has operated continuously
since its opening in 1969. Owned and operated by the Pezzelli family, it provides long and short
stay skilled nursing care, dementia care, and hospice. The organization has an established record
of excellence; it has routinely earned CMS ratings of 4-stars or higher.

Type of Transaction
Distribution of ownership shares held in trust to its beneficiaries,
Summary of Transfer Documents

Transfer documents consist of assignments from the 2012 trust and the 2014 Trust to the
respective beneficiaries, the cancellation of the existing stock certificates and the issuance of new
stock certificates in the amounts and to the beneficiaries listed above, There are no other transfer
documents.

Summary of Organizational Structure

The corporation will be governed by an executive board consisting of the three voting
shareholders, The board will appoint by vote a Chief Executive Officer who will be empowered
to oversee the operation of the nursing facility, manage finances, hire key personnel, and other
duties.
