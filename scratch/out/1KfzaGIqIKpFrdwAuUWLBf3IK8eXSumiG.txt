I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of AdviniaCare
Scallop Shell, LLC (“Applicant” or “licensee”) and 55 Scallop Shell, LLC (“property”) for
a Change in Effective Control of 981 Kingstown Road Operating Company, LLC d/b/a
Elderwood of Scallop Shell at Wakefield (“Scallop Shell” or “Facility”), a licensed nursing
facility [LTC00352], located in Wakefield, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

The Applicant filed a Change in Effective Control (“CEC”) application to acquire both the
operations and the real estate of Elderwood of Scallop Shell at Wakefield (the “Facility”).

The Facility is an 80-bed skilled nursing facility located at 55 Scallop Shell Way in South
Kingstown.

Purchase price for the operations and real estate of the facility is $8,500,000.00.

Yosef Meystel, Ben Berkowitz, and David Berkowitz, principals of the Applicants,
combined, own and/or operate a total of 111 licensed facilities in eight states.

The Applicants affiliated entities own and operate 7 licensed facilities in Rhode Island:

           1.   AdviniaCare Pawtucket;
           2.   AdviniaCare Providence;
           3.   Heatherwood Rehabilitation & Health Care Center;
           4.   Oakland Grove Health Care Center;
           5.   Orchard View Manor Rehabilitation & Nursing Center;
           6.   Summit Commons Rehabilitation & Health Care Center; and
           7.   Waterview Villa Rehabilitation

III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed a CEC application of the subject-
licensed facility. This request is made because the statute requires that any proposed change in
owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council and
approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the reviews would commence on March 21, 2026. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by April 19,
2026, when practicable. A letter was submitted on April 17, 2026, by Katie Gradowski, Special
Assistant Attorney General, from the Rhode Island Office of the Attorney General.

IV.       FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.
          As presented by the Applicant and its legal counsel:

      •   “The Applicants’ principals have vast experience in owning and operating skilled
          nursing facilities and have owned two Rhode Island facilities since February 2025
          (managed since February 2023.) Since then, the Applicants’ principals acquired
          five additional Rhode Island nursing facilities from Athena Healthcare.
      •   Their track record in Rhode Island demonstrates a high quality of care and
          commitment to serving their residents and the community – the 7 Rhode Island
          facilities are all in good standing.
      •   The experience of the principals at their other 111 facilities in numerous states,
          many of which previously faced operational challenges and include diverse resident
          populations, has equipped them to provide high-quality care to all of the residents
          at Scallop Shell.”

          The Council considered the information provided at the meeting.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that the request for the CEC application,
be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing skilled nursing facility license (LTC00352) issued to 981 Kings Town Road
Operating Company LLC d/b/a Elderwood of Scallop Shell at Wakefield, and the
issuance of a new skilled nursing facility license to AdviniaCare Scallop Shell, LLC and
the approval and implementation of the acquisition of the real estate housing 55 Scallop Shell,
LLC.


VI.    CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total purchase price shall not exceed $8,500,000.00

       2. that the Applicant implement the projects as approved;

       3. that within two years the Applicant attain and maintain an accreditation from a
          nationally recognized accrediting agency;

       4. that the Applicant shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
          employees prior to employment;

       5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
          resident information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

6. that the Applicant will provide services to all residents without discrimination
   including payment source and ability to pay;

7. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an alternative
   payor source when another payor source is no longer available. Further that the
   Applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge the resident while an
   application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid) is pending, and for
   Medicaid residents, maintain compliance with state regulation, 210-RICR-50-00-
   7, “Involuntary Discharge from a Long Term Care Facility.”; and

8. that the Applicant provide, through administrative and operational policies
   and procedures individualized and resident-centered care, services, and
   accommodations.
