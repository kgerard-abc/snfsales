===== SLIDE 3 =====
Introduction
Silver Creek Manor SNF, LLC (the “Applicant”) seeks change in effective control approval to acquire the nursing home owned by KAR Little Box, LLC d/b/a Silver Creek Manor (“Silver Creek”).
Silver Creek is a family owned and operated duly licensed nursing facility providing services since 1970. 
The facility is licensed for 128 beds.
It offers short-term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care.
It also 
offers physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, discharge planning, hospice care and respite care. 
7 Creek Lane, Bristol, Rhode Island
Silver Creek prides itself on maintaining a home-like atmosphere, while providing modern up to date amenities and an array of activities for an enjoyable experience.  
Ranked Top 
P
erformer in 2017-2018 U.S. News & World Report for Best Nursing Homes.
Silver Award Winner of the Quality Award from the American Healthcare Association.
The Applicant will continue to provide such quality services to Silver Creek’s residents.

===== SLIDE 4 =====
Silver Creek COVID-19 
Response & Vaccine Status
4
Vaccine Status:
Approximately 95% of residents have been vaccinated with both doses.
Approximately 50% of staff has received at least one dose.  Silver Creek has provided the information to all staff regarding vaccination.
Silver Creek has been diligent in protecting 
its residents from COVID-19
.
Silver Creek performs weekly surveillance testing for residents and staff.
Until November 2, 2020, Silver Creek did not have any residents test positive for COVID-19.  
Currently, one fully vaccinated resident has tested positive and is asymptomatic.  No staff has tested positive since January 2021.
Silver Creek has maintained appropriate PPE in supply at all times.
The Applicant will continue this COVID-19 protocol.
