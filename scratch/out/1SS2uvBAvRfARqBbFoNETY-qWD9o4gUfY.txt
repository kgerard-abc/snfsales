Dear Michael Kotzen,

Attached is the final Report of the Health Services Council on the Application of Hope Nursing
Home Care, LLC [Community Based Care, LLC; Martis Partners III PV, LP] for a Change in
Effective Control of Home Care Services of Rhode Island, Inc., a Home Nursing Care Provider
Agency Located in Woonsocket, Rhode Island.

The Rhode Island Department of Health accepts the recommendation of the Health Services
Council and hereby approves the application and adopts the attached report in its entirety.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the existing home
nursing care provider license issued to Home Care Services of Rhode Island, Inc., and the issuance
of a new home nursing care provider license to Hope Nursing Home Care, LLC.

Additionally, please complete the attached license application at least two weeks in advance of the
closing in order to implement the changes in effective control in a timely manner. Please contact the
Center for Health Facilities Regulations at (401) 222-2566 with regards to any questions regarding
the license application.
