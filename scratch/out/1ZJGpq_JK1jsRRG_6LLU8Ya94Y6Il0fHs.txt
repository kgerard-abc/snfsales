===== PAGE 3 =====
                             Background
• Providence RI Property Holdings LLC (“Providence Property Holdings”)
  and Pawtucket RI Property Holdings LLC (“Pawtucket Property Holdings”)
  (collectively, the “Applicants”) seek Change in Effective Control (“CEC”)
  approval to purchase the real estate that houses AdviniaCare Providence
  Dodge Rehab Center, LLC (“AdviniaCare Providence”) and AdviniaCare
  Pawtucket Pleasant Rehab Center, LLC (“AdviniaCare Pawtucket”).
• On January 31, 2025 RIDOH approved the CEC Applications of
  AdviniaCare Providence, a 161 bed skilled nursing facility located at 135
  Dodge Street, Providence, RI, and AdviniaCare Pawtucket, a 129 bed skilled
  nursing facility located at 544 Pleasant St. Pawtucket, RI, for the transfer of
  operations at the facilities.
• Effective February 1, 2025, licensees were issued to AdviniaCare Providence
  and AdviniaCare Pawtucket to operate the facilities.
• Effective October 3, 2023 CEC approval is required for the sale of real estate
  housing skilled nursing facilities.

===== PAGE 4 =====
                   Background Continued
• Presently, the real estate that houses the two facilities is owned by Bannister
  Realty Associates LLC (“Bannister”) and Oak Hill Realty Associates LLC
  (“Oakhill), respectively, leased to Providence Property Holdings and Pawtucket
  Property Holdings, respectively, and subleased to the licensed operators,
  AdviniaCare Providence and AdviniaCare Pawtucket, respectively
• Pursuant to the (1) Purchase Option Agreement (Tab 14) by and among
  Bannister, Shady Acres Realty Associates LLC and Oak Hill (collectively, the
  “Seller” or “Lessor”) and Providence Property Holdings, West Kingston Rhode
  Island Properties Holdings LLC and Pawtucket Property Holdings, (collectively,
  the “Purchaser” or “Lessee”) entered into on December 19, 2022, (2) Master
  Lease Agreement (Tab 14) dated as of December 19, 2022, by and among the
  Lessor and Lessee, and (3) Amendment to Master Lease Agreement (Tab 14) by
  and between the Lessor and Lessee dated December 23, 2024, the Sellers,
  granted Providence Property Holdings and Pawtucket Property Holdings an
  option to purchase the real estate.
• The Applicants have exercised the purchase option and now seek CEC approval.
• The purchase price for both properties is $28,500,000 and closing costs are
  $2,500,000 for a total acquisition cost of $31,000,000.

===== PAGE 5 =====
                   AdviniaCare Providence

• AdviniaCare Providence is a 161 bed nursing home located at
  135 Dodge Street, Providence, Rhode Island.
• AdviniaCare Providence offers a full range of nursing care and
  social services. It also provides specialty programs including
  rehabilitation therapy programs (physical, occupational and
  speech language), health management (nutrition, diabetes care,
  wound care), recreational therapy and support services.
• AdviniaCare Providence provides resident-centered practices to
  ensure that each resident receives the highest level of care. (See
  Quality Assurance Policy at Tab 22 of the May 23, 2024 CEC
  applications for the transfer of operations).
   • The Quality Assurance Policy is designed to identify the
     high risk, problem prone and high-volume areas to evaluate
     for improvement and identify, collect and use data relevant
     to the unique characteristics and needs of the resident.

 Character, Commitment, Competence, and Standing in
                  the Community
• The principals of the Applicants and AdviniaCare Providence and
  AdviniaCare Pawtucket have vast experience in owning and operating
  skilled nursing facilities and have operated the two Rhode Island
  facilities since February, 2025.
• Pursuant to the Management Agreement approved by Rhode Island
  Department of Health (“RIDOH”) AdviniaCare Providence and
  AdviniaCare Pawtucket began managing the facilities in February
  2023.
• The principals of the Applicants and operators have experience
  owning and/or operating over 150 facilities in numerous states, many
  of which previously faced operational challenges and include diverse
  resident populations and currently provide high-quality care to all
  residents.
