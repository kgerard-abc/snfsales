$18,590,000 purchase price for Hope Nursing Home Care
Hope Nursing Home Care will continue to operate in Rhode Island as currently operated as part of the Community Based Care family of healthcare providers and will continue to operate as a certified home nursing and home care provider

Community Based Care currently operates out of 85 locations in North Carolina, Virginia and Florida

===== SLIDE 5 =====
Hope Nursing Home Care provides home nursing care services to Rhode Island patients who require assistance with everyday living, including health education and supportive services to those in need.
Hope Nursing Home Care services include personal care, homemaking and companion care.
The Applicant intends to continue with the mission established by Hope Nursing Home Care to provide patients home nursing care services to support patients and their families. 
The Applicant does not anticipate that any services will be added, terminated, expanded or reduced from those currently offered and intends to provide services within the scope of its license.
Using its patient centric local approach to care that is supported by the resources and experience of the larger Community Based Care organization, the Applicant plans to improve efficiency of care in a cost effective manner. 
The Applicant believes that it has the experience to effectively manage costs without adversely affecting care delivery and is well positioned to respond to rapidly evolving health care reform efforts.
