I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the applications of AdviniaCare
Newport, LLC; AdviniaCare Oakland Grove, LLC; AdviniaCare Orchard, LLC;
AdviniaCare Summit Commons, LLC; and AdviniaCare Waterview Villas, LLC
(“Applicants”) for Changes in Effective Control (“CEC”) of Heatherwood RI LLC;
Oakland Grove Associates, L.P; Athena Orchard View LLC; Summit RI SNF LLC;
and Waterview RI SNF, LLC respectively, and Bellevue Ave, LLC; Cumberland Hill,
LLC; Tripps Lane, LLC; 99 Hillside, LLC and South Broadway, LLC for Changes in
Effective Control of Heatherwood Landlord LLC; Oakland Grove Landlord, LLC;
Athena Orchard View Landlord LLC; Summit RI Landlord LLC; and Waterview RI
Landlord LLC, respectively be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

As presented in the applications, the Applicants, and its affiliated entities (AdviniaCare”) are
seeking CEC approval for the acquisition of five skilled nursing facilities across Rhode Island,
along with corresponding approvals for the transfer of the real estate associated with each
facility.

Heatherwood Rehabilitation & Health Care Center (Newport)- AdviniaCare Newport,
LLC seeks approval to acquire the 114-bed Heatherwood Rehabilitation & Health Care Center,
currently operated by Heatherwood RI LLC. Bellevue Ave, LLC seeks approval to acquire the
real estate, presently owned by Heatherwood Landlord LLC.

Oakland Grove Health Care Center (Woonsocket)- AdviniaCare Oakland Grove, LLC
seeks approval to acquire the 178-bed Oakland Grove Health Care Center, currently
operated by Oakland Grove Associates LP. Cumberland Hill, LLC seeks approval to
acquire the real estate, presently owned by Oakland Grove Landlord, LLC.


Orchard View Manor Rehabilitation & Nursing Center (East Providence)-
AdviniaCare Orchard, LLC seeks approval to acquire the 166-bed Orchard View Manor
Rehabilitation & Nursing Center, currently operated by Athena Orchard View LLC. Tripps
Lane, LLC seeks approval to acquire the real estate, presently owned by Orchard View
Landlord LLC. Summit Commons Rehabilitation & Health Care Center
(Providence)- AdviniaCare Summit Commons, LLC seeks approval to acquire the 165-
bed Summit Commons Rehabilitation & Health Care Center, currently operated by
Summit RI SNF, LLC. 99 Hillside, LLC seeks approval to acquire the real estate, presently
owned by Summit RI Landlord LLC.

Waterview Villa Rehabilitation (East Providence)- AdviniaCare Waterview Villas, LLC
seeks approval to acquire the 132-bed Waterview Villa Rehabilitation facility, currently
operated by Waterview RI SNF, LLC. South Broadway, LLC seeks approval to acquire the
real estate, presently owned by Waterview RI Landlord LLC.

III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicants filed five (5) CEC applications of the
subject-licensed facilities. These requests are made because the statute requires that any
proposed change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by
the Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the applications, found them to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicants
and the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-
mail that the reviews would commence on October 18, 2025. The notice also advised that all
persons wishing to comment on the applications submit their comments to the state agency by
November 16, 2025, when practicable. A letter, dated November 14, 2025, was submitted by
Katie Gradowski, Special Assistant Attorney General, from the Rhode Island Office of the
Attorney General, and a letter, dated November 18, 2025, was submitted by Kathleen Gerard,
Director for Advocates for Better Care in Rhode Island.

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.
         As presented by the Applicants and its legal counsel:

             •   “The Applicants’ principals have vast experience in owning and
                 operating skilled nursing facilities and have owned two Rhode Island
                 facilities since February 2025 and managed them since February 2023.

             •   Their track record in Rhode Island demonstrates a high quality of care and
                 commitment to serving their residents and the community.

             •   The experience of the principals at their other 111 facilities in numerous
                 states, many of which previously faced operational challenges and include
                 diverse resident populations, has equipped them to provide high-quality
                 care to all of the residents at the five Athena affiliated facilities.”

        The Council considered the information provided at the meeting and the written
        public comments.

         Finding: The Council finds that the Applicants satisfy this criterion at the
         time, place, and circumstances as proposed.

      2. The extent to which the facility will provide, without material effect on its
         viability, safe and adequate treatment for those individuals receiving the
         facility’s services.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicants, the Council recommends that the request for five (5) CEC
applications, be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing skilled nursing facility license issued to Heatherwood Rehabilitation & Health
Care Center and the issuance of a skilled new nursing facility license to AdviniaCare
Newport, LLC.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing skilled nursing facility license issued to Oakland Grove Health Care Center and
the issuance of a new skilled nursing facility license to AdviniaCare Oakland Grove, LLC

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing skilled nursing facility license issued to Orchard View Manor Rehabilitation &
Nursing Center and the issuance of a new skilled nursing facility license to AdviniaCare
Orchard, LLC.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing skilled nursing facility license issued to Orchard View Manor Rehabilitation &
Nursing Center Summit Commons Rehabilitation & Health Care Center and the
issuance of a new skilled nursing facility license to AdviniaCare Summit Commons,
LLC.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing skilled nursing facility license issued to Waterview Villa Rehabilitation and the
issuance of a new skilled nursing facility license to AdviniaCare Waterview Villas, LLC.

The recommendations of approval also include the acquisition of the five real estate assets.


VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant applications shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total purchase price shall not exceed $75,000,000 and the debt will be
          limited to 50,000,000;
                                             11

===== PAGE 14 =====
2. that the Applicants implement the projects as approved;

3. that within two years the Applicants attain and maintain an accreditation from
   a nationally recognized accrediting agency;

4. that the Applicants shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
   employees prior to employment;

5. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
   resident information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

6. that the Applicants will provide services to all residents without discrimination
   including payment source and ability to pay; and

7. that the Applicants will work in good faith with residents to establish an
   alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
   Further that the Applicants will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge
   the resident while an application for an alternative payor source (such as
   Medicaid) is pending, and for Medicaid residents, maintain compliance with
   state regulation, 210-RICR-50-00-7, “Involuntary Discharge from a Long-
   Term Care Facility.”
