===== SLIDE 3 =====
Introduction
	
Elmhurst Operator LLC d/b/a Elmhurst Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center (“Elmhurst” or the “Applicant”) is seeking
 change in effective control approval for an internal reorganization as the result of the passing of Yonah Kohn and for estate planning purposes which impacted all affiliated licensed nursing facilities in seven states (RI, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Maryland, Virginia and Florida).
Pursuant to a Consent Agreement with RIDOH executed on December 29, 2021, the transaction consummating this reorganization closed on December 31, 2021.
The reorganization does not affect the day-to-day operations of the Applicant which continues to provide quality services to its residents. 
3
===== SLIDE 4 =====
Elmhurst
Elmhurst is a 206-bed skilled nursing facility located at 50 Maude Street, Providence, Rhode Island.
The Applicant has operated the facility since 2016 and offers a full complement of services including Physical, Occupational, and Speech Therapies, Alzheimer’s/Dementia Care, 24/7 Skilled Nursing, Subacute Rehabilitation, and Cardiopulmonary Care.
Elmhurst is the only community in Rhode Island that is part of Eden Alternative ® and received its annual recertification in August, 2021.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
The Applicant
The Applicant and its affiliates are third-generation operators of skilled nursing facilities.
The Applicant is part of a long-standing family of 55 nursing and assisted living facilities operating in 7 states: Rhode Island, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Florida and Virginia. 
In Rhode Island, the Applicants’ affiliates own and operate:
Elmhurst Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center, a 206 bed in Providence, Rhode Island; 
Lincolnwood Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center, a 220 bed SNF in North Providence, Rhode Island; and
Bayview Rehabilitation and Healthcare Center at Scalabrini, a 120 bed SNF in North Kingstown, Rhode Island
Harmony Hospice Care, a licensed Hospice provider. 
The Applicant’s affiliates recently received Change in Effective Control approval for the acquisition of four Rhode Island facilities currently owned and operated by Health Concepts Ltd. affiliated entities.
The Applicant is fully experienced with the Rhode Island market and committed to providing high quality, long-term care services to its resident.
