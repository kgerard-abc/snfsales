I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the AdviniaCare Providence
Dodge Rehab Center, LLC (“AdviniaCare” or “Applicant”) [Pointe RI OpCo LLC],
Change in Effective Control (“CEC”) of Bannister Center for Rehabilitation & Health
Care, a 161-bed skilled nursing facility currently operated by Bannister Operations
Associates LLC located at 135 Dodge Street, Providence, Rhode Island, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

The Applicant is seeking approval to acquire the operations of:

AdviniaCare Providence Dodge Rehab Center, LLC [Pointe RI OpCo LLC], for a Change in
Effective Control of Bannister Center for Rehabilitation & Health Care, a 161-bed skilled nursing
facility currently operated by Bannister Operations Associates LLC located at 135 Dodge Street,
Providence, Rhode Island.

The Applicant’s sole member is Pointe RI OpCo LLC. The principals of Pointe RI OpCo LLC
are Ben Berkowitz, David Berkowitz, and Yosef Meystel.

The facility is presently subject to a Management Agreement with Pointe Group Care, LLC. At
the meeting of the Council on December 10, 2024, the Applicant stated that there would be no
management company with respect to the Rhode Island facilities.


III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 23-17-14.3 and 23-17-14.4, titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed its CEC application of the subject-
licensed facility. This request is being made because the statute requires that any proposed
change in owner, operator, or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the
Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant and
the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and e-mail
that the review would commence on September 10, 2024. The notice also advised that all persons
wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by October
10, 2024, when practicable.

The CEC Application is included in the following link:

AdviniaCare Providence Dodge Rehab, LLC (Bannister Center for Rehabilitation & Health
Care)'s CEC Application

The Council reviewed the proposal on December 10, 2024, with the Applicant and its legal
counsel in attendance at the meeting.

IV.       FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators, or directors of the health care facility.
As stated in the application, Ben Berkowitz, David Berkowitz, and Yosef Meystel own and/or
operate a total of eighty-two (82) licensed facilities in seven (7) states. The application contained
the following representations:

      •   “Ben Berkowitz is an owner operator of 15 nursing facilities and senior housing facilities
          in Florida and Massachusetts, as well as an investor in facilities in other states (a
          complete list is included at Appendix B and G). Specifically, he is the Chief Executive
          Officer of Pointe Group Care, LLC (“PGC”), which is headquartered in Massachusetts.
          PCG is the corporate manager of Pointe RI OpCo LLC. PGC employs staff focusing on
          operations, accounting, clinical, business development, technology, and accounts
          receivable and payable.

      •   David Berkowitz and Yosef Meystel are the founders of Aperion Care, Inc. (“Aperion
          Care”). Aperion Care is an administrative and management company firm that partners
          with geriatric healthcare facilities. It specifically provides management services to skilled
          nursing facilities and assisted living communities throughout Illinois and Indiana. Mr.
          Meystel began his career in healthcare as an Assistant Administrator at Brightview Care
          Center in Chicago, Illinois. Following Brightview, Mr. Meystel was the Administrator
          for EMI/EKS Management, where he became the Administrator for Emerald Park Health
          Care Center. In 2006, Mr. Meystel founded YAM Management, LLC and in 2014 formed
          Aperion Care, Inc., for which he has served as CEO, a director, and a shareholder.

      •   David Berkowitz serves as the President of Aperion Care, Inc., as well as a director and
          a shareholder. He began his career in healthcare in 2002 as an administrator in training
          at Sebos Nursing & Rehab. David Berkowitz then served as an administrator at a number
          of other skilled nursing facilities. He has ownership interest in the real estate and/or
          operating entities of many skilled nursing facilities located throughout Illinois, Indiana,
          Missouri, Massachusetts, Florida, and Kansas.”

          The Council considered the information contained in the record, the
          representations made by the Applicant and the public comments at its meeting of
          December 10, 2024, and, on balance, found that the Applicant satisfied this
          criterion.
          Finding: The Council finds that the Applicant satisfies this criterion at the
          time, place, and circumstances as proposed.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that the request for the CEC application,
be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility issued to Bannister Center for Rehabilitation & Health Care and
the issuance of a new nursing facility license to AdviniaCare Providence Dodge Rehab
Center, LLC, located in Providence.


VI. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the Applicant implement the application as approved;

       2. that within two years the Applicant attain and maintain accreditation from a
          nationally recognized accrediting agency;

       3. that the Applicant shall conduct national criminal background checks on their
          employees prior to employment;

       4. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization, and demographic
          resident information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

       5. that the Applicant will provide services to all residents without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay; and

       6. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
          alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
          Further that the Applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge the
          patient while an application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid)
          is pending; and

       7. that the Applicant provide, through administrative and operational policies and
          procedures individualized and resident-centered care, services, and
          accommodations.
