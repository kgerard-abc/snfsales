I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of Con V Care
Inc. d/b/a Woonsocket Health & Rehabilitation Centre (“Applicant”) for the Change in
Effective Control of Woonsocket Health & Rehabilitation Centre located at 262 Poplar
Street, Woonsocket, Rhode Island, be approved.

II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

As presented in the application, Woonsocket Health & Rehabilitation Centre (“Facility”) is
a family owned and operated business, since 1975. The facility provides skilled nursing
services for short and long term, dementia care, and hospice, located at 262 Poplar Street,
Woonsocket, Rhode Island.

As presented in the application, the purpose of this proposal is to transfer the ownership of
the shares of Con V Care Inc., currently held in trust in the estate of the former sole owner
Norma M. Pezzelli (deceased), into the possession of her children named as recipients of the
shares.

As presented in the application, the Applicant will continue the efficient management of the
facility and provision of resident care and there will be no change of administrators or key
staff members.

The Applicant states there is no additional debt or equity commitments required for the
Change in Effective Control transaction.


III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed an application for a Change in
Effective Control of the subject-licensed facility. This request is made because the statute
requires that any proposed change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility
be reviewed by the Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to
implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant
and the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and
e-mail that the review would commence on May 7, 2021. The notice also advised that all
persons wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency by
June 5, 2021, when practicable. There were no public comments.

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      A. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.

         The Facility is a family owned and operated business, since 1975.

         As presented in the application, the Applicant will continue the efficient
         management of the Facility and provision of resident.

         The Council was not apprised of any information of concern regarding the
         Applicant and the application, itself.

         Finding: The Council finds that the Applicant satisfies this criterion at the
         time, place and circumstances as proposed.

      B. The extent to which the facility will provide, without material effect on its
         viability, safe and adequate treatment for those individuals receiving the
         facility’s services.

         As noted above, there is no additional debt or equity commitments required for the
         Change in Effective Control transaction.

         The following information is projected by the Applicant for the facility in FY 2022:

                             Total Revenues $12,747,379.00
                             Total Expenses $12,547,864.00
                             Profit         $ 199,515.00

         Finding: The Council finds that the Applicant satisfies this criterion at the time,
         place and circumstances as proposed.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that this request for a Change in Effective
Control be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to Con V Care Inc. d/b/a Woonsocket Health &
Rehabilitation Centre and the issuance of a new nursing facility license to Con V Care Inc.
d/b/a Woonsocket Health & Rehabilitation Centre.

VI.      CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

         1. that there be no debt associated with the implementation of this application;

2. that the Applicant shall comply with the rules and regulations, Licensing of
   Nursing Facilities (216-RICR-40-10-1);

3. that the Applicant implement the project as approved;

4. that the Applicant maintains an accreditation from a nationally recognized
   accrediting agency;

5. that the Applicant shall conduct national background checks on its employees;

6. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
   patient information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

7. that the Applicant will provide services to all patients without discrimination
   including payment source and ability to pay;

8. that the Applicant will provide, through administrative and operational policies
   and procedures individualized and resident-centered care, services and
   accommodations; and

9. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
   alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
   Further that the Applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge the
   patient while an application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid)
   is pending.
