I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the application of Greentree
Nursing and Rehab, LLC (“Applicant”) for the Change in Effective Control of Saint
Elizabeth Manor, a skilled nursing and rehabilitation facility located at 1 Dawn Hill Road,
Bristol, Rhode Island, be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

The applicant is a newly-formed limited liability company that is wholly owned by Rhode
Island Healthcare Holdco 2 LLC, which is owned by Aharon Stern (29.5%), Simon Stern
(29.5%), GRI SNF Investors LLC (17.5%), MRI SNF Investors LLC (17.5%) and individual
investors comprising the remaining 6.5% with none owing in excess of 2% each. The
members of GRI SNF Investors LLC are the Dasay 2019 Trust (96%) and Dov Green (4%).
The members of MRI SNF Investors LLC are the GAMTA 2020 Trust (96%) and Boruch
Mermelstein (4%).

Affiliated entities of the Applicant are also in the process of obtaining approval to acquire
two other nursing facilities in Rhode Island: Silver Creek Manor and Berkshire Place.

Upon approval of the instant application, acquisition of the subject nursing facility and
through various activities and transactions with affiliated entities as described in the
application, the Applicant will seek to be the licensee of the subject nursing facility, enter
into a lease agreement with an affiliated entity, 1 Dawn Hill Property, LLC and enter into a
management agreement with an affiliated entity, Green Tree Healthcare 2, LLC. Following
the closing, the facility will be named The Dawn Hill Home for Rehab and Healthcare.

As presented in the application, the total project cost is $15,100,000 to be financed with
$3,050,200 (21%) equity and $12,049,800 (79%) debt. As an element in the transaction, the
Applicant shall, make no less than five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000.00) in capital
expenditures in connection with the renovation of the Facility.


III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island entitled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicant filed an application for a change in
effective control of the subject-licensed facility. This request is made because the statute
requires that any proposed change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility
be reviewed by the Council and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to
implementation.

Staff reviewed the application, found it to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicant
and the general public by a notice on the Rhode Island Department of Health’s website and
e-mail that the review would commence on February 9, 2021. The notice also advised that
all persons wishing to comment on the application submit their comments to the state agency
by March 10, 2021, when practicable. St. Elizabeth Manor is presently a not-for-profit. The

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      A. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.

         According to the application and representations made by the Applicant at the
         meeting of March 16, 2021, four of the individuals holding membership interest
         in the Applicant and its affiliated entities, Aaron Stern, Simon Stern, Dov Green
         and Boruch Mermelstein have longstanding and extensive experience in the
         ownership and management of nursing facilities in other states.

         The Council was not apprised of any concerns related to the applicant and its
         affiliates.

         Finding: The Council finds that the applicant satisfies this criterion at the
         time, place, and circumstances as proposed.

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicant, the Council recommends that this request for a change in effective
control be approved.

Approval and implementation of this application will result in the termination of the
existing nursing facility license issued to Saint Elizabeth Manor, East Bay, and the issuance
of a new nursing facility license to Greentree Nursing and Rehab, LLC.

VI.    CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

The Council recommends that approval of the instant application shall be subject to the
following conditions:

       1. that the total project cost not exceed $15,100,000 and that debt not exceed
          $12,049,800;

       2. that the operating costs for the first full operating year not exceed $14,493,524.10;

       3. that the Applicant implement the project as approved, including the required
          $500,000.00 in capital improvements;

       4. that the Applicant attain and maintain an accreditation from a nationally
          recognized accrediting agency;

       5. that the Applicant shall conduct national background checks on its employees;

       6. that data, including but not limited to, finances, utilization and demographic
          patient information, be furnished to the state agency, upon request;

       7. that the Applicant will provide services to all patients without discrimination
          including payment source and ability to pay; and

       8. that the Applicant will work in good faith with residents to establish an
          alternative payor source when another payor source is no longer available.
          Further that the applicant will not withhold or discontinue care or discharge the
          patient while an application for an alternative payor source (such as Medicaid)
          is pending.
