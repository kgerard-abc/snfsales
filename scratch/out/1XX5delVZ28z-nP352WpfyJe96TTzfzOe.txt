I. SYNOPSIS

The Health Services Council (“Council”) recommends that the applications of Providence
RI Property Holdings LLC (“Providence Property Holdings”) and Pawtucket RI
Property Holdings LLC (“Pawtucket Property Holdings”) (together the “Applicants”)
for Change in Effective Control (“CEC”) of the real estate of AdviniaCare Providence
Dodge Rehab Center, LLC (“AdviniaCare Providence”) and AdviniaCare Pawtucket
Pleasant Rehab Center, LLC (“AdviniaCare Pawtucket”) be approved.


II. PROPOSAL DESCRIPTION

As presented in the applications Providence RI Property Holdings LLC, owned by
Rhode Island Propco JV, LLC, owned by Pointe Group Care, LLC & CCG YUTA JE
Falls, LLC and Pawtucket RI Property Holdings LLC, owned by Rhode Island
Propco JV, LLC, owned by Pointe Group Care, LLC & CCG YUTA JE Falls, LLC
are seeking CEC approval to purchase the real estate housing AdviniaCare Providence
and AdviniaCare Pawtucket. The agreed purchase price for both properties is
$28,500,000, with an additional $2,500,000 in closing costs, resulting in a total acquisition
cost of $31,000,000.

On January 31, 2025, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) approved the CEC
applications for AdviniaCare Providence, a 161-bed skilled nursing facility located at 135
Dodge Street, Providence, RI, and AdviniaCare Pawtucket, a 129-bed skilled nursing
facility located at 544 Pleasant Street, Pawtucket, RI, authorizing the transfer of operations
for both facilities.

Effective February 1, 2025, licenses were issued to AdviniaCare Providence and
AdviniaCare Pawtucket to operate the facilities.

III.   INTRODUCTION

Pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 23-17 of the General Laws of Rhode Island titled
"Licensing of Health Care Facilities," the Applicants filed two CEC applications of the subject-
licensed facilities. These requests are made because the statute requires that any proposed
change in owner, operator or lessee of a licensed health care facility be reviewed by the Council
and approved by the state-licensing agency prior to implementation.

Staff reviewed the applications, found them to be acceptable in form, and notified the Applicants
and the general public by a notice on RIDOH’s website and e-mail that the reviews would
commence on September 20, 2025. The notice also advised that all persons wishing to comment
on the applications submit their comments to the state agency by October 19, 2025, when
practicable. A letter, dated October 20, 2025, was submitted by Julia Harvey, Special Assistant

IV.      FINDINGS

Section 23-17-14.3 of the licensing statute requires the Council to consider specific
review criteria in formulating a recommendation for a change in effective control. The
Council's comments and findings on each of the criteria follow:

      1. The character, competence, commitment, and standing in the community of
         the proposed owners, operators or directors of the health care facility.
         As presented by the Applicants and their legal counsel:

             •   “The principals of the Applicants and AdviniaCare Providence and
                 AdviniaCare Pawtucket have vast experience in owning and operating
                 skilled nursing facilities and have operated the two Rhode Island facilities
                 since February, 2025.

             •   Pursuant to the Management Agreement approved by Rhode Island
                 Department of Health (“RIDOH”) AdviniaCare Providence and
                 AdviniaCare Pawtucket began managing the facilities in February 2023.

             •   The principals of the Applicants and operators have experience owning
                 and/or operating over 150 facilities in numerous states, many of which
                 previously faced operational challenges and include diverse resident
                 populations and currently provide high-quality care to all residents.”

         Finding: The Council finds that the Applicants satisfy this criterion at the
         time, place, and circumstances as proposed.

      2. The extent to which the facility will provide, without material effect on its
         viability, safe and adequate treatment for those individuals receiving the
         facility’s services.

         As presented in the applications, the total acquisition cost comprises a purchase price
         of $28,500,000 for the properties housing AdviniaCare Providence and AdviniaCare

V. RECOMMENDATION

After considering each of the review criteria as required by statute and the representations
made by the Applicants, the Council recommends that the request for the CEC applications,
be approved.

Approval and implementation of these applications will result in the acquisition of the real
estate housing AdviniaCare Providence Dodge Rehab Center, LLC and AdviniaCare
Pawtucket Pleasant Rehab Center, LLC by Providence RI Property Holdings LLC
and Pawtucket RI Property Holdings LLC. Both entities are owned by Rhode Island
Propco JV, LLC, which are jointly owned by Pointe Group Care, LLC and CCG YUTA
JE Falls, LLC.
