===== SLIDE 3 =====
Introduction
Berkshire Place SNF, LLC (the “Applicant”) seeks change in effective control approval to acquire the skilled nursing facility duly licensed and operated by Berkshire Place Associates, LP d/b/a Berkshire Place Nursing & Rehabilitation (“Berkshire Place”). 
Berkshire Place 
has been family owned and operated for more than 25 years and is dedicated to providing exceptional and compassionate care. 
The facility is licensed for 220 beds. 
It offers short-term rehabilitation and long-term nursing care.
It also 
offers 
Alzheimer’s/Dementia Care, Intravenous Therapy, Behavioral Health Management, Diabetic Management, Palliative Care, and Hospice Care.  
It is located at 455 Douglas Ave, Providence.
The Applicant will continue to provide such quality services to Berkshire Place’s residents.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
The Transaction
5
Pursuant to the terms of the Asset Purchase and Sales Agreement (“APA”) entered into as of October 19, 2020, between 
Green Tree Healthcare Management, LLC (“Green Tree
”) and Berkshire Place, Green Tree will acquire Berkshire Place’s operations and purchase Berkshire Place’s real estate.
Immediately at closing:
Green Tree will assign the operations of the Facility to the Applicant.
The Applicant and 
Green Tree will enter into a Lease in which the 
Applicant will 
lease the real estate from Green Tree. 
The Applicant will enter into 
a management agreement with a Green Tree affiliate.
