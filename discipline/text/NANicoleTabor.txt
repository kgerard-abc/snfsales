Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health ("RIDOH") has

| ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode island
under Nursing Assistant License Number NA45951.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Steere House Nursing &
Rehabilitation Center, 100 Borden Street, Providence, Rl 02903.

3. On or about March 3, 2023, the Respondent was witnessed being verbally abusive to a
patient.

conduct pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely fo resolve the
Complaint.
