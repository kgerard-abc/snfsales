CONSENT ORDER

Napoleao Vieira (Respondent) was licensed as a nursing assistant in Rhode Island. The
Director of the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) summarily suspended his
license on July 16, 2020. Respondent voluntarily surrendered his nursing assistant license on
October 15, 2020, Respondent has requested reinstatement of his nursing assistant license.
After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to reinstatement of Respondent’s
nursing assistant license, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

Respondent was a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island under
Nursing Assistant License Number NA 42743.

Respondents nursing assistant license was summarily suspended on July 16, 2020 because
he engaged in a sexual relationship with a patient in his care.

The summary suspension of Respondent’s nursing assistant license was upheld on September
16, 2020 following an administrative hearing.

Respondent voluntarily surrendered his nursing assistant license on October 15, 2020, Under
the terms of the voluntary surrender Consent. Order, Respondent agreed that if, in the future,
he sought to re-apply for a nursing assistant license, he would be required to provide proof of
a psychiatric evaluation to determine his understanding of boundary issues and to show
whether the psychiatrist made any recommendations and whether Respondent followed any
such recommendations.
