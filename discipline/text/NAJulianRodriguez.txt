NOTICE OF SUMMARY SUSPENSION

This Order is issued pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is licensed by the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) as a Nursing
Assistant under license number NA59813.

2. On or about October 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at an assisted
living facility.

3. Onor about November 21, 2025, a resident at the facility gave a detailed statement to RIDOH
in which he stated that the Respondent, who had been assigned to assist him with showering,
had utilized a massage tool on the patient’s genitals, had placed his hands on the patient’s
penis, and had placed the patient’s penis in his mouth-during the month of October, 2025.

4. The conduct described in paragraph three constitutes unprofessional conduct in violation of
R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and 216-RICR-40-05-22.

5. After considering the above facts, the Director of the Department of Health finds that public
health, safety, or welfare imperatively requires emergency action.
Based on the foregoing and pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14, Respondent’s nursing
assistant license are SUSPENDED until further order of the Department.

A written request for a hearing on the summary suspension may be sent to the Director within
ten days after service of this Notice. Questions may be directed to at 401-222-4390.
