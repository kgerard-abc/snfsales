CENTER FOR PROFESSIONAL BOARDS AND LICENSING

IN THE MATTER OF: SARAH KING

LICENSE NUMBER: NA25475
COMPLAINT NUMBER : C25 - 0112

CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated a complaint
alleging unprofessional conduct by SARAH KING (“Respondent”). After review and
consideration by RIDOH the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a nursing assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA25475.

2. On or about January 26, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at a
facility.

3. On or about January 26, 2025, Respondent spoke to a patient in a disrespectful manner.

4, The conduct described in paragraph three constitutes unprofessional conduct pursuant to
R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

5. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, effective the day this Consent
Order is last executed.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. Respondent understands that the purpose of this Consent Order is to resolve the
complaint against her license.
8. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent’s behalf;

d. The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e. The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained
herein;

f. The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

g. Any objection that this Consent Order will (i) be reported to the National
Practitioner Data Bank, (ii) become a permanent part of Respondent’s record
maintained by RIDOH and a part of the public record of this proceeding, or (iii)
be posted on RIDOH’s public website.
