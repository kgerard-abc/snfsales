CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RJ. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations

promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the
Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
BEATRIZ DALOMBA, NA45225 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to
disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration
by RIDOH and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall
constitute

Go

FINDINGS OF FACT

Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA45225,

At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Nursing Placement, Inc.

On or about February 27, 2018, and on at least two other occasions, Respondent admits
that she flicked water in a patient’s face when she felt that the patient was crying
excessively. Respondent also admits that she used her cell phone to take a picture of the

patient and showed the picture to one of her own family members.

That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this
complaint:

Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand on her Nursing
Assistant license, NA45225.

If Respondent is currently employed, she must notify the Board, in writing, of the
identity of her current employer. Respondent is required to participate in in-
service trainings on patients’ rights and HIPPA through her identified employer
within thirty (30) days of the ratification of this Consent Order and provide the
Board with proof of such completion.

If Respondent is not currently employed, she must notify the Board, in writing,
within five (5) days of the date that she secures employment, and must also
provide the identity of her employer. Respondent is required to participate in in-
service trainings on patients’ rights and HIPPA through her identified employer
within thirty (30) days of her original employment date, and provide the Board
with proof of such completion.

This Consent Order shall become a public record.
