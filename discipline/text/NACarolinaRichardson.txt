Pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
(“RIDOH") has investigated a complaint charging CAROLINA RICHARDSON, nursing
assistant license number NA 54215 (“Respondent”), with violations of R.I. Gen Laws
§ 23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rnode
Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 54215.

2. On December 29, 2024, and January 4, 5, 11, 12, 2025, Respondent was employed
as a nursing assistant by a nursing assistant staffing agency and provided care to
patients at their homes. Respondent was scheduled to provide care to Patient A
from 6:30 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. and to patient B from 2:15 p.m. to 4:15 p.m.

Travel time between the two homes as depicted by Google Maps is between 14
and 17 minutes.

3. Electronic visits verification logs produced to RIDOH by the staffing agency
demonstrate that on December 29, 2024, Respondent clocked in at the home of
Patient A at 6:27 a.m. and clocked out at 2: 01 p.m., and then clocked in at the
home B at 2:03 p.m.

4. Electronic visits verification logs produced to RIDOH by the staffing agency
demonstrate that on January 4, 2025, Respondent clocked in at the home of
Patient A at 6:30 a.m. and clocked out at 2: 07 p.m., and then clocked in at the
home B at 2:08 p.m.
