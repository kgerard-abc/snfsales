RHODE ISLAND DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH
DIVISION OF HEALTHCARE QUALITY AND SAFETY
CENTER FOR PROFESSIONAL BOARDS AND LICENSING

IN THE MATTER OF: LAURA GOLDSTEIN
LICENSEE NO.: NA 37011
COMPLAINT ID #: C24-0877

CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated
Complaint No. C24-0877 (the “Complaint”) charging LAURA GOLDSTEIN, nursing assistant license
number NA 37011(“Respondent”) with violations of R.I|. Gen Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional conduct
of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island under
Nursing Assistant License Number NA 37011.

2. That on or about August 8, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant by a staffing
agency and was assigned to work at a nursing facility.

3. That on or about August 8, 2024, Respondent arrived at the facility for a shift scheduled to begin
at 7:00 a.m. and to end at 3 :00 p.m. Respondent arrived at the facility at 7:00 a.m. but clocked
out, terminated her position at the facility and left the building at approximately 8:00 a.m., thus
abandoning residents who were in need of her care.

4. Respondent states that she left the facility after telephoning her recruiter and resigning from
employment with the staffing agency. Respondent states that her recruiter accepted her
resignation and told her that the agency would try to find a replacement for her, but never
advised her that an abrupt resignation constituted abandonment. Respondent states that had
she been so advised, she would have remained at the facility to take care of her
patients. Respondent states that she is deeply remorseful and assures that this will never
happen again.
