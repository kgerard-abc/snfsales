CONSENT ORDER FOR ACTIVE PROBATION

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”), has investigated these
complaints regarding allegations of unprofessional conduct in violation of R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-
17.9-1 et seq. against ROSALIE ROGERS (“Respondent”), and after review and consideration
by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the
following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 36813.

2. That during 2024, Respondent was employed by a nursing facility.

3. That during 2024, Respondent was involved in a romantic relationship with a patient in

her care at the facility. , a

4. Respondent admits that her conduct as described in paragraph three above was in
violation of R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and 216-RICR-40-05-22.6(c). Respondent
was in a romantic relationship with the patient prior to the patient’s admission to the
facility and was told by the facility’s administrator that she and the patient could continue
the relationship off the premises of the facility.

5. That during 2024, Respondent was involved in a financial relationship with a different
patient in her care at the facility.

6. Respondent admits that her conduct as described in paragraph five above was in violation
of R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and 216-RICR-40-05-22.6(c). Respondent states
that she repaid the money to the patient in full.

21. Respondent acknowledges that she understands that it is a violation of Rhode Island’s
rules and regulations regarding Nursing Assistants to have a physical or financial
relationship with a patient and that the patient’s consent to or agreement to such a

22. Respondent will appear quarterly at a closed session of the Nursing Assistant Advisory
Board to discuss the above acknowledgment, what she learned during the required
continuing education courses described above and how she has applied this information
about ethics and boundaries in her daily practice as a nursing assistant. These quarterly
appearances will typically take place in February, May, August and November of each
year. Notice of the exact meeting time and place will be sent to an email address to be
provided to the department when respondent returns this signed consent order.

23. Respondent understands that if she fails to appear at a quarterly appearance without prior
approval of the Department, RIDOH shall be entitled to presume that she is not employed

Inactive” until Respondent appears before the Board or Respondent and RIDOH agree to
an alternative to the appearance that is acceptable to the Department.

24. If Respondent changes her contact email address Respondent must provide RIDOH with
the new email address within two weeks of the change.

25. After Respondent completes 24 months of employment as a nursing assistant at a hospital
or nursing facility with the status of Active Probation, Respondent may apply to RIDOH
for her license status to be changed to “Active.”

26. Any violation of, or failure to adhere to, the terms of this Consent Order shall constitute
