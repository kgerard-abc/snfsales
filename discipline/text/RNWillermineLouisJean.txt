Willermine Gigliola Louis-Jean ("Respondent") has been licensed as a Registered Nurse
pursuant to R.J Gen. Laws §5-34-11 and the Rules and Regulations for Licensing of Nurses 216-

The Respondent recognizes that allegations of professional misconduct have been submitted to the
Rhode Island Board of Nursing Registration and Nursing Education (the "Board") and hereby agrees
to voluntarily surrender the Licenses effective immediately.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. The Respondent holds a license (RN70880) to practice as a registered nurse in the state of

Rhode Island.

their license after acknowledging that the nursing school they attended (Carleen Health

Institute in West Palm Beach Florida) provided school transcripts without completion of

the necessary required program hours and clinical training to obtain an Associate in
Science nursing degree and transcript.

3. The Respondent voluntarily submits this surrender of License to the Board of
Nursing Registration and Nursing Education in exchange for the Board of Nursing
Registration and Nursing Education to cease an investigation and states that no
promises or representations have been made to her other than those terms and
conditions stated herein.
