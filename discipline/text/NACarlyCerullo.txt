RHODE ISLAND DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH
DIVISION OF HEALTHCARE QUALITY AND SAFETY
CENTER FOR PROFESSIONAL BOARDS AND LICENSING

IN THE MATTER OF: CARLY LYNN CERULLO

LICENSEE NO: NA31393
COMPLAINT ID #: C25-0408

CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

In accordance with R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 23-17.9-1-23-17.9-14 (the “Registration and Duty
of Care of Nursing Assistants” or “Nursing Assistant Statute”) and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RI ADC T. 216, Ch. 40, Subch.05, Pt. 22 (the “Nursing Assistant
Regulations”), the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated this
complaint #C25-0408 (the “Complaint”’), charging CARLY LYNN CERULLO
(“Respondent”) with unprofessional conduct.

Pursuant to Section 14.15 of the Rules and Regulations Pertaining to the Practices and
Procedures before the Rhode Island Department of Health, 216-RICR-10-05-4.15, RIDOH and
Respondent, (together, the “Parties”), agree to dispose of the above-captioned matter by entering
into this Consent Order in lieu of an administrative hearing. By consent of the parties, this Consent
Order is enforceable as a final order of RIDOH.

Respondent admits that RIDOH has jurisdiction over this matter and agrees to waive
further procedural steps and any right to appeal this Consent Order. Respondent further agrees that
RIDOH may make the Findings of Fact and Conclusions of Law set forth below.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a nursing assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA31393 (the “License”).

2. At all relevant times, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant with Cedar Crest
Nursing Centre.

3. On or about April 9, 2025, RIDOH received a Complaint alleging that, on that, on or about
April 8, 2025, Respondent told a nursing home resident in her care (“Resident”) to “shut
up” while the Resident was requesting to go to the bathroom.
10.

11.

The Complaint also alleges that, while Respondent was in the Resident’s room, the
Resident was heard yelling at Respondent, “get your hand off my mouth” and don’t put
your hand over my mouth, you dope.”

The Complaint further alleged that, as Respondent left the Resident’s room, the
Respondent could be heard saying, “I can’t stand [the Resident] or these people.”

Respondent has stated that the Resident “was being very loud and it was late” and so she
told the Resident to “please be quiet.”

Respondent has also stated that, when she was in the Resident’s room, the Resident asked
the Respondent, “why did you cover my mouth,” but that this was while Respondent was
making the Resident’s bed.

Upon investigation, RIDOH has determined that there is credible evidence that Respondent
told the Resident to “shut up.”

Upon investigation, RIDOH has also determined that there is credible evidence that it is
more likely than not that Respondent placed her hands on the Resident’s mouth.

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

The conduct described in above paragraphs 3-5, if proved true, constitutes “abuse” within
the meaning of the Nursing Assistant Regulations, 216-RICR-40-05-22.2(A)(1) (“Abuse"
means... any conduct which harms or is likely to harm a patient, ... including, but not
limited to, ridiculing or demeaning a patient, making derogatory remarks to a patient.”).

The conduct described in above paragraphs 3-5 of this Consent Order, if proved true,
constitutes unprofessional conduct pursuant to the following provisions:

a. Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8(2) (“the nursing assistant has violated any of
the provisions of this chapter or the rules enacted in accordance with this chapter; or
acted in a manner inconsistent with the health and safety of the patients of the home
in which he or she is providing nursing assistant services’);

b. Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8(5) (the nursing assistant “has engaged in
conduct detrimental to the health, welfare and safety of patients/residents in his or
her care”);

c. Section 22.6(A)(2), of the Nursing Assistant Regulations, 216-RICR-40-05-22
(“the nursing assistant... has engaged in unprofessional conduct including, but
not limited to, departure from, or failure to conform to, the standards of acceptable
and prevailing practice”);
d. Section 22.6(A)(5), of the Nursing Assistant Regulations, 216-RICR-40-05-22
(“the nursing assistant ... has engaged in any form of abuse as defined in this
Part”).

Whereas Respondent wishes to avoid a disciplinary proceeding on this matter and agrees
to voluntarily enter into this Consent Order in order to resolve the Complaint against her License,
and whereas RIDOH finds the within disposition sufficiently protective of the public health, safety,
and welfare, and for good cause shown, RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following

disposition of the Complaint.

ACCORDINGLY, IT IS THEREFORE AGREED AND ORDERED:

12. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, starting on the date this Consent
Order is last executed by both of the Parties.

13. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall become
binding once signed by the Parties.

14. Respondent understands that the purpose of this Consent Order is to resolve the
Complaint against her License.

15. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s own
choosing at said hearing;

The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent’s behalf;
The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained herein;
The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

Any objection that this Consent Order will (i) be reported to the National
Practitioner Data Bank, (ii) become a permanent part of Respondent’s record
maintained by RIDOH and a part of the public record of this proceeding, or (iii) be
posted on RIDOH’s public website.
