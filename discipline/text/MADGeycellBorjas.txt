CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.|l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
and the Nursing Assistant and Medication Aide Advisory Board (collectively, “RIDOH”)
have investigated Complaint No. C21-0301 (the “Complaint’) charging Geycell P. Borjas,
Medication Aide license number MAD01853, (“Respondent”) with violations of R.I, Gen
Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq..

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Medication Aide licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Island.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Darlington Assisted
Living Center, 124 Armistice Boulevard, Pawtucket, R!] 02860.

3. That on or about February 25, 2021, Respondent admits to taking controlled
substance medication for her personal use from a patient assigned to her care.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5, That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain
under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order
shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.
