CONSENT ORDER FOR VOLUNTARY SURRENDER OF LICENSE |

Sherri Cornlius (“Respondent”) is licensed as a registered nurse by the Board of Nurse
Registration and Nursing Education (“Board”) and holds license no. RN44010. A complaint
(case # RN11-045) has been filed with the Board against Respondent’s Rhode Island registered
nurse license, pursuant to Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-25. In resolution of said complaint,
Respondent and the Board do hereby stipulate and agree to the following:

1. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Board and agrees to waive further

procedural steps before an administrative hearing officer and any right to appeal this.
Consent Order.

2. Respondent VOLUNTARILY SURRENDERS her license as a registered nurse for

an indefinite period but for at least twelve (12) consecutive months, commencing

with the date on which the Board signs this Consent Order (“Effective Date”).

3, This surrender of Respondent’s license shall continue until such time as Respondent
successfully applies for reinstatement to the Board. :

4. - After the Effective Date of this Consent Order and at such time as when Respondent
may wish to apply for the reinstatement of her registered nurse license, Respondent
must complete to the satisfaction of the Board all of the requirements set forth in
EXHIBIT A, (a non-public document to be maintained by the Board within the
Department of Health).

5. The Board reserves the right to determine whether to reinstate Respondent's
‘registered nurse license. 7

6. In the event that Respondent’s registered nurse license is reinstated by the Board, the
Board reserves the right to place restrictions and/or limitations upon her license,
including, but not limited to, a period of probation.
