CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated thereunder,
216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated a complaint
charging, nursing assistant JULIANA CARRION BRITO license number NA 58905
(“Respondent”), with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional conduct
of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island under
Nursing Assistant License Number NA 58905.

2. That on or about November 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant by a nursing
assistant staffing agency and assigned to work at the home of a patient with a shift ending at 5:30
p.m.

3. That on or about December 13, 2024, the patient called the staffing agency and reported that the
Respondent had been leaving her home at 4:00 p.m., and not at 5:30 p.m. as scheduled, thus
abandoning the patient who was in need of her care. The patient also reported that she, the patient,
did not sign Respondent’s electronic timecard when the Respondent left her home at 4:00 p.m.
Further investigation demonstrated that Respondent had forged the patient’s signature on the
electronic timecard.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct
pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated there
under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under the
jurisdiction of RIDOH.

Complaint.
