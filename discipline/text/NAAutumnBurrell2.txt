CONSENT ORDER FOR ACTIVE PROBATION

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated complaints
charging Autumn Burrell (“Respondent”), nursing assistant license number NA 57825 and
medication aide license number 03409, with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the
regulations promulgated pursuant thereto, and R.I. Gen Laws § 5-34-29. After review and
consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional conduct and misdemeanor
charges against Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. Respondent was licensed by RIDOH as a nursing assistant under license number NA 57825 on
November 28, 2022 and as a medication aide under license number MAD 03409 on July 12,
2023. Respondent is not licensed as a nurse in Rhode Island or any other jurisdiction.

2. On or about September 12, 2025, Respondent submitted an application for employment as a
registered nurse for a home health care agency.

3. In response to the question “Are you a licensed RN?” on the application, Respondent stated

“yes.”
4, In response to an inquiry from the home health care agency, Respondent provided a license

card purportedly issued by RIDOH indicating that Respondent was licensed as a Registered
Nurse, License Number RN 03409.

5. A review of RIDOH records found that license number RN 03409 had been expired since
March 1, 1993 and had not been reissued to Respondent.

6. On or about November 21, 2025, Respondent submitted an application for employment as a
nurse to a nursing facility.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

Unless and until Respondent is licensed as a nurse by Rhode Island or any other state or
jurisdiction, Respondent may not represent herself as a nurse or work as a nurse.

Respondent’s licenses shall remain suspended unless and until a Rhode Island licensed
psychiatrist or a Rhode Island licensed psychologist, pre-approved by RIDOH, submits a
letter to RIDOH stating that the psychiatrist or psychologist has met with or treated the
Respondent, and that it is the psychiatrist or psychologist’s professional opinion that
Respondent understands that despite her involvement in a practical nursing course, she has
never been licensed as a nurse, that she cannot practice as a nurse, that she cannot tell anyone
that she is a nurse, and that she violated Rhode Island law by creating and circulating
fraudulent documents stating that she was licensed as a registered nurse. Respondent must
provide RIDOH with the name of the Rhode Island licensed psychiatrist or Rhode Island
licensed psychologist with whom she plans to meet with and/or treat, and RIDOH must pre-
approve the Rhode Island licensed psychiatrist or Rhode Island licensed psychologist with
whom she plans to meet with and/or treat. No statements will be accepted by RIDOH unless
the Rhode Island licensed psychiatrist or a Rhode Island licensed psychologist submitting the
statement was pre-approved by RIDOH.

If the suspensions of Respondent’s licenses are lifted, Respondent will be placed on “active
probation” and must demonstrate two years of employment as a nursing assistant and/ or
medication aide at a hospital or nursing facility in the State of Rhode Island with direct
supervision. Respondent may not work in a private home or anywhere else without direct
supervision.

If at any time Respondent is not employed as a nursing assistant and/or medication aide, the
active probation period will be extended until the required two year period of employment as
a nursing assistant and/or medication aide is completed.

After Respondent completes two years of employment as a nursing assistant and/or medication
aide with the status of active probation, Respondent may apply to RIDOH for her license
statuses to be changed to active.
