CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and the
regulations promulgated thereunder, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with the Board of Nurse
Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Yasmin Vazquez (“Respondent”), a
licensed registered nurse, with a violation of Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode
Island.

The Board, through its investigating committee, has found that Respondent has committed
unprofessional conduct and made the following:
