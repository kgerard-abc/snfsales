CONSENT ORDER

VOLUNTARY SURRENDER OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA46981.

2. On or about, October 29, 2019 was arrested and charged with larceny by the Westerly,
Rhode Island Police Department. Video surveillance captured Respondent taking cash
from the purse of F.L., a woman for whom Respondent was caring for at her home in
Westerly.

3. By engaging in theft from a patient in her care, Respondent engaged in unprofessional
conduct.

4. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

5. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

6. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this
complaint:

—

Respondent voluntarily surrenders her Nursing Assistant license.
on This Consent Order shall become a public record.
