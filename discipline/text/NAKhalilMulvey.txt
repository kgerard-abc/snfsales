CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging KHALIL
MULVEY, NA44403 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration by RIDOH
and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA44403.
2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Tockwotton on the Waterfront.

3. On or about November 21, 2017, Respondent admits that he asked another Nursing
Assistant to secure a specific medication for him from a patient, that he and the other
Nursing Assistant entered a patient’s room, that the other Nursing Assistant removed
medication from the patient’s medication box, and gave said medication to the Respondent

who did ingest said medication.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

- 5, That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
7. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent? s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this

complaint:

1. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand on his Nursing Assistant
license, NA44403.

2. Respondent will participate in 3 hours of continuing education classes in

medication safety and will provide the Board with proof of completion on or before

July 15, 2018. If proof of completion is not provided to the Board on or before July

15, 2018, additional disciplinary action may be taken against Respondent’s license.
3. This Consent Order shall become a public record.
