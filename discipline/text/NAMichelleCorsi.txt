CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the
Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
MICHELLE CORSI, NA12435 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R.J. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration by RIDOH

and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA12435.

2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was self-employed.

3. On or about September 18, 2018, it was discovered that Respondent was advertising the
offering of Nursing Assistant services by means of a “business” she had attempted to
form for such purpose. The services being offered fall outside the scope of practice of a

licensed Nursing Assistant. Respondent admits that she has performed services which

fall outside of her scope of practice while employed as a licensed Nursing Assistant.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules

and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
