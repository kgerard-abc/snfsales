IN THE MATTER OF: JOSEPHINE FARRELL
License Number MAD 03256
Case No. C23-0676AB
CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws 8 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
JOSEPHINE FARRELL, MAD03256 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to
disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R.I. Gen, Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and
consideration by RIDOH and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the
following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Medication Aide licensed to practice in Rhode Island under

Medication Aide License MAD03256.

2. Atall times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed as a Medication Aide for a

staffing agency.

3. Onor about July 13, 2023, Respondent was working at a health care facility through
the staffing agency. She was not familiar with the facility or the residents. She was
dispensing medications when a resident approached her medication cart in the
hallway, She asked an employee of the facility to confirm the identity of the resident
before she administered his medication. The employee confirmed the name of the
resident and the Respondent dispensed the medication. It was eventually
determined that the resident to whom the Respondent had administered the
medication was not the person for whom the medication had been prescribed, but
another resident who had the same first name as the resident for whom the

medication was prescribed.
4, That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R. |, Gen, Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules and
Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order
shall become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of
Respondent’s own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s
behalf; at a hearing; and

d, Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING
RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this complaint:

1:. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand on her Medication
Aide license, MAD03256.

2. This Consent Order shall become a public record.
