SUMMARY SUSPENSION

Kevin Gwozdz (hereinafter "Respondent") is licensed as a registered nurse pursuant to R.I.

General Laws§ 5-34-11 and holds license number RN40176.

FINDINGS OF FACT:
At the time of the incidents, Respondent’s last known registered nurse employment was at
the Charlesgate Nursing Center, 100 Randall St, Providence, RI 02904, Rhode Island.
Respondent is alleged to be in violation of R.I. Gen. Laws§ 5-34-24 and the Rules and
Regulations for the Licensing of Nurses 216-RICR-40-05-3 and the Nursing Scope and
Standards of Practice.
On or about June 10, 2020, the RI Department of Health received a complaint indicating
that Respondent, suffered a relapse from his sobriety and is exhibiting signs of self-
destructive behavior.
On or about June 26, 2020, another complaint was received about the Respondent’s
unacceptable workplace behavior, texting inappropriate comments to staff members and
management. Respondent was verbally harassing staff members and management through
voicemail, swearing while apparently slurring his words.
On or about October 13, 2020, a third complaint was received by the Rhode Island of

Health, about the Respondent’s unacceptable behavior on July 1, 2020 at Roger Williams

Medical Center emergency room. He was violent, tried to choke a nurse and had to be
restrained. The next day he returned to the emergency room for alcohol intoxication and
had to be medicated and restrained due to violent behavior.

6. On or about October 20, 2020, the Respondent placed several calls to the Rhode Island

nursing license.
. These actions constitute grounds for discipline pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-34-24.

Based upon the foregoing, the Director has determined that the continuation of the license of
Respondent as a registered nurse constitutes an immediate danger to the public, Accordingly, pursuant

suspended effective October 22, 2020 and shall remain suspended pending further investigation and
further Order of the Department of Health.
