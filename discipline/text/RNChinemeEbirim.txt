CONSENT ORDER

Pursvant to § 5-34-24 of the Rhode Island General Laws and the Rulesand Regulations
promulgated there under, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter the “Department”),
and the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (hereinafter the “Board”), has
investigated a complaint charging Chineme Ebirim, RN36412 (hereinafter the “Respondent”)
with a violation of § 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Committee of the Board, the following constitutes the

Findings of Fact with respect to the professional performance of the Respondent:

1, Respondent is a registered nurse licensed to practice nursing in the State of Rhode Island

under registered nurse License Number RN36412.

2. That at all pertinent times Respondent was employed at Charlesgate Nursing Home,

Providence, Rhode Island.

3. Itis alleged that Respondent willfully disregarded the standards of RN practice by physically
restraining a patient for the convenience of the staff, for non-therapeutic reasons without a

physician’s order in-a skilled nursing facility.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct

as defined in § 5-34-24 (6v) and the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under.
