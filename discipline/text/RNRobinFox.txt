CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to Section 14.1 of the Rules and Regulations Pertaining to the Practices and
Procedures Before the Rhode Island Department of Health, the Board of Nurse Registration and
Nursing Education (“Board”) and Robin Fox (“Respondent”), a registered nurse (RN) who is
licensed by the Board, agree to resolve the above-referenced complaint against her license by
entering into this Consent Order for Reprimand. Respondent and the Board further agree that
this Consent Order is enforceable as a final Order of the Board.

Wishing to resolve this matter without an administrative hearing, Respondent admits to
the jurisdiction of the Board and agrees to waive further procedural steps and any right to appeal
this Consent Order. Respondent admits that the following Findings of Fact are true and that the
Conclusions of Law are correct.

FINDINGS OF FACT:

1. Respondent is licensed to practice as a registered nurse in the State of Rhode Island under
license RN49400.

matter was opened upon the Board’s receipt of a complaint against Respondent’s license,
case no. C17-0239 (the “Complaint’”), alleging that Respondent had had an inappropriate
relationship with a patient.

3. While Respondent was employed as a Home Health Nurse for Addiction Recovery
