Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 ef seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA51092.
2. Atal! times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Evergreen House Healthcare.

3. On or about September 24, 2017, Respondent was witnessed making derogatory

statements to a patient in his care. Respondent admits to the incident reported.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this
complaint:

Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand on her Nursing
Assistant license, NA51092.

If Respondent is currently employed, he must notify the Board, in writing, of the
identity of his current employer. Respondent is required to participate in in-
service trainings on patients’ rights, abuse, cultural sensitivity, and managing
difficult behaviors, through his identified employer within ninety (90) days of the
ratification of this Consent Order and provide the Board with proof of such
completion.

If Respondent is not currently employed, he must notify the Board, in writing,
within five (5) days of the date that be secures employment, and must also
provide the identity of his employer. Respondent is required to participate in in-
service trainings on patients’ rights, abuse, cultural sensitivity, and managing
difficult behaviors, through his identified employer within (90) days of his
original employment date, and provide the Board with proof of such completion.
This Consent Order shall become a public record.
