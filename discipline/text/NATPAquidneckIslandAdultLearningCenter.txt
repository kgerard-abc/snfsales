CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I, Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health {“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0891 (the “Compliaint"}
charging The Aquidneck Island Adult Learning Center ("Respondent") with
violations of RI. Gen. Laws and Department of Health Regulations,

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW
1, That Respondent was licensed by RIDOH as a nursing assistant training program
under NATP License Number 00108 from January 15, 2015 until January 15, 2023

when its license expired.

2. Subsequent to January 15, 2023, Respondent continued to operate as a nursing
assistant training program despite the fact that its license had expired.

3, On or about October 15, 2024, Respondent was relicensed and issued NATP
License Number 00171.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph two (2) herein constitutes a violation
216- RICR-40-05-22.7.1(A}, promulgated pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws § 23-17,9-3.

5, That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH,

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent
Order shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.
7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to
resolve the Complaint.

9, Respondent agrees to a finding that it was operating as a nursing assistant
