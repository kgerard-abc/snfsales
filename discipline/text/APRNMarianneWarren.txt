CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §§ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4 of
volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with

the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Marianne Warren (the
“Respondent”), an advanced practice registered nurse (license APRN00599), with a violation of

Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of the Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is an advanced practice registered nurse licensed to practice in the State of
Rhode Island under License Number APRN00599. . '

2. That on.or about September 14, 2024, Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOR) received a
complaint alleging that the Respondent had failed to prescribe progesterone to a patient that may
have led to the patient requiring further medical treatment and/or complications.

3. An Investigative Committee (IC) of the Board reviewed the allegations and relevant evidence,
including the Respondent’s response to the allegation. The IC made a preliminary determination
that the Respondent provided services in a manner inconsistent with the health and safety of a
person entrusted to her care. Additionally, the Respondent failed to provide follow up with the

patient leading to incomplete care.

4. That the conduct described in paragraphs three (2) and four (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-40(c).
