IN THE MATTER OF: REGINA DELILLE

COMPLAINT ID #: C24-0149
CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.L Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health (“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No, C20-0273 (the “Complaint”)
charging REGINA DELILLE, nursing assistant license number NA 570980,
(“Respondent”) with violations of R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Iskand under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 57090.

2. That on or about February 5, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant for a home care agency.

3, That on or about February 5, 2024, Respondent was scheduled to work at the
home of a patient from 12 p.m. to 3:00 p.m., but she did not report to the home
of the patient to provide services, thus abandoning her patient.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
