CONSENT ORDER FOR SUSPENSION OF LICENSE

In accordance with R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 23-17.9-1-23-17.9-14 (the “Registration and Duty
of Care of Nursing Assistants” or “Nursing Assistant Statute”) and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RI ADC T. 216, Ch. 40, Subch.05, Pt. 22 (the “Nursing Assistant
Regulations”), the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated this complaint
#C25-0315A-B (the “Complaint”), charging CHRISTINA GONCALVES DEPINA
(“Respondent”) with unprofessional conduct.

Pursuant to Section 14.15 of the Rules and Regulations Pertaining to the Practices and
Procedures before the Rhode Island Department of Health, 216-RICR-10-05-4.15, RIDOH and
Respondent, (together, the “parties”), agree to dispose of the above-captioned matter by entering
into this Consent Order in lieu of an administrative hearing. By consent of the parties, this Consent
Order is enforceable as a final order of RIDOH.

Respondent admits that RIDOH has jurisdiction over this matter and agrees to waive
further procedural steps and any right to appeal this Consent Order. Respondent further agrees that
RIDOH may make the Findings of Fact and Conclusions of Law set forth below.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 45399 (the “License”).

2. Atall relevant times, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant with Visiting
Angels of Rhode Island.

3. While employed as a nursing assistant, Respondent permitted a patient in her care
(“Patient A”) to designate Respondent as a beneficiary of Patient A’s Individual
Retirement Account (IRA).

4, Respondent also administered morphine to Patient A on multiple occasions.
5. Asa licensed nursing assistant, Respondent was not licensed or trained to administer
morphine, a controlled substance.

6. Respondent has stated that Patient A asked her to be an IRA beneficiary, and that
Respondent intended for her designation as Patient A’s IRA beneficiary to be temporary.

7. Respondent has also stated that Patient A was too weak to administer the morphine.

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

8. The conduct described in Paragraphs 3-4 above of this Consent Order constitutes
unprofessional conduct under the following provisions:

a. Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8(2) (“the nursing assistant has violated any of
the provisions of this chapter or the rules enacted in accordance with this chapter;
or acted in a manner inconsistent with the health and safety of the patients of the
home in which he or she is providing nursing assistant services”);

b. 23 R.I. Gen. Laws Ann. § 23-17.9-8(5) (the nursing assistant “[h]as engaged in
conduct detrimental to the health, welfare and safety of patients/residents in his or
her care”);

c. Section 22.6(A)(6), of the Nursing Assistant Regulations, 216-RICR-40-05-22
(“the nursing assistant... has participated ina... financial relationship with a
patient”).

Whereas Respondent wishes to avoid a disciplinary proceeding on this matter and agrees
to voluntarily enter into this Consent Order in order to resolve the Complaint against her License,
and whereas RIDOH finds the within disposition sufficiently protective of the public health, safety,
and welfare, and for good cause shown, RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following

disposition of the Complaint.
ACCORDINGLY, IT IS THEREFORE AGREED AND ORDERED:
Suspension of License
9. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a six (6) month Suspension of her License, to

take effect on the date this Consent Order is signed by both of the parties (the “Effective
Date”).

One (1) Year Active Probation

When Respondent has completed the six (6) month suspension of her License and the
conditions outlined in above Paragraph 10, Respondent’s nursing assistant License will
be reinstated, subject to a one (1) year period of probation, under the following terms and
conditions:

a. Respondent must work as a nursing assistant at a hospital or nursing facility with
direct supervision for a period of twelve (12) months. Respondent may not work
as a nursing assistant in a private home or anywhere without direct supervision.

b. Respondent’s license status shall be listed as “Administrative Inactive” until
Respondent provides RIDOH with documentation from a hospital or nursing
facility stating that the hospital or facility employs or intends to employ
Respondent as a nursing assistant. Upon receipt of this documentation from
Respondent or the hospital or facility, Respondent’s licensing status will be
changed to “Active Probation.”

c. If Respondent changes jobs during her probationary period, Respondent must
provide RIDOH with the name of the new hospital or nursing facility where she is
employed within two weeks of starting employment.

d. Respondent must provide all hospitals and/or nursing facilities where she is
employed as a nursing assistant with a copy of this Consent Order and a Job
Performance Progress Report form to be provided to her by RIDOH.

e. Respondent will ensure that Respondent’s employer provides Job Performance
Progress Reports to RIDOH. The due dates of Respondent’s quarterly reports are
as follows:

i. 1 Quarter Report (January —March) due by April 15th each year
