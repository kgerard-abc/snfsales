ORDER OF SUMMARY SUSPENSION
OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

Alisha Kingston (“Respondent”), was licensed as a nursing assistant in Rhode Island on
or about June 6, 2013 by the Rhode Island Department Health pursuant to RI. Gen. Laws § 23-
17.9-1 et seg. This Order is issued pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14.

FINDINGS OF FACT
1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA45894.
2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Greenwich Farms at Warwick,

an assisted living residence located in Warwick, RI.

3, On or about February 25, 2019, Respondent was charged with obtaining money under false
pretenses and accessing a computer for fraudulent purposes by the Warwick Police
Department.

4, In a Warwick Police Department Incident Report dated February 26, 2019, Respondent
admitted that she had utilized a credit card that belonged to a resident of Greenwich Farms
at Warwick to make in-store and online purchases for her own benefit without the
knowledge or consent of the assisted living resident.

5. In a Warwick Police Department Incident Report dated February 26, 2019, Respondent
stated to police that she had worked at Greenwich Farms at Warwick while she was
intoxicated.

6. After considering the above facts, the Director of the Rhode Island Department Health
finds that public health, safety, and welfare imperatively requires emergency action.
