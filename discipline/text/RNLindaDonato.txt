IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED:

1. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Board and agrees to waive further
procedural steps before an administrative hearing officer and any right to appeal this

Consent Order.

2. Commencing with the date this Consent Order is signed and accepted by the Board
(“Effective Date”), this Consent Order shall be enforceable as an Order of the Board.

3. Commencing with the Effective Date, Respondent’s registered nurse license shall be
REINSTATED under a one (1) year period of PROBATION, subject to the terms
and conditions set forth herein.

4, Any period in which Respondent is not employed as a registered nurse shall serve to
toll (that is, to stop) the running of the probationary period.

5. During the period of probation, Respondent is required to submit quarterly
evaluations (that is, evaluations in three-month intervals) from her employer relating
to Respondent’s conduct and performance, It shall be the responsibility of
Respondent to have her employer timely submit said reports.

6. During the period of probation, Respondent agrees to notify the Director of the Board
in writing of any change in her employment or employment status, including the
name and address of any new employer and the reason for the change.

7. This Consent Order is not effective unless it is accepted by both parties and it is
ratified by the Board.

8. If accepted by both parties and signed and ratified by the Board, this Consent Order
shall be made public and shall be a permanent and public part of Respondent’s record
maintained by the Board.

9. Any failure by Respondent to comply with this Consent Order, once signed and
accepted by both parties, shall subject her to disciplinary action.
