CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, “RIDOH") have investigated Complaint No. C21-0913
(the “Complaint") charging Teresa Edith Hazard, Medication Aide: license number
MADO01935, (“Respondent”) with violations of R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Medication Aide licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Medication Aide License Number MADO1935.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Pawtucket Falls
Healthcare Center, 70 Gill Avenue, Pawtucket, RI 02861.

3. That on or about June 28, 2021, Respondent refused her work assignment and left the
facility, thereby abandoning the patients assigned to her care.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH. /~.
