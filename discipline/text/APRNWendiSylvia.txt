Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §§ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4 of
volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with
the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Wendi Sylvia (the
“Respondent”), an advanced practice registered nurse (license APRNO1 192), with a violation of
Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of the Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1, That Respondent is an advanced practice nurse licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under License Number APRNO1 192.

2. That on or about April 30, 2025, Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) received a
complaint with allegations against the Respondent.

3. That on or about March 25, 2025, Respondent removed three (3) vials of Lidocaine 2%, rather
than the intended Lidocaine 0.5%.

4. The three (3) vials of medication were removed under one (1) patient name; however, the
medication was removed for three (3) patients. Respondent attributed the medications to the
other two patients at a later point in time,

5. Respondent prepared and administered the incorrect concentration of Lidocaine for three (3)
patients. The patients had varying levels of transient reactions.

6. Additionally, Respondent did not document the procedures performed or medication
administered in the patient medical records.

7. That on October 20, 2025, an Investigative Committee (IC) of the Board reviewed the allegations
and relevant evidence, including the Respondent’s response to the allegation. The IC made a

preliminary determination that the Respondent failed to maintain standards established by the
nursing profession and willfully failed to file or record medical records and reports.

8. That the conduct described in paragraphs three (3) to six (6) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-40(c2)(i)(F). Additionally, the conduct
described in those paragraphs constitutes unprofessional conduct as defined in Rhode Island
Gen. Laws § 5-34-40(c)(2)(i)(H) as it specifically, violated Standard 5 Implementation in the
American Nurses Association (ANA) Code of Ethics for Nurses,

AGREEMENT:
The Board and Respondent (together, the Parties”) hereby agree as follows:

9. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Board and hereby agrees to remain under the
jurisdiction of the Board.

10. The Respondent admits to the conduct described in paragraph three (3) to six (6) above.

11. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that it is a proposal of an
Investigative Committee of the Board and is subject to final ratification by the Board. This
Consent Order and the contents herein are not binding on Respondent until final ratification by
the Board.

12, Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a) The right to appear personally or by counsel before the Board;
b) The right to produce witnesses and evidence on their behalf at a hearing;

c)~-The right to cross-examine witnesses;

d) The right to have subpoenas issued by the Board;

e) The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained herein;

f) Any and all right of appeal of this Consent Order;

g) Any objection to the fact that this Consent Order will be presented to the Board for
consideration and review;

h) Any objection to the fact that it will be necessary for the Board to become acquainted with all
evidence pertaining to this matter in order to adequately review this Consent Order;

i) Any objection to the fact that the Board reviewing this Consent Order may be the same as the
hearing committee presiding over this matter should it later be brought to an administrative
hearing.

j) Any objection to the fact that potential bias against the Respondent may occur as a result of

the presentation of this Consent Order to the Board.

13. This Consent Order shall become part of the public record of this proceeding once it is accepted
by all parties and accepted by the Board.

14. The findings set forth herein are based upon a preliminary assessment by the Investigative
Committee and were not the result of an adjudicatory proceeding.
