CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health (“RIDOH") has investigated a complaint charging Niah Cummings, Nursing
Assistant license number NA 38031-T (“Respondent”), with violations of R.I. Gen
Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent was a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of
Rhode Island under temporary license number NA38031-T, which expired on
September 28, 2024.

2. That on or about August 11, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant by a staffing agency.

3. That on or about August 11, 2024, Respondent failed to call or appear for her
scheduled shift at a nursing facility, thus abandoning residents of the facility who
were in need of her care.

4. The Respondent states that she did not intentionally fail to call or appear and that
the reason she did not call or appear was due to a scheduling error by the staffing
agency.

5. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.
