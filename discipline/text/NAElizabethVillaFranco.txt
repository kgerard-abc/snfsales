CENTER FOR PROFESSIONAL BOARDS AND LICENSING

IN THE MATTER OF; ELIZABETH VILLA FRANCO
LICENSEE NO.: NA 52548
COMPLAINT ID #: C25-0162

CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health ("“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0925 (the “Complaint”)
charging ELIZABETH VILLA FRANCO, nursing assistant license number NA 52548
(“Respondent”) with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of
Rhode Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 52548.

2. That on or about February 4, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant by a staffing agency and was assigned to work at the home of a
patient.

3. That on or about February 4, 2025, Respondent failed to appear at the home
of a patient as scheduled, thus abandoning a patient who was in need of her
care.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant fo R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the
Rules and Regulations promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent
Order shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to
resolve the Complaint.

8. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a} The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b) The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attomey of
Respondent's own choosing at said hearing;

c) The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent's
behalf;

d) The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e) The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically
contained herein;

f) The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

g) Any objection that this Consent Order will (i) be reported to the National
Practitioner Data Bank, (ii) become a permanent part of Respondent's
record maintained by RIDOH and a part of the public record of this
proceeding, or (iii) be posted on RIDOH's public website.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this
Complaint:

9. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, effective the day
this Consent Order is last executed.

10.Respondent acknowledges that this Consent Order is an agreement
between Respondent and RIDOH.
