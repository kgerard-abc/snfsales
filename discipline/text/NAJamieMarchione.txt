COMPLIANCE ORDER

SUMMARY SUSPENSION OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

Jamie Marchione (“Respondent”), was licensed as a nursing assistant in Rhode Island on
or about December 17, 2001, pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. This Order is issued
pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14 and 23-1-20.

FINDINGS OF FACT
1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA46987.
2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Nursing Placement as a nursing

assistant assigned tothe home of F.L.

3. On or about,. October 29, 2019, Respondent was arrested and charged with five counts of
larceny by the Westerly Police Department. Video surveillance captured Respondent
taking cash from the purse of F.L., a woman for whom Respondent was caring for at her
home in Westerly. Respondent admitted to taking money from F.L.’s purse on October 29,
2019 and on prior occasions in October and September.

4. Respondent admitted to being addicted to cocaine during the month of September.
5. Respondent was released from employment by Nursing Placement.

6. After considering the above facts, the Director of Health finds that public health, safety, or
welfare imperatively requires emergency action.

ORDER

1. Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14, Respondent’s nursing assistant license is
SUSPENDED until further order of the department.
