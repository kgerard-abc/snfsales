CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RI. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 ef seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the
Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
BEATRIZ DALOMBA, NA45225 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to
disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R.1. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration
by RIDOH and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall
constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing.

Assistant License NA45225.
2. Atall times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Nursing Placement, Inc.

3. On multiple occasions while assigned to care for this patient, Respondent committed
multiple infractions including, but not limited to: threatening the patient with transfer to
a nursing home, calling the patient a liar, not following proper sanitation or infection
control guidelines, stealing patient’s food and drink from her home, refusal to perform
any homemaking tasks for patient, and selling products to the patient for personal

financial gain.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
7, That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;
b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and
d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this

complaint:

1. Respondent is hereby issued a 6 month Suspension on her Nursing Assistant
license, NA45225.

2: Respondent is required to complete twelve (12) continuing education units on

patients’ rights, patient abuse, infection control, and boundary violations, and
must also submit proof of completion within thirty (30) days of the ratification of
this Consent Order. On-line continuing education units in the required subjects are
acceptable from any of the following websites:

https://www.mycnajobs.com

https://www.nursingworld.org

https://www.nesbn.org

3, All documentation of continuing education for proof of compliance must be
submitted to DOH.PRCompliance@health.ri.gov
4. This Consent Order shall become a public record.
