PROBATION ORDER
1. Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island
Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has reviewed an application submitted by YOUR CHOICE OF
TRAINING PROGRAM LLC (Applicant) to assume the ownership of YOUR CHOICE OF
TRAINING PROGRAM LLC, which was licensed by RIDOH as a nursing assistant training
program on May 5, 2023, under license number NATP 00154.
After review and consideration of the application, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

2. That Your Choice of Training Program LLC was licensed by RIDOH as a nursing assistant training
program on May 5, 2023.

3. That since being licensed on May 5, 2023, Your Choice has been owned by Jose Nunez but .
managed and operated by Evelyn Pinto De Duran.

4, That Your Choice, by and through Evelyn Pinto De Duran, has applied to RIDOH to be licensed
with Evelyn Pinto De Duran listed as the owner of Your Choice.

5. That RIDOH requested additional documentation in connection with the application and that
RIDOH conducted an unannounced site visit at the premises of Your Choice on or about March .
20, 2025.

6. That RIDOH conducted an announced site visit at the premises of Your Choice on April 4, 2025.

7. The site visits and investigation revealed the following:

a) The program was unable to produce clinical logs and other educational documents for many
of the students in the previous graduating class, and many other documents were without dates

or signatures.

b) Each class section (morning and evening) contained 60 students.

c) The clinical facilities involved in clinical training were unable to verify that all the students
were at the facility identified by the program or that they had completed the required hours of
clinical training identified in the curriculum.

d) The program was using two different clinical facilities, and some students were completing _
clinical hours at an assisted living facility. Neither facility was approved by RIDOH as —
required, nor were facility contracts in place for either facility.

e) While the submitted program materials indicate that English proficiency is evaluated,

i. the program was unable to produce any student documentation regarding this
evaluation;

ii. classes were being instructed predominantly in the Spanish language as evidenced ~
by the text books the students were using and observation by RIDOH staff; and

iii. several students from the prior graduating class who were contacted by RIDOH or
who contacted RIDOH independently were unable to communicate with RIDOH staff or
answer questions about their clinical training in English.

8. The conduct described in paragraph seven (7) constitutes violations of 216- RICR-40-05-22.7.1.

9. The conduct described in Paragraph seven (7) is inconsistent with the program documents -
and program criteria submitted in connection with the application.

ACCORDINGLY AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and Applicant hereby agree to the following:

14. Applicant voluntarily agrees to accept a two (2) year Probationary period license during which
Evelyn Pinto De Duran may own Your Choice Training Program LLC. The probationary
provisions are as follows:

a) Each Monday through Friday, morning and afternoon section shall be limited to 30
students per section. No weekend sections will be allowed;

b) All English as a Second Language (ESL) students shall pass an independent English
proficiency entrance exam such as Comprehensive Adult Student Assessment Systems
(CASAS), Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) or other RIDOH approved
English proficiency testing program;

c) No clinical hours shall begin prior to week 4 of the program;

d) All clinical hours set forth in the curriculum will be completed in a preapproved skilled
nursing facility and will be completed in their entirety;

e) RIDOH will be notified of any staff or facility changes 48 business hours prior to those
changes going into effect;

f) RIDOH shall be provided with a list of all clinical groupings including who is in the group,
where and when they will be completing their clinical hours seven (7) days prior to the first
day of each clinical session;

g) Your Choice will maintain individual educational files for each student which shall
include those student records identified in the application including but not limited to
student’s applications, references, interview notes, entrance exam results, ESL proficiency
exam results, all scored tests or evaluations given during the program period, Bureau of
Criminal Investigation. (BCI) and health records required by the clinical facility, and the fully
signed clinical documentation form. These files shall be maintained for a period of 2 (two)
years after completion of the program and shall be available to RIDOH staff immediately
upon request;

h) During the probationary period Your Choice shall not enroll any students whose tuition is
paid for in whole or in part by the State of Rhode Island; and

i) Quarterly Your Choice will provide RIDOH with a list of the names of all students who
graduated from their program and are currently employed by Your Choice Home Care LLC,
Your Choice of Home Care, or any other home care provider owned or managed by Evelyn
Pinto De Duran, and whether any of the graduates will be scheduled to care for two (2) or
fewer individuals. Reports are due by the 15" of January, April, July and October.

15. Should Your Choice not comply with the laws and regulations governing Nursing Assistant
Training Programs, or the terms of this probationary agreement, its license may be subject to
disciplinary action including but not limited to suspension or revocation. _

16. After applicant completes two (2) years of probation with the status of Active Probation,
Applicant may apply to RIDOH for the license status to be changed to “Active.” Applicant
understands and agrees that formal discharge from this Probation Order shall take place only
upon Applicant’s successful completion of the terms and conditions stated herein and by the
approval of RIDOH.

17. Any violation of, or failure to adhere to, the terms of this Probation Order may subject Applicant .
to further disciplinary action at the discretion of RIDOH, including but not limited to revocation ~
of its license.

18. Applicant acknowledges that this Probation Order is an agreement between Applicant and
RIDOH and incorporates all representations made in the application and its supporting
documents.

19. This Probation Order is effective upon signature by RIDOH and Applicant.
