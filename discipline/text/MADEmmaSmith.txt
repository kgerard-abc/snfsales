CONSENT ORDER FOR VOLUNTARY SURRENDER OF LICENSE

Emma Smith (“Respondent”) is licensed as a Medication Aide by the Nursing Assistant
Advisory Board (“Board”) and holds ficense MADO1165. A complaint (Case: 16-1550)
has been filed with the Board against Respondent’s Medication Aide license, pursuant
io RIGL 23-17.9 and the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under. In resolution
of said complaint, Respondent and the Board do hereby stipulate and agree to the
following:

1. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Board and agrees to waive further
procedural steps before an administrative hearing officer and any night to appeal! this
Consent Order.

2. Respondent VOLUNTARILY SURRENDERS her license as a Medication Aide for an
indefinite period.

3. This surrender of Respondent's license shall continue until when Respondent
successfully applies for reinstatement from the Board.

4. That the Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent
Order shall become binding once signed by the Respondent and the Department.

This Consent Order shall constitute a final disposition of the matter identified as
Case: C16-1550.
