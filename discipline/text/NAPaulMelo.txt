CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to R.J. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
(“RIDOH”) has investigated Complaint Nos. C25-0229AB (the “Complaint”) charging PAUL
MELO, nursing assistant license number NA41036 (“Respondent”) with violations of R.I. Gen
Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Nursing Assistant License Number NA41036.

2. That on or about March 11, 2025, Respondent failed to adhere to boundaries that separate
personal and professional life and roles to ensure a safe, ethical, and effective relationship
between professionals and those they serve.

3. That the conduct described in paragraph two constitutes unprofessional conduct pursuant to
R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under.

4. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under the
jurisdiction of RIDOH.

5. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

6. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve the
Complaint.
