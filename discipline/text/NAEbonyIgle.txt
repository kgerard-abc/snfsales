SUMMARY SUSPENSION OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

Ebony Igle (“Respondent”) has been licensed as a Nursing Assistant pursuant to R. |.
Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. since May 15, 2009. Complaint ID #C23-0002 (the
“Complaint”) recently came before the Rhode Island Department of Health, Division of
Customer Services,.Center for Professional Boards and Licensing (“RIDOH”) alleging
that Respondent had engaged unprofessional conduct pursuant to R. |. Gen. Laws
§23-17.9-1 ef seq. This Summary Suspension is issued related to the Complaint and
pursuant to R. |. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14(c). After careful consideration and further
investigation by RIDOH, the following constitute the findings of fact and conclusions of

law with respect to the allegations against Respondent in the Complaint:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode

island under License Number NA39406 (the “License”).

2. That on or about December 31, 2022, RIDOH received notice that Respondent

admitted that items found on a table in a patient's room consisting of a white
powdery substance, a mirror, and a razor blade, did belong to her. Testing of the
white powdery substance determined it to be cocaine.

. That, at all times during which the incident described in the Paragraph(s) above
took place, Respondent was employed by Grand Islander Center, 333 Green
End Avenue, Middletown, RI 02842.

State of Rhode Island
4. That the RIDOH director (the “Director of Heaith”) has reviewed the facts and
conclusions in this case and finds that public health, safety, or welfare
imperatively requires emergency action.

ORDER

5. Based on the foregoing and pursuant to R. |. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq, the
License is hereby SUSPENDED until further Order of RIDOH.

6. A written request for hearing may be filed with the Director of Health within ten
(10) days after service of this Suspension at the address below. If a hearing is
requested and held, an Administrative Hearing Officer shall preside over such
hearing. As a result of the hearing, discipline may be imposed on the License
(for example, it may be suspended or revoked), or no discipline may be imposed
on the License.
