                                    CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq, and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder. 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, "RIDOH") have investigated Complaint No. Cl9-
1293 (the "Complaint") charging Kellyann Guindon, Nursing Assistant license number
NA26 l 4 l , ("Respondent") with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq ..

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to.the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent. the following are and shall constitute


FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
   under Nursing Assistant License Number NA2614 l.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Trinity Health &
   Rehabilitation Center, Woonsocket, RI.

3. That on or about September 28, 2019, Respondent exhibited a lack of professionalism
   with a difficult resident.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
   conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
   promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain
   under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order
   shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve
   the complaint(s) of unprofessional conduct against Respondent alleged in this
   Consent Order.
