CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated a complaint
alleging unprofessional conduct by MORENA MAYORGA (“Respondent”). After review and
consideration by RIDOH, the following are and shall constitute

1.

FINDINGS OF FACT

Respondent is a nursing assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 57344.

On or about October 1, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at an
assisted living facility.

On or about October 1, 2025, Respondent struck a resident of the facility.
The conduct described in paragraph three constitutes unprofessional conduct pursuant to

R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, effective the day this Consent
Order is last executed.

Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

Respondent understands that the purpose of this Consent Order is to resolve the
complaint against her license.
