CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.J. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations

promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the
Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
DAPHNEY PIERRE, NA50107 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration by RIDOH
and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall constitute

G2

FINDINGS OF FACT

Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA50107.

At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by the Rhode Island Veterans

Home.

On or about July 17, 2018, Respondent failed to follow protocol and an ambulation order

issued by a Physician. Said failure resulted in injuries to a patient.

That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this

complaint:

i Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand on her Nursing
Assistant license, NA50107.

2 Respondent is required to obtain one (1) continuing education credit in safe

patient handling within sixty (60) days of ratification of this Order, and to provide
the Board with proof of such completion.
3. This Consent Order shall become a public record.
