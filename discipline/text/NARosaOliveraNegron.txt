                                       CONSENT ORDER

  Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
  thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health ("RIDOH") has
  investigated Complaint No. C24-0360 (the "Complaint") charging ROSA OLIVERA NEGRON,
  nursing assistant license number NA 54522 ("Respondent"), with violations of R.I. Gen Laws
  §23-17.9-1 et seq.

  After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
  conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

                       FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

  1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
     under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 54522.


_.,, 2. That on or about March 28, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant and was
        assigned to worl< for a patient at the patient's home.

  3. That on or about March 28, 2024, Respondent did not go to the patient's home, thus
     abandoning the patient.

  4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
     conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
     promulgated there under.

  5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
     the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

  6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
     become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.
7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve the
   Complaint.
