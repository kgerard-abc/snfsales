           SUMMARY SUSPENSION OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE


CHRISTINA SCANLON (“Respondent”) has been licensed as a Nursing Assistant
pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. since March 21, 2011. Complaint ID
#C23-1228 (the “Complaint”) and other documentation recently came before the Rhode
Island Department of Health, Division of Customer Services, Center for Professional
Boards and Licensing (“RIDOH”) alleging that Respondent had engaged unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. This Summary Suspension is
issued related to the Complaint and pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14(c). After
careful consideration and further investigation by RIDOH, the following constitute the
findings of fact and conclusions of law with respect to the allegations against
Respondent in the Complaint:

                 FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

   1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
      Island under License Number NA 42354 (the “License”)

   2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant
      at the home of E.E., a person over 65 years of age.

   3. That on or about December 15, 2023, Respondent was arrested and charged with
      three felony counts, to wit: fraudulent use of a credit card, embezzlement and
      fraudulent conversion, and exploitation of an elder based on evidence the theft of
      over $25,000.00 from E.E. See RI Judiciary, District Court Case No. 62-23-10349.




                                   State of Rhode Island
   4. That the RIDOH director (the “Director of Health”) has reviewed the facts and
      conclusions in this case and finds that public health, safety, or welfare imperatively
      requires emergency action.


                                             ORDER

   1. Based on the foregoing and pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq, the
      License is hereby SUSPENDED until further Order of RIDOH.

   2. A written request for hearing may be filed with the Director of Health within ten (10)
      days after service of this Suspension at the address below. If a hearing is
      requested and held, an Administrative Hearing Officer shall preside over such
      hearing. As a result of the hearing, discipline may be imposed on the License (for
      example, it may be suspended or revoked), or no discipline may be imposed on
      the License.
