CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
(“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0304 (the “Complaint") charging
NICOLE RODI, nursing assistant license number NA45182("Respondent”) with
violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
iskand under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 45182.

2. That on or about August 11, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant at a nursing facility.

3, That on or about August 11, 2024, Respondent arrived at the facility for a shift
scheduled to begin at 7:00 a.m. and to end at 1:00 p.m. Respondent arrived at
the facility at 7:00 a.m. and reported to the second floor. A nurse on the second
floor asked her to work on the first floor instead of working on the second floor, at
which time Respondent terminated her position at the facility and left the
building, thus abandoning the residents who were in need of her care.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
