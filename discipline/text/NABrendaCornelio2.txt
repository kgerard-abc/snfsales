RELIEF OF SANCTION OF REPRIMAND

Name: BRENDA CORNELIO |
License Type: Nursing Assistant
License Number: NA31319
Complaint #: C 12-978

The Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (Board) considered this licensee's request
to relieve a sanction of a reprimand that was placed on her Nursing Assistant
License #NA31319. The ratified sanction, dated January 31, 2013 stipulates that
this licensee must work in a Supervised environment and is prohibited from

providing home care.

After careful review and evaluation, the Board unanimously recommended that
her Nursing Assistant license, #NA31319, be relieved of the sanction. The board
concluded that Ms. Cornelio has satisfied the Board that her conduct when
working as a Certified Nursing Assistant is and shall continue to be in adherence
to RIGL§ 23-17.9 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated

thereunder.
