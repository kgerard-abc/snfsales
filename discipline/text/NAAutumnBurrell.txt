RHODE ISLAND DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH
DIVISION OF HEALTHCARE QUALITY AND SAFETY
CENTER FOR PROFESSIONAL BOARDS AND LICENSING

In the Matter of: AUTUMN BURRELL
License Nos.: NA57825 and MAD03409
Complaint ID #s: C25-1229A, C25-1365A, and C25-1530

NOTICE OF SUMMARY SUSPENSION
OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE AND MEDICATION AIDE LICENSE

This Order is issued pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14.

FINDINGS OF FACT

. Respondent was licensed by the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) as a Nursing

Assistant under license number NA57825 on November 28, 2022, and as a Medication Aide
under license number MAD03409 on July 12, 2023. Respondent is not licensed as a nurse in
Rhode Island or any other jurisdiction.

. On or about September 12, 2025, Respondent submitted an application for employment as a

registered nurse for a home health care agency.

. In response to the question “Are you a licensed RN?” on the application, Respondent stated

66. 2

yes

. In response to an inquiry from the home health care agency, Respondent provided a license

card purportedly issued by RIDOH indicating that Respondent was licensed as a Registered
Nurse, License Number RN03409.

. A review of RIDOH records found that license number RN03409 had been expired since

March 1, 1993, and had not been reissued to Respondent.

. On or about October 14 and 15, 2025, Respondent stated in emails to RIDOH that she was

working at a substance abuse clinic as a dispensing nurse, and that “a dispensing nurse is a
nurse who dispenses methadone doses to patients who have severe pain or tapering off other
uses of narcotics/pain relivers.”

. Medication Aides licensed by RIDOH are not permitted to dispense Schedule II controlled

substances. Methadone is a Schedule II controlled substance.

1
8. On or about November 19, 2025, Respondent submitted an application for employment as a
nurse to a nursing facility.

9. In response to an inquiry from the nursing facility, Respondent provided a license card
purportedly issued by RIDOH indicating that Respondent was licensed as a Registered Nurse,
License Number RN034091.

10. A review of RIDOH records found that license number RN034091 was not issued to
Respondent or any other individual.

11. Respondent has fraudulently represented herself as a registered nurse to two prospective
employers and has stated that she has dispensed methadone.

12. Respondent is in violation of R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-34-29, § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and 216-RICR-
490-05-22.

13. Respondent has worked beyond the scope of her nursing assistant and medication aide licenses
and presents a danger to public health and safety because she is not properly educated or
trained to work as a nurse.

14. After considering the above facts, the Director of the Department of Health finds that public
health, safety, or welfare imperatively requires emergency action.

ORDER

Based on the foregoing and pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14, Respondent’s nursing
assistant license and medication aide licenses are SUSPENDED until further order of the
Department.

A written request for a hearing on the summary suspension may be sent to the Director within
ten days after service of this Notice. Questions may be directed to at 401-222-4390.
