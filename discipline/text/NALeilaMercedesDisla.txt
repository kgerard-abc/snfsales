CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health (“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0925 (the “Complaint”)
charging LEILA MERCEDES DISLA, nursing assistant license number NA 48823
(“Respondent”) with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 58007.

2. That on or about August 16, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant by a staffing agency and was assigned to work at the home of a patient.

3. That on or about August 16, 2024, Respondent failed to appear at the home of
the patient as scheduled, but clocked in and out as if she were in the home,
checked off tasks as completed when in fact they were not, and forged the
client's signature, thus committing fraud and abandoning the patient.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. aime the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
