CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health ("RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0403 (the “Complaint”}
charging ADELCIA GOMES, Nursing Assistant license number NA 45733,
("Respondent") with violations of R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute,

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent was licensed by the State of Rhode Island as a Nursing
Assistant under license number NA 45733 which expired on June 30, 2022.

2. On or about April 12, the Rhode Island Department of Health determined
that Respondent was employed by at a hospital after her license had
expired, and was thus working as a nursing assistant without a license.

3. Respondent states that she unintentionally forgot to renew her nursing
assistant license.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and
the Rules and Regulations promuigated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent
Order shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to
resolve the Complaint.

8. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of
Respondent's own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on
Respondent's behalf;

d. The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e. The tight to further procedural steps except for those specifically
contained herein;

f. The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order;

and
g. Any objection that this Consent Order will be reported to the National
Practitioner Data Bank as well as posted on RIDOH's public website.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this
Complaint:

9. Respondent agrees to a finding that she engaged in unprofessional
conduct because she continued to work as a nursing assistant after the
expiration of her nursing assistant license.

10. Respondent acknowledges that this Consent Order is an agreement
between Respondent and RIDOH.

11. This Consent Order shall become a permanent part of Respondent's
record maintained by RIDOH and a part of a public record of this

proceeding.
