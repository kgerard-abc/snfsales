CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the
Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
DAPHNEY PEROU, NA46434 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration by RIDOH
and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA46434.
2. Atall times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Comfort Keepers.

3. Respondent abandoned her overnight shift when she left the client’s home on November
6, 2017 at 11:53 p.m. and did not return until November 7, 2017 at 1:39 a.m. Respondent
also left that shift on November 7, 2017 at 6:23 a.m. but did not clock out until 8:12 a.m.
on that date. Respondent also took five hundred ($500.00) dollars cash from the client’s

home while they were sleeping. Respondent admits to the incidents reported.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. IL. Gen. Laws $23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
7. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this
complaint:

—

Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Suspension on her Nursing
Assistant license, NA46434, for a period of two (2) years.

2. If Respondent chooses to re-train and re-apply for a Nursing Assistant license
after the conclusion of her two (2) year Suspension, she will be required to work
in a facility, under supervision, for one (1) year after the issuance of the license.

3 This Consent Order shall become a public record.
