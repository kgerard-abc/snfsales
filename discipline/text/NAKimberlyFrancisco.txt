CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-]1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
and the Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, “RIDOH") have investigated
Complaint No. C20-0273 (ihe “Complaint”) charging Kimberly Francisco, Nursing
Assistant license number NA43977, (“Respondent”) with violations of R.l. Gen Laws
§23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA43977.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Health Care
Services, 400 Reservoir Avenue, Suite LL-J, Providence, RI 02907 and worked at the
home of client M.L.

3. Respondent admits that beginning on or about December 20, 2019, and on
subsequent occasions, Respondent purchased items for herself using client M.L.’s
credit card.

4, Respondent has made full restitution to the client.

5. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

6. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain
under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

7. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order
shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

8. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve
the Complaint.
9. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a) The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b) The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of
Respondent's own choosing at said hearing;

behalf;

d) The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e) The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained
herein;

f) The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

g) Any objection that this Consent Order will be reported to the National
Practitioner Data Bank as well as posted on RIDOH’s public website.
