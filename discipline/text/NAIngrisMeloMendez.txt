RHODE ISLAND DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH
DIVISION OF HEALTHCARE QUALITY AND SAFETY
CENTER FOR PROFESSIONAL BOARDS AND LICENSING

IN THE MATTER OF: INGRIS M. MELO MENDEZ

LICENSEE: NA59932
COMPLAINT ID #: C25-0809B

CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

In accordance with R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 23-17.9-1-23-17.9-14 (the “Registration and Duty
of Care of Nursing Assistants” or “Nursing Assistant Statute”) and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RI ADC T. 216, Ch. 40, Subch.05, Pt. 22 (the “Nursing Assistant
Regulations”), the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated this complaint
#C25-0809B (the “Complaint”), charging INGRIS M. MELO MENDEZ (“Respondent”) with
unprofessional conduct.

Pursuant to Section 14.15 of the Ru/es and Regulations Pertaining to the Practices and
Procedures before the Rhode Island Department of Health, 216-RICR-10-05-4.15, RIDOH and
Respondent, (together, the “parties”), agree to dispose of the above-captioned matter by entering
into this Consent Order in lieu of an administrative hearing. By consent of the parties, this Consent
Order is enforceable as a final order of RIDOH.

Respondent admits that RIDOH has jurisdiction over this matter and agrees to waive
further procedural steps and any right to appeal this Consent Order. Respondent further agrees that
RIDOH may make the Findings of Fact and Conclusions of Law set forth below.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a nursing assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA59932 (the “License”).

2. Atall relevant times, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant with Nursing
Placement, Inc., located at 588 Pawtucket Ave., Pawtucket, Rhode Island, and charged
with providing home care services to a client (“Client A”).

3. On July 3, 2025, Respondent was scheduled to work a five-hour shift from 1:30 p.m. to
6:30 p.m. at Client A’s residence.
4. In or about July 3, 2025, 2025, Respondent left Client A’s house in the middle of her
shift.

5. After leaving Client A’s home mid-shift, Respondent returned to Client A’s home at 6:29
p.m., solely for the purpose of “clocking out,” thereby misrepresenting the hours she
worked in Client A’s home that day.

6. When Respondent returned to Client A’s home to “clock out,” Client A was no longer at
home and Respondent “clocked out” from the parking lot, using a mobile app.

7. Although Client A was not home when Respondent “clocked out,” Respondent signed
Client A’s name on her timesheet, creating the false impression that it was Client A’s
signature.

8. Respondent has stated that, on July 3, 2025, she asked Client A for “permission” to leave
mid-shift to go to her agency to address an administrative issue. (Respondent stated that

she “had forgotten to clock in on the agency’s mobile app”).

9. Respondent has also stated that Client A told her it was “okay” for her (Respondent) to
leave mid-shift because the Client was planning to go to the store with their daughter.

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

10. The conduct described in Paragraphs 4-7 of this Consent Order constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to the following provisions:

a. Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8(2) (“the nursing assistant has violated . . .
the rules enacted in accordance with this chapter’);

b. Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8(6) “Any other causes that may be set forth
in regulations promulgated under this chapter.”);

c. Section 22.6(A)(2), of the Nursing Assistant Regulations, 216-RICR-40-05-22
(“the nursing assistant ... has engaged in unprofessional conduct including, but
not limited to, departure from, or failure to conform to, the standards of
acceptable and prevailing practice”);

d. Section 22.6(A)(4) of the Nursing Assistant Regulations, 216-RICR-40-05-22
(“the nursing assistant... abandoned a patient or failed to appear for scheduled
employment”).
Whereas Respondent wishes to avoid a disciplinary proceeding on this matter and agrees
to voluntarily enter into this Consent Order in order to resolve the Complaint against her License,

and whereas RIDOH finds the within disposition sufficiently protective of the public health, safety,

disposition of the Complaint.

ACCORDINGLY, IT IS THEREFORE AGREED AND ORDERED:

11. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, starting the day this Consent
Order is last executed.

12. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

a. The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent’s behalf;

d. The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e. The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained

f. The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and
