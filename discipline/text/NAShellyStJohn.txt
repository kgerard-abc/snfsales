CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, "RIDOH") have investigated Complaint No. C22-0901
(the “Complaint") charging Shelly Alyce St. John, Nursing Assistant license number
NA35329, (“Respondent”) with violations of R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1, That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
Under Nursing Assistant License Number NA35329.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by The Fogarty Center, 310
Maple Avenue, Barrington, RI 02806.

3, On or about June 30, 2022, it was determined that the Respondent had continued to
work as a Nursing Assistant after the expiration of her Nursing Assistant license, NA35329,
on June 30, 2021.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
