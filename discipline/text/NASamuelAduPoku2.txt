CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17,9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated |
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, "RIDOH") have investigated Complaint No, C22-0621
{the “Complaint") charging Samuel Adu-Poku, Nursing Assistant license number NA18206,
(“Respondent”) with violations of R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Nursing Assistant License Number NA18206.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Eleanor Slater Hospital,
111 Howard Avenue, Cransfon, Ri 02921.

3. On or about May 6, 2022, Respondent made statements to a patient that were
disrespectful, demeaning and threatening.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.|, Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5, That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees fo remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shail
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve the
Complaint.
