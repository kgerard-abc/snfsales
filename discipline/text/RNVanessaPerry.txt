CONSENT ORDER WITH PROBATION
Pursuant to Section § 5-34-25 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island, as
Amended, a complaint was filed with the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education
(hereinafter “Board”) charging, Vanessa Perry RN56585 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with the
violation of Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island, as Amended.
The Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (“Board”) and Vanessa Perry

(“Respondent”) do hereby agree and stipulate as follows:

FINDINGS OF FACT:

l. Respondent is licensed to practice as a registered nurse in the State of
Rhode Island under license RN42955.

2. Respondent was employed as a nurse at Kent County Memorial Hospital,
Warwick, Rhode Island, on or about March 14, 2018.

3. Respondent was precepting as a circulating nurse in a surgical case
involving a right partial nephrectomy on or about March 14,2018.

4, Respondent and members of the surgical team verbally verified the
incision mark during the universal timeout right before the incision but did
not visually verify the mark.

5. The Board issued a hearing notice dated April 17, 2019 pursuant to which
it charged Respondent with unprofessional conduct and scheduled a
hearing on the issue of whether or not to impose discipline against
Respondent's license.

6. The Department of Health issued a Notice of Violation and Compliance
Order to the hospital on or about November 21, 2018.

The hospital entered into a Consent Agreement with the Department of
Health pursuant to which the hospital submitted plans of correction.

The Hospital revised its policy regarding Universal Protocol for Correct
Patient, Procedure, Site Side Surgery and Invasive Procedures,

AGREEMENT

Respondent submits to the jurisdiction of the Board and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of the Board.

. Commencing with the date this Consent Order for Probation is signed and

accepted by the Board (“effective date”), this Consent Order shall be
enforceable as an Order of the Board.

Commencing with the Effective Date, Respondent's registered nurse license
shall be under a one (1) year period of PROBATION, subject to the terms and
conditions set forth herein.

. Any period in which Respondent is not employed as a registered nurse shall
serve to TOLL (this is, to stop) the running of the probationary period.

During the period of probation, Respondent shall fully comply with the laws
and regulations governing the practice of nursing.

The hearing referenced in paragraph 5 above is hereby stayed.

Should Respondent fail to comply with the terms of this Consent Order for
Probation, the stay referenced in paragraph 6 above shall be lifted, giving way
to a hearing at which a determination shall be made as to whether or not
disciplinary action shall issue against Respondent's license. Respondent shall
be afforded all rights to Practices and Procedures Before the Rhode Island
Department of Health,

. Respondent agrees to complete twenty-five (25) continuing education units
("CEUs") during the one (1) year period of Probation. Such CEUs shall be in
addition to the annual 10 CEU requirements to maintain licensure. Such
CEUs shall be in addition to any CEUs already taken at the direction of the
hospital. Respondent shall submit documentation of successful completion of
the CEUs by the probationary deadline set forth in this Consent Order for
Probation.

Respondent agrees to notify the Director of the Board in writing of any change in her
employment or employment status, including the name and address of any new
employer and the reason for change of employment.
Upon completion of the Respondent’s one (1) year probationary period, the
Respondent shall provide evidence that she has met the terms of the Consent Order for
Probation.

This Consent Order shall constitute a full and final disposition of this complaint.

This Consent Order shall become part of the public record of this proceeding once
it is accepted by all parties and accepted by the Board. Respondent's license shall reflect

that the status is PROBATIONARY.
