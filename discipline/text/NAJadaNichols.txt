CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
("RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0419 (the “Complaint") charging
JADA L. NICHOLS, nursing assistant license number NA 57652, (“Respondent”) with
violations of R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 57652.

2, That between March 3, 2024 through March 14, 2024, Respondent was employed
as a hospice nursing assistant by a nursing agency and was assigned to visit
patients at a nursing facility.

3, That between March 3, 2024 through March 14, 2024, Respondent reported her
arrival and departure at the assigned nursing facility to her employer by utilizing
an electronic application, but in thirteen of the reports sent to her employer the
global positioning records indicate that the reports were sent from a location
adjacent to the building in which Respondent resides, and not from the nursing
faculty.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6, That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent
Order shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to
resolve the Complaint.
