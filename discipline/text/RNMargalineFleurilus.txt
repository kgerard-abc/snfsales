CONSENT ORDER FOR VOLUNTARY SURRENDER

The Rhode Island Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (“The Board”) and
Margaline Fleurilus (“Respondent”) agree to dispose of the above-captioned matter by consent
order in lieu of an administrative hearing. Whereas the Boatd finds the within disposition
protective of the public health, safety, and welfare, and for good cause shown. By. consent of the
parties, this Consent Order is enforceable as a final order.

AUTHORITY

The Board is authorized to regulate and enforce Rhode Island law goverming the practice of
nursing, pursuant to R.I. Gen, Laws § 5-34-7 and 216-RICR-40-05-3.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1, The Respondent holds a license (RN74274) to practice as a registered nurse in the state of Rhode
Island pursuant to R.I Gen. Laws §5-34-11 and the Rules and Regulations for Licensing of
Nurses 216-RICR-40-05-3,

2. The Respondent is the subject of a Complaint before the Board.

3. That the Respondent is accused of obtaining a nursing license and diploma without completing
the necessary didactic or clinical hours.

CONCLUSION S OF LAW

4. The findings above constitute professional misconduct and grounds for the discipline of
Respondent’s License pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(1) and the Regulations at 216-

RICR-40-05-3.9,
