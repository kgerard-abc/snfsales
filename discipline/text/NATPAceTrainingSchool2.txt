CONSENT ORDER FOR PROBATION

Pursuant to Section 14.1 of the Rules and Regulations Pertaining to the Practices and
Procedures before the Rhode Island Department of Health, 216-RICR-10-05-4, the Rhode Island
Department of Health (RIDOH” or the “Department”) and ACE Home Health Aid Training School,
LLC, aka “ACE Training School, Inc.” (the “Respondent”), (together, the “parties”), agree to dispose
of the above captioned matter by entering into this Consent Order in lieu of an administrative hearing.
By Consent of the parties, this Consent Order is enforceable as a final Order of RIDOH.

Respondent admits that RIDOH has jurisdiction over this matter and agrees to waive further

procedural steps and any right to appeal this Consent Order. Respondent further agrees that RIDOH

may make the Findings of Fact. and Conclusions of Law set forth below.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1, ACE Home Health Aid Training School, LLC (“Respondent”), is an entity registered under the
State of Rhode Island and licensed by the Department as a nursing assistant training program under
the assumed name, “Ace Training School, Inc.,” is the Respondent in this matter.

2. Respondent, ACE Home Health Aid Training School, LLC ((AHHATS”), is a domestic limited
liability company registered with the Rhode Island Department of State, with a business described

on the Rhode Island Secretary of State’s corporate website as a “teaching school for individuals to
become certified nursing assistants.”

. AHHATS is located at 815 Reservoir Avenue, in Cranston, Rhode Island, 02910, and operates
under the alias “ACE Training School, Inc.”

. At all times relevant, Dean DeNuccio (“Mr. DeNuccio”) was the sole owner and President of
AHHATS.

. “ACE Training School, Inc.” is neither a corporate entity nor a DBA (“doing business as”) name
registered with the Rhode Island Department of State,

. On April 21, 2022, RIDOH ostensibly issued a nursing assistant training program (“NATP”)
license (number NATP00150) to “ACE Training School, Inc.” In reality, said license was issued
to Respondent AHHATS.

. At all times relevant, Mr. DeNuccio served as the “contact person” for RIDOH on behalf of
Respondent, which was operating under the alias “Ace Training School, Inc.”

. On or about April 9, 2025, RIDOH received a complaint (the “Complaint”), against AHHATS
(aka “ACE Training School, Inc.”), alleging that the NATP was not providing students with the
required clinical training hours within a nursing home, Additionally, the complaint alleged that a
nursing assistant was acting as the program coordinator for the NATP program.

On April 15, 2025, an unannounced site visit was conducted at 815 Reservoir Avenue in response
to the Complaint against Respondent.!

10. The site visit and investigation revealed the following:

a) That the individual the NATP program identified as its program coordinator denied being
the coordinator at the time of the visit, indicated that they were an instructor, indicated they
had just returned from Florida, and indicated that the program coordinator was another
individual who is licensed as a nursing assistant.

b) The NATP program was not able to produce a detailed course curriculum that met all the
state requirements in accordance with the regulations.

¢) The clinical facility involved in clinical training were unable to verify that all the students
were at the facility identified by the program or that they had completed the required hours of
clinical training identified in the curriculum.

d)The students’ certificates of completion were signed by a registered nurse who indicated they
had no role at the NATP and indicated they were only responsible for the separately licensed

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1}. The conduct described in above paragraphs 10{a)-(g) constitutes violations of Title 216, Chapter
40, Subchapter 05, Part 22, sections 22.7, 22.12, and 22.12.2 of the Rhode Island Administrative
Code, 216-RICR-40-05-22.7 (“Requirements Pertaining to Nursing Assistant Training
Programs”), id. at section 22,12 (“Duties and Training Program Curriculum”), and id. at 22.12.2

(“Curriculum Outline Basic Nursing Assistant Training Program’).
Whereas Respondent wishes to avoid an administrative hearing and agrees to voluntarily enter
into a Consent Order providing for a sanction of two (2) years probation, and whereas RIDOH finds

the within disposition protective of the public health, safety, and welfare, and for good cause shown,

IT IS THEREFORE AGREED AND ORDERED

1. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of two (2) years probation, to begin the date this Consent
Order is executed by all the parties (the “Effective Date”),

2. Prior to restarting classes, Respondent must provide RIDOH with a copy of the following:

a) A signed clinical agreement form within ten (10) business days after the full execution of
this Consent Order; and

3. Respondent must obtain RIDOH’s approval of the signed clinical agreement form identified in
above paragraph 2 prior to restarting classes, and under no circumstances will Respondent restart
classes absent such approval.

As a condition precedent to reopening classes and prior to the execution of this Consent Order
(on June 3, 2025), Respondent submitted to RIDOH a detailed curriculum, which RIDOH has
approved, that will be used in Respondent’s program.

As a condition precedent to reopening classes and prior to the execution of this Consent Order
Responderit submitted resumes to RIDOH (on multiple dates in June 2025) for a program
coordinator and instructors, which RIDOH has approved, subject to the following conditions:

a) Any individual serving as program coordinator in Respondent’s program must be
physically present and residing in Rhode Island.

b) Should any instructor or program coordinator approved by RIDOH leave the program or
otherwise be terminated from the program, Respondent will submit the resume of any
proposed replacement to RIDOH, which RIDOH will either approve or reject within ten
(10) business days following its receipt of said resume from Respondent.

Respondent acknowledges and accepts that, during two (2) year probationary period, it will be
subject to a probation with the following terms:

a) Respondent will hold no more than two (2) simultaneous course sections (such as a
morning and evening section), and Respondent will limit each course section to no more
than twenty-five (25) students, that is, no more than twenty-five (25) students will be
enrolled in the morning or evening section in a class totaling (50) students.;

b) ACE will provide a program that permits all enrolled students to engage and participate
meaningfully, and that trains and prepares students to communicate effectively with
patients;

¢) No clinical hours shall begin prior to week 6 of the NATP program;

d) All clinical hours set forth in the curriculum will be completed in a skilled nursing facility
that is preapproved by RIDOH and will be completed in their entirety;

e) Respondent will notify RIDOH of any staff or facility changes at least 48 business hours
prior to those changes going into effect. In the event of an emergency necessitating that a
change of staff or facility go into effect in less than 48 hours, Respondent will notify
RIDOH of the change immediately (that is, without delay and as soon as reasonably
possible under the circumstances).

f) Seven (7) days prior to the first day of each clinical session, Respondent will provide
RIDOH a list of all clinical groupings, including who is in the group, and where and when
they will be completing their clinical hours;

g) Respondent will maintain individual educational files for each student and graduate. Said
educational files shall include any student’s application, ESL proficiency exam results, all
scored tests or evaluations given during the program period, Bureau of Criminal
Investigation (BCI) and health records required by the clinical facility, and the fully signed
