RHODE ISLAND DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH
DIVISION OF HEALTHCARE QUALITY AND SAFETY
CENTER FOR PROFESSIONAL BOARDS AND LICENSING

IN THE MATTER OF: SHAREASE SMITH
LICENSE NO.: NA37320
COMPLAINT ID #: C25-0966AB

CONSENT ORDER FOR ACTIVE PROBATION

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”), has investigated Complaint Nos.
C25-0996AB regarding allegations of unprofessional conduct in violation of R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-
17.9-1 et seq. against SHAREASE SMITH, nursing assistant license number NA37320
(“Respondent”), and after review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA37320.

2. That on or about July 27, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant by a
staffing agency assigned to provide care to patients at a nursing facility.

3. On July 27, 2025, Respondent was observed utilizing improper feeding techniques while
feeding a patient. On several occasions Respondent spoon fed the patient, turned her back
to the patient to continue speaking on her personal cell phone, and ignored the patient when
the patient began coughing after being fed.

4. The conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct subject
to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.
