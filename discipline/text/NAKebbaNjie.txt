SUMMARY SUSPENSION OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

Kebba Nijie (“Respondent”) has been licensed as a Nursing Assistant pursuant to R. |.
Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. since May 24, 2016. Complaint ID #C23-0010A and
#C23-0010B (the “Complaints”) recently came before the Rhode Island Department of
Health, Division of Customer Services, Center for Professional Boards and Licensing
(“RIDOH") alleging that Respondent had engaged in unprofessional conduct pursuant
to R. |. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. This Summary Suspension is issued related to
the Compiaints and. pursuant to R. |. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14(c). After careful
consideration and further investigation by RIDOH, the following constitute the findings
of fact and conclusions of law with respect to the allegations against Respondent in the
Complaints:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Island under License Number NA50183 (the “License’).

2. That on or about January 4, 2023, and January 6, 2023, RIDOH received notices
that Respondent sexually assaulted a patient on or about January 1, 2023, while
providing care to her.

3. That, at all times during which the incident described in the Paragraph(s) above
took place, Respondent was working at Greenwood Center, 1139 Main Avenue,
Warwick, RI 02886.

ORDER

5. Based on the foregoing and pursuant fo R. |. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq, the
