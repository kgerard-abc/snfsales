CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §$ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4 of
volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with
the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), alleging Caitlin Cooney (the
“Respondent”), a licensed registered nurse (license RN60779), violated Chapter 5-34 of the General
Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a registered nurse licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island under
License Number RN60779.

2. That on or about March 31, 2025, Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) received a
complaint with allegations against the Respondent.

3. The Respondent, then employed at an acute care facility located in Rhode Island, on more than
one occasion and without seeking permission, took medication from a colleague’s pocketbook
for personal use.

4, That on September 8, 2025, an Investigative Committee (IC) of the Board reviewed the
allegations and relevant documents, including the Respondent’s response to the allegation. The
IC made a preliminary determination that the Respondent failed to maintain the standards
established by the nursing profession.

5. That the conduct described in paragraphs three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct as
defined in Rhode Island Gen, Laws § 5-34-24.
