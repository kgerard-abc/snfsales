COMPLIANCE ORDER

SUMMARY SUSPENSION OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

Debra Good (“Respondent”), was licensed as a nursing assistant in Rhode Island on or

about December 17, 2001, pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. This Order is issued
pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14 and 23-1-20.

FINDINGS OF FACT

Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 28896.

At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Lifetime Medical Support
Services as a nursing assistant assigned to the home of S.K.

On or about October 14, 2018, Respondent was arrested and charged with two counts of
felony sexual assault and one count of misdemeanor simple assault or battery by the
Pawtucket, Rhode Island Police Department upon S.K.

Respondent was released from employment by Lifetime Medical Support Services.

On or about January 2, 2019, the Rhode Island Department of Health received a complaint
from the Pretrial Services Unit of the State of Rhode Island Sixth Division District Court
alleging that on January 2, 2019, Respondent stated to court personnel that she had been

on a job interview earlier in the day.

After considering the above facts, the Director of Health finds that public health, safety,
or welfare imperatively requires emergency action.

ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14, Respondent’s nursing assistant license is
SUSPENDED until further order of the department.

A written request for a hearing may be filed with the Director within ten days after
