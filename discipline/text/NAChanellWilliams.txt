CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) has investigated a
complaint charging CHANELL WILLIAMS (Respondent), with conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration by RIDOH
with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. On or about May 24, 2019, Respondent was licensed as nursing assistant, License
NA49751.

2. On or about May 24, 2019, while employed as a nursing assistant at Charlesgate,
Respondent failed to complete her scheduled shift.

3. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
4. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
5. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:
a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;
b. The right to represent herself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s own
choosing at said hearing;
c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf at

said hearing; and
d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED UPON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree as follows:

1. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a reprimand.

This Consent Order shall become a public record.
