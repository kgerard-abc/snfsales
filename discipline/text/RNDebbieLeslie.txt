CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §§ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4 of
volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with
the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Debbie Leslie (the
“Respondent”), a licensed registered nurse (license RN57237), with a violation of Chapter 5-34 of the
General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a registered nurse licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island under
License Number RN57237.

2, That on or about November 4, 2022, the Respondent was employed by a nursing agency and was
assigned to work at a long-term care facility in Rhode Island.

3. That on or about November 7, 2022, Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) received a
complaint alleging inconsistency of documentation and administration of controlled substances
to thréé (3) Separate residents while tinder the Réspondent’s cares ee

4. That on June 12, 2023, the Respondent appeared before an Investigative Committee (IC) of the
Board to respond to these allegations. The Respondent admitted there were times where they
failed to document controlled substance administration.

5. That the conduct described in paragraphs three (3) and four (4) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(6) (iii) and § 5-34-24(6) (v).
