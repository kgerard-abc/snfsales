ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING
RIDOH and Respondent héreby agree to the following disposition of this Complaint:

9. Respondent is héreby issued the sanction of Probation, starting the day this
Consent Order is last executed (the “Effective Date").

10.Commenéing with the Effective Date:

a) Respondent's Nursing Assistant license will be subject to a two (2) year period
of probation and Respondent's license shall reflect the status “Active
Probation.”

b) Respondent must be employed at a hospital or nursing facility.

c} The probationary period will abate for the duration of any period in which
Respondent ceases to be employed as a Nursing Assistant.

d) Respondent will notify RIDOH of any lapse in employment and the probation

completed.

e} Respondent must provide RIDOH with the name and address of any new

f) Respondent shall provide his employer with a copy of this Consent Order and

practice of Nursing Assistants during the period of probation and with the

11.Respondent acknowledges that:

a) this Consent Order is an agreement between Respondent and RIDOH:

disciplinary action at the discretion of RIDOH, including but not limited to
