CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
(“RIDOH”) has investigated Complaint No. C25-1138 (the “Complaint”) charging KATIE
GUTIERREZ-CANTOS, nursing assistant license number NA 45163 (“Respondent”) with
violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. This Consent Order is entered into as a negotiated
resolution of the Complaint and does not constitute a formal hearing determination on the merits.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 45163.

2. That on or about August 30, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at a
nursing facility.

3. On or about August 30, 2025, Respondent had a verbal interaction with a resident.
RIDOH alleges that Respondent used inappropriate language.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three constitutes unprofessional conduct
pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve the
Complaint.

That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter,

The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s own
choosing at said hearing;

The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent’s behalf;

The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained herein,

The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

Any objection that this Consent Order will (i) be reported to the National Practitioner
Data Bank, (ii) become a permanent part of Respondent’s record maintained by RIDOH
and a part of the public record of this proceeding, or (iii) be posted on RIDOH’s public

website.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this Complaint:

Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, effective the day this Consent Order is
last executed, as a negotiated resolution of the Complaint without a formal administrative

hearing determination.
