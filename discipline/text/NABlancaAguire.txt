CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode lskand Department of Health
("RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C23-1139 (the “Complaint”) charging
BLANCA AGUIRE , nursing assistant license number NA 56615 (“Respondent”) with
violations of R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 56615.

2. That on or about October 26, 2023, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant by a staffing agency and was assigned fo work at the home of a patient.

3. That on or about October 26, 2023, Respondent arrived at the home of fhe
patient, who informed her that he had cancelled her services for the day and
sent her home. However, Respondent clocked in at 1:30 p.m. and clocked out at
4:30 p.m., and as if she had worked for the patient, thus committing fraud.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant fo R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
