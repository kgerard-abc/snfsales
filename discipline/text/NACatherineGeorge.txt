CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
CATHERINE GEORGE, NA18706 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to
disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R.. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration
by RIDOH and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall
constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode
Island under Nursing Assistant License NA18706.

2. On or about June 16, 2017, Respondent entered a plea of nolo contendere to one count of

Medicaid Fraud, a felony, in the matter of State of Rhode Island v. Catherine George, P2-2017-
0682A.

3. A felony conviction as described above constitutes conduct subject to disciplinary proceedings
pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

4. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

5. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall become

binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
6. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;
b. The right to represent herself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s own
choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and
d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this

complaint:

L. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a revocation of her Nursing Assistant
license, NA18706.
