SUMMARY SUSPENSION OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

Jason Robert Silva (hereinafter “Respondent”) is licensed as a nursing assistant
pursuant to R.J. Gen. Laws chapter 23-17.9 and holds license number NA33440. This
complaint came before the Department of Health, Board of Nursing Assistants .
(hereinafter "Department") upon allegations that Respondent engaged in conduct that
constituted grounds for disciplinary action against his nursing assistant license pursuant
to Rhode Island General Laws § 23-17.9-8(1) and the Rules and Regulations Pertaining
to Rhode Island Certificates of Registration for Nursing Assistants, Medication Aides,
and the Approval of Nursing Assistant and Medication Aide Training Programs Rule
6.1(a). After careful consideration and further investigation by the Department, the
following constitutes the Findings of Fact with respect to the actions of the Respondent

relative to this complaint:

Respondent was arrested on charges of downloading child pornography
after an investigation by the Rhode Island Internet Crimes Against
Children Task Force learned that a computer used by Respondent was on a
peer-to-peer file-sharing files of suspected child pornography. An
investigator viewed one of the several files and determined that its content
was consistent with the Rhode Island statutory definition of child
pormography. The investigator traced the IP address of the computer to
Respondent’s home, and then executed a search warrant. Respondent
waived his Miranda rights and admitted to downloading child
pomography via the internet. His computer contained child pornography
files. He was arrested and released on bail, with a condition that he not
have contact with children and not use the internet.

The above facts, if proven at hearing, constitute violations of Rhode Island
General Laws § 23-17.9-8(1) and the Rules and Regulations Pertaining to
Rhode Island Certificates of Registration for Nursing Assistants,
Medication Aides, and the Approval of Nursing Assistant and Medication
Aide Training Programs Rule 6.1 (a).

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 42-35-14(c) and 23-17.9-7 and -8(1) and
the Rules and Regulations Pertaining to Rhode Island Certificates of

Registration for Nursing Assistants, Medication Aides, and the Approval
