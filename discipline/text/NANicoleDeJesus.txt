Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations

Health (“RIDOH”") has investigated Complaint No. C23-1138 (the “Complaint”)

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent was a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of
Rhode Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 45572.

2. That on or about November 1, 2023, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant by a staffing agency and was assigned to work at the home of a patient.

3. That on or about November 7, 2023, Respondent arrived at the home of the
patient as scheduled, and left the home of the patient at 1:00 p.m., but clocked

out at 3:30 p.m., thus committing fraud.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules

and Regulations promulgated there under.
