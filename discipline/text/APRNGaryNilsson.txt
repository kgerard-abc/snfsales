CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §§ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4 of
volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with
the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Gary Nilsson (the
“Respondent”), a licensed advanced practice registered nurse (license APRN00863), with a violation
of Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of the Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is an advanced practice registered nurse licensed to practice in the State of
Rhode Island under License Number APRNO00863.

2. That on or about September 26, 2023, the Respondent was employed at an ambulatory surgical
center located in Rhode Island.

3. That on or about October 4, 2023, Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) received a
complaint alleging that the Respondent, during a state survey, was witnessed not performing
hand hygiene consistent with the facility policy and standard precautions as the Respondent
failed to wash their hands and wear gloves on multiple occasions.

4. That on May 13, 2024, an Investigative Committee (IC) of the Board reviewed the allegations
and relevant evidence including the Respondent’s response to the allegation and the
recommendation of the Advanced Practice Nurse Advisory Committee. The IC made a
preliminary determination that the Respondent negligently, willfully, or intentionally acted in a
manner inconsistent with the health and safety of person entrusted to their care by their actions.

5, That the conduct described in paragraphs three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct as
defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-40(2)(i)(C).
