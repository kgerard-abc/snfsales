CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §§ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4 of
volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with
the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Michelle Conti (the
“Respondent”), a licensed registered nurse (license RN54939), with a violation of Chapter 5-34 of
the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a registered nurse licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island under
License Number RN54939.

2. That on or about December 1, 2022, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) received a |
complaint alleging that the Respondent, who was working in an acute care facility, did not
properly document medication administration including controlled substances.

3. In response to this incident, the facility conducted an audit of the Respondent’s Pyxis machine

transactions and the Respondent’s documentation of medication administration which included
controlled substances from December 2021 through November 15, 2022.

4. That on January 22, 2024, an Investigative Committee (IC) of the Board reviewed the allegations
and relevant evidence. The IC made a preliminary determination that Respondent violated the
standards of nursing practice.

AGREEMENT:
The Board and Respondent (together, the “Parties”) hereby agree as follows:

1. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Board and hereby agrees to remain under the
jurisdiction of the Board.

Consent Order and the contents herein are not binding on Respondent until final ratification by
the Board.
