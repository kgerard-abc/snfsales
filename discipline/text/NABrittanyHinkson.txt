CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, “"RIDOH”) have investigated Complaint No. C20-0523
{the “Complaint") charging Brittany Hinkson, Nursing Assistant license number NA41143,
(“Respondent”) with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

—

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island

under Nursing Assistant License Number NA41143.

That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by the Kent Regency
Center.

That on or about April 8, 2020, Respondent did arrive for her shift at the facility, did receive
her shift assignment and then left the building and did not return, thereby abandoning
her patients.

That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve the
Complaint.
