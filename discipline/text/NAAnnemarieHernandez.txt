CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to Rl. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rnode Island Department of Health
(“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0926 (the “Complaint”) charging
ANNAMARIE HERNANDEZ, nursing assistant license number NA 53573 ("Respondent")
with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1, That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rnode
island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 53573.

2. That on or about August 21, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant by a staffing agency and was assigned to work at the home of a patient.

3. That on or about August 21, 2024, Respondent failed to appear at the home of a
patient at 4:30 PM as scheduled, yet clocked in as if she had arrived at the
patient's home at 4:27 PM, when in fact she did not arrive at the patient's home
until approximately 5:30 PM, thus committing fraud.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant fo R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

5, That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
