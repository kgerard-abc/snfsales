VOLUNTARY SURRENDER

Kayleigh Richard f/k/a Kayleigh Beale a/k/a Kayleigh Bellisle RN (hereinafter “Respondent”) is licensed as a
registered nurse in Rhode Island pursuant to R.I. General Laws § 5-34-11 and holds RI license number
RN49634. Respondent notified the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (hereinafter “Board”)
that she wishes to surrender her license and not practice nursing for a period of time to attend to her medical
issues.

ORDER
The parties agree as follows:

1. That Respondent voluntary surrenders her registered nurse license number RN49634.
2. That at the time that Respondent seeks reinstatement of her registered nurse license,

she shall submit documentation to the Board evidencing that she has successfully
addressed her medical issues.

3. That the surrender of Respondent’s license to practice nursing is accepted and shall
remain in full force and effect until past agreed upon requirements of the Board have
been completed and reviewed by the Board to the Board’s satisfaction. The terms of
reinstatement shall include but not be limited to fitness for duty from a Board of
Nursing approved provider, and appearance before the Board of Nursing committee
assigned to this matter. Upon completion of these conditions will her request for
reinstatement be heard by the Board.
