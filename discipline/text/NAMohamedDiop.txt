SUMMARY SUSPENSION OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

Mohamed Diop (“Respondent”), was licensed as a nursing assistant in Rhode Island on or
about May 30, 2008, pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. This Order is issued pursuant
to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14. After careful consideration and further investigation by the Rhode
Island Department of Health, the following constitute the findings of fact and conclusions of law
with respect to the allegations against Respondent:

FINDINGS OF FACT

. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing Assistant
License NA 38050.

. Atall timed relevant hereto, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at Trinity Health and
Rehabilitation Center, Woonsocket, Rhode Island.

. On or about August 8, 2023, Respondent was arrested and charged with first degree sexual assault
upon J.C., a patient in his care in the course of his employment as a nursing assistant at Trinity
Health and Rehabilitation Center, Rhode Island Judiciary Case Number 62-2023-06455.

. Respondent was held at the Adult Correctional Institute, Cranston, Rhode Island, from August 9,
2023, until September 11, 2023, when he was released on home confinement.

. Individuals released on home confinement are permitted to leave the home for the purpose of going
to work.

Should Respondent return to work as a nursing assistant, the health, safety, and welfare of patients
in his care may be threatened.
7. After considering the above facts, the Director of Health finds that public health, safety, or
welfare imperatively requires emergency action.

ORDER

1. Based on the foregoing and pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws§ 42-35-14, Respondent’s nursing
assistant license is SUSPENDED until further order of the Department of Health.

2. A written request for a hearing on the summary suspension may be filed with the Interim
Director within ten days after service of this Notice. Questions may be directed to Linda Esposito at
