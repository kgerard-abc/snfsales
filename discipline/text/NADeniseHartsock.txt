CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RJ. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the
Nursing Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging
DENISE HARTSOCK, NA34932 (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to
disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration
by RIDOH and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall
constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA34932.
2. Atall times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Lifetime Medical.

3. On or about January 2, 2018, it came to the attention of Respondent’s employer that
Respondent had been providing prescribed medications to a client in her care and had
taken verbal medication orders from a physician, without the proper license to do so.

Respondent also committed a medication error.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall

become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

4,

The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and

Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this
complaint:

Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand on her Nursing
Assistant license, NA34932.

If Respondent is currently employed, she must notify the Board, in writing, of the
identity of her current employer. Respondent is required to participate in in-
service trainings on Nursing Assistant duties through her identified employer
within thirty (30) days of the ratification of this Consent Order and must provide
the Board with proof of such completion.

If Respondent is not currently employed, she must notify the Board, in writing,
within five (5) days of the date that she secures employment, and must also
provide the identity of her employer. Respondent is required to participate in in-
service trainings on Nursing Assistant duties through her. identified employer
within thirty (30) days of her original employment date, and must provide the
Board with proof of such completion.

This Consent Order shall become a public record.
