(“Respondent”), was licensed as a nursing assistant in Rhode Island on or about August

FINDINGS OF FACT

l. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice i in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA50450.

De At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Home Care Network.

3. On or about September 6, 2020, Respondent was providing in-home care to a
developmentally disabled, non-verbal child.

4, On or about September 6, 2020, the mother of the disabled child reviewed video taken by
a camera in the child’s bedroom. The video showed Respondent hitting the child with a
strap or cord and placing her hand on the back of the child’s neck and pushing her onto a

bed.
5: The conduct described in paragraph four constitutes unprofessional conduct subject to
disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules — and

Regulations promulgated thereunder.

6. After considering the above facts, the Director of Health finds that public health, safety, or
welfare imperatively requires emergency action.

ORDER

ls Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14, Respondent’s nursing assistant license is
SUSPENDED until further order of the department.
