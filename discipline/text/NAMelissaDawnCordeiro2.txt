                         ORDER OF SUMMARY SUSPENSION
                          OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

        MELISSA CORDEIRO, ("Respondent"), is licensed as a nursing assistant in Rhode
Island under license number NA 48239. This Order is issued pursuant to R.l. Gen. Laws § 42-35-
14.

                  FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

   1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
      Assistant License NA 48239 with a status ofActive Probation pursuant to a Consent Order
      ratified on August 24, 2023.

   2. Under the terms of the Consent Order, Respondent must be employed at a hospital or
      nursing facility and may not be employed by an agency that provides home care.
      Respondent is currently employed at a nursing facility. The underlying factual basis for
      this Consent Order was the Respondent's development of an inappropriate relationship
      with a patient.

   3. Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at a nursing facility where A.G. was a
      patient from the fall of2022 through July 2023. Prior to A.G.'s discharge from the nursing
      facility, Respondent established an inappropriate personal relationship with A.G. which
      ultimately led to Respondent moving into A.G.' s home in South Kingstown, Rhode Island
      in August 2023 and establishing herself as A.G. 's caretaker and friend.

   4. On or about September 2023 A.G. moved to a rehabilitation center where she remained
      until April of2024 when Respondent removed A.G. from the facility and took her back to
      the South Kingstown home, where Respondent and A.G. continued to reside.

   5. On or about May 26, 2024, a relative of A.G. with ownership rights in the property
      contacted the South Kingstown police who responded to the property and issued a no
      trespassing order to the Respondent for the property.
