CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health (“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C25-0244 (the “Complaint”)
charging SANDY CUELLAR, nursing assistant license number NA 60055
(‘Respondent"), with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of
Rhode Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 60055.

. That on or about February 27, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant at a nursing facility.

. That on or about February 27, 2025, a resident at the facility was found in her
bed with a sheet over her waist, seemingly place this way to prevent her from
getting out of bed; the sheet was loosely tied with each end tied to each (left
and right) siderail, and Respondent admitted that she had tied the sheet this
way to protect the resident from getting up from bed and falling.

. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.i. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the
Rules and Regulations promulgated there under.

. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
