CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, “RIDOH") have investigated Complaint No, C22-0226
(the “Complaint") charging Alejandra Sofia Barrientos, Nursing Assistant license number
NA50231, ("Respondent") with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Nursing Assistant License Number NA50231.

2, That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Oak Hill Nursing and
Rehabilitation Center, 544 Pleasant Street, Pawtucket, Ri 02860,

3. That on or about February 15, 2022, Respondent was found in possession of medication
that had been prescribed to a resident who had been discharged in December 2021.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.I, Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5, That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve the
Complaint.

8. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a) The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter,

b) The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent's
own choosing at said hearing;

c) The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent's behalf;

d} The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e}) The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained herein;

f) The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

g) Any objection that this Consent Order will be reported to the National Practitioner
Data Bank as well as posted on RIDOH's public website.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING
RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this Complaint:

9. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, starting the day this Consent
Order is last executed,

10. Respondent acknowledges that this Consent Order is an agreement between
Respondent and RIDOH.

11. This Consent Order shall become a permanent part of Respondent's record maintained
by RIDOH and a part of a public record of this proceeding.
