CONSENT ORDER
VOLUNTARY SURRENDER .

Kristen Hunt Flood RN (hereinafter “Respondent”) is licensed as a registered nurse in Rhode Island
pursuant to R.I. General Laws § 5-34-11 and holds license number RN36953. Respondent notified the Board of
Nursé Registration and Nursing Education (hereinafter “Board”) that she wishes to surrender her license and not

practice nursing for a period of time to attend to her medical issues.
ORDER

The parties agree as follows:

(a) That Respondent agrees to the voluntary surrender of her registered nurse license.

(b) That Respondent shall not seek reinstatement for a minimum of six (6) months. At
the time that Respondent seeks reinstatement of her registered nurse license, she
shall submit documentation to the Board evidencing that she is capable and
competent! to engage in the practice of nursing, including documentation from a
Board approved, health care provider indicating consistent participation in
treatment.

(c) That the surrender of Respondent’s license to practice nursing is accepted and shall

remain in full force and effect until further order of the Board.
