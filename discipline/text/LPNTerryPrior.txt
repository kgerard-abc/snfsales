CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and the regulations promulgated
thereunder, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with the Board of Nurse Registration and
Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Terry Prior, LPN09945 (“Respondent”) with a
violation of Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by an Investigating Committee of the Board with respect
to the allegations of unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the Board has made the following

| FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW:

1. Respondent is a licensed practical nurse licensed to practice nursing in the State of Rhode
Island under practical nurse License Number LPN 09945.

2. At all pertinent times, Respondent was employed at Westerly Health Center, in Westerly,
Rhode Island. . .

3. On December 25, 2015, Respondent engaged in inappropriate conversation with a
resident in her care.

4. The conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct as
defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws | § 5-34-24(6)(v) and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder.

AGREEMENT:

The Board and Respondent (together, the “Parties”) hereby agree as follows:

1. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Board and hereby agrees to remain under the
jurisdiction of the Board.
