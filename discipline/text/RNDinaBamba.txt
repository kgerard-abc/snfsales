CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and the regulations promulgated
thereunder, a complaint (the “Complaint’”’) was filed with the Board of Nurse Registration and
Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Dina Bamba (“Respondent”), a licensed registered
nurse (license number #RN45197), with a violation of Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the
State of Rhode Island. |

After review and consideration by an Investigating Committee of the Board with respect
to the allegations of unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the Board has made the following

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW:
1. Respondent is licensed to practice as a registered nurse in the State of Rhode Island under
license #RN45197.
2. At all pertinent times, Respondent was employed at Pawtucket Skilled Nursing and

Rehabilitation in Pawtucket, Rhode Island.

3. On or about April 4, 2015, a resident in Respondent’s care developed respiratory distress.

4, Following the decline in her patient’s respiratory status, Respondent left the facility
without notifying her supervisor or reporting off to another nurse.

5. When Respondent left the facility, she took with her the keys to the facility’s medication
cart.

6. Respondent remained out of the facility for two hours during her shift.

7. Respondent failed to punch back in when she returned to the facility.

8. The conduct described in paragraphs four (4) and six (6) herein constitutes unprofessional

conduct as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(6)(i), (“abandonment of a

patient”).

9. The conduct described in paragraph seven (7) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct
as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(6)(i), (willfully making and filing
false reports or records in the practice of nursing”).

10. The conduct described in paragraph five (5) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct as
defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(6)(v), (“willful disregard of standards of
nursing practice and failure to maintain standards established by the nursing profession”).
