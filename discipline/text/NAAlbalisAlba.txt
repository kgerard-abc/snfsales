CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (hereinafter “Board”) have investigated a complaint charging ALBALIS
ALBA (hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to
R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration by RIDOH and the Board with respect
to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA44580.

2. Atall times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant by Morning
Star Home, LLC, 349 Centerville Road, Warwick, RI.

3. On or about January 11, 2018, Respondent signed a statement in which she admitted that
she utilized an ATM card that belonged to a patient in her care, and, without the patient’s
knowledge or consent, made withdrawals from the patient’s bank account in the amount of
$1,070.00.

4. The conduct described in paragraph 3 constitutes unprofessional conduct pursuant to
RIGL 23-17.9-8(1) and (5), and § 6.1(a) and (e) of the Rules and Regulations Pertaining
to Rhode Island Certificates of Registration for Nursing Assistants, Medication Aides and
the Approval of Nursing Assistant and Medication Aide Training Programs.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall

become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
