CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §§ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4
of volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a complaint (the "Complaint") was filed
with the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the "Board"), charging Amelinda
Dubois (the "Respondent"), a licensed registered nurse (license RN38934), with a violation of
Chapter 5-34 of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF
LAW

1. That Respondent is a registered nurse licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under License Number RN38934.

2. That on or about November 19, 2024, Respondent was employed at an acute care facility
located in Rhode Island.

3. That on or about November 21, 2024, Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH)
received a complaint alleging that the Respondent had improperly transcribed an order and
entered it in a patient's medical record.

4. That on or about November 19, 2024, while participating in the readmission of a resident to
the facility, Respondent made an error related to transcribing and documenting a
medication order into the patient's medical record.

5. That the conduct described in paragraphs three (3) and four (4) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws§ 5-34-24 (6)(v).
AGREEMENT:
The Board and Respondent (together, the "Parties") hereby agree as follows:

1. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Board and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of the Board.

2. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that it is a proposal of an
Investigative Committee of the Board and is subject to final ratification by the Board.
This Consent Order and the contents herein are not binding on Respondent until final
ratification by the Board.

3. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a) The right to appear personally or by counsel before the Board;

b) The right to produce witnesses and evidence on their behalf at a hearing;

c) The right to cross-examine witnesses;

d) The right to have subpoenas issued by the Board;

e) The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained herein;

f) Any and all right of appeal of this Consent Order;

g) Any objection to the fact that this Consent Order will be presented to the Board
for consideration and review;

h) Any objection to the fact that it will be necessary for the Board to become acquainted
with all evidence pertaining to this matter in order to adequately review this Consent
Order;

i) Any objection to the fact that the Board reviewing this Consent Order may be the same as
the hearing committee presiding over this matter should it later be brought to an
administrative hearing.

j) Any objection to the fact that potential bias against the Respondent may occur as a
result of the presentation of this Consent Order to the Board.

4. This Consent Order shall become part of the public record of this proceeding once it is
accepted by all parties and accepted by the Board.

5. Respondent's acceptance of this Consent Order constitutes an admission by Respondent that
the Board's factual findings and legal conclusions, as contained herein, are accurate and
correct.

6. Respondent agrees to accept the sanction of an eighteen (18) month suspension,
retroactive to January 17, 2025, to be followed by a period of probation not to exceed
eighteen (18) months.

7. Atthe conclusion of the period of suspension, Respondent shall appear before the Board,
at which time it will decide on an appropriate period of probation, not to exceed eighteen
(18) months.
8. The terms of the probation shall include quarterly consultation between Respondent’s supervisor
and a Board representative. Respondent agrees to allow any employer to discuss job
performance with such representative.

9. During the probationary period, Respondent must notify the Department of Health of the name,

location, and supervisor contact information for each employer where Respondent is employed
as a nurse.

10. That should Respondent fail to comply with the terms of this Consent Order, or the laws and
regulations governing the practice of nursing, their license as a registered nurse may be
subject to other appropriate disciplinary action.

DISPOSITION OF THE COMPLAINT:

Accordingly, and based on the foregoing, the Parties hereby agree to the following disposition of
the Complaint:

1. That Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a period of suspension and probation.

2. That this Consent Order shall become part of a public record of this proceeding.
