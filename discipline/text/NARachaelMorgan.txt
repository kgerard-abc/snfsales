CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RIGL 23-17.9 and the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under, the
Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) and the Nursing Assistant Advisory
Board, have investigated an allegation of unprofessional conduct in violation Rhode
island General Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. against Rachael Morgan, NA44994
(“Respondent”).

After review and consideration by RIDOH and the Nursing Assistant Advisory Board, the
following are and shall constitute
FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Nursing Assistant License Number, NA44994.

2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Riverview Healthcare,
Coventry, RI.

3. That on or about November 2, 2016, Respondent was given direction to use the EZ-
Stand to assist a resident transfer out of bed. The Respondent chose not to use it
and the resident sustained an injury.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
there under.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That the Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent
Order shall become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That the Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order js solely to
resolve the complaint of unprofessional conduct alleged against the Respondent.

8. That the Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a) The right to have an Administrative Hearing on this matter:

b) The right to represent himself/herself or be represented by an Attorney of
Respondent's own choosing at said hearing;

Cc) The right to present testimony, evidence, and witnesses on Respondent's
behalf;

d) The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e) The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically
contained herein;

f) The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

The Rhode Island Department of Health and the Respondent hereby agree to the
following disposition of this complaint: |

1. That Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand.
2. That Respondent shail take one (1) course on Patient Safety.

3. That this Order shall become part of a public record of this proceeding.
