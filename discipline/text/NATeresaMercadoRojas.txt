FINDINGS OF FACT

Respondent is a Nursing Assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 56507 (the “License”).

At all relevant times, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at Joy Home Care,
Inc. (“Joy Home Care”), located at 845 Allens Ave., Providence, Rhode Island, 02905,
and charged with providing a client (“Client A”) with home care services.

At all relevant times, Respondent was scheduled to provide Client A care on weekdays,
Monday through Friday, 6:00AM to 8:00AM and 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

Client A is a young adult with a developmental disability and has bowel and bladder
incontinence. At all relevant times, Client A required total assistance with all activities of
daily living (ADLs) and Instrumental Activities of Daily Living (IADLs).

As Client A’s assigned nursing assistant, Respondent was responsible for maintaining
Client’s A’s activities of daily living, including bathing/grooming, ensuring a clean
environment (including tasks such as changing bed linens, garbage disposal, and
laundry), and incontinence care.

Two unannounced site visits of Client A’s home were conducted on Monday, March 17,
2025.

Following said unannounced site visits, it was alleged that Respondent failed to provide
Client A with a clean and sanitary environment and living conditions.

It was further alleged that Respondent had been keeping Client A in a small and dark
room, locked from the outside so that Client was not free to leave.

It was alleged that Client A’s room was filled with storage boxes, had no working light
fixture, and was furnished with a bare twin mattress on the floor.

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

The conduct alleged in above paragraph 8, if proved, would constitute “abuse” within the
meaning of 216-RICR-40-05-22.3(A)(1) (“any conduct which harms or is likely to harm a
patient”).

The conduct alleged in above paragraphs 7-9, if proved, would constitute grounds for
discipline under the following provisions:

a. Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8(2) (“the nursing assistant has violated any of
the provisions of this chapter or the rules enacted in accordance with this
chapter; or acted in a manner inconsistent with the health and safety of the
patients of the home in which he or she is providing nursing assistant services”);
