CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RL. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 er seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated

thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) and NICOLE DELGADO
(Respondent) enter into this Consent Order.

FINDINGS OF FACT

. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License Number NA34913.
On various dates between November 2017 and January 2019, Respondent engaged in
unprofessional conduct by maintaining a friendship with and accepting cash and gifts from
an individual for whom she had provided care.
The conduct described in paragraph two constitutes conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8(3) and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder.
Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:
a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;
b, The right to represent herself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s own
choosing at said hearing;
c, The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf at
said hearing; and
d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED UPON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree as follows:
1. Respondent will be on probation for a period of two years from the date of ratification of
this Consent Order and shall ensure that her employer submits quarterly Job Performance
Progress Reports to RIDOH.

2. Respondent must work under direct supervision of a licensed nurse in a facility and may
not work under indirect supervision in a private home during the probationary period,

3, Respondent must take 3 continuing education units on Ethics and 3 continuing education
units on Boundary Issues, and submit proof thereof, within 30 days of the date of
ratification of this Consent Order. Online continuing education units are acceptable. All
documentation of continuing education for proof of compliance must be submitted to
DOH, PRCompliance@health.ri.gov

4, Failure to comply with the requirements of paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 above may result in
additional disciplinary action.

This Consent Order shall become a public record.
