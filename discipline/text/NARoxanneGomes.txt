CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
(“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C24-0300(the “Complaint”) charging
ROXANNE GOMES, nursing assistant license number NA 50865 (“Respondent”), with
violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rnode
Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 50865.

2. That on or about March 21, 2024, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant
at a nursing facility.

3, That on or about March 21, 2024, Respondent clocked in at the facility at 8:18
a.m. for a scheduled shift that began at 8:00 a.m. and ended at 3:00 p.m., and
clocked out at 9:51 a.m. and left the facility, thus abandoning residents of the
facility in her care.

4, The Respondent contends that she left the facility because she had been
assigned to take care of 15 residents on one hallway by herself when two other
hallways of 11-12 residents each had two nursing assistants assigned to each
hallway, which she felt was unfair and unsafe because there was not enough time
in the day for her to attend to the needs of 15 residents. In addition, she had never
taken care of these residents before and was not provided with any reports
regarding their needs from the nursing staff before starting work on the hallway.
As a result of being placed in this stressful situation, Respondent suffered a panic
attack and was unable to complete her shift. Respondent immediately sought
treatment for the panic attack and has successfully responded to treatment.

5. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.

6. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

7. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent
Order shall become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

8, That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to
resolve the Complaint.

9. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a} The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b} The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of
Respondent's own choosing at said hearing;

c) The right to present testimony, evidence, and witnesses on Respondent's
behalt:;

d) The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e) The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained
herein;

f} The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

g) Any objection that this Consent Order will (i) be reported to the National
Practitioner Data Bank, {ii} become a permanent part of Respondent's
record maintained by RIDOH and a part of the public record of this
proceeding, or {ili} be posted on RIDOH’s public website.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING
RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this Complaint:

9. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, effective on the day
this Consent Order is last executed.

10. Respondent acknowledges that this Consent Order is an agreement between
Respondent and RIDOH.
