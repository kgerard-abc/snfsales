CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to 23-17.9 and the Rules and Regulations promutgated there under, the Rhode
Island Department of Health (hereinafter the “Department’), and the Nursing Assistant
Advisory Board (hereinafter the “Board”), has investigated a complaint charging ADAMA
FALL, NA37784 (hereinafter the “Respondent’) with a violation of 23-17.9,

After review and consideration by the Department and the Board with respect to the
allegations of unprofessional conduct of the Respondeni, the following are and shall
constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the
State of Rhode Island under Nursing AssistantLicense Number,
NA37784.

2. Atall times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by The
Highlands on the East Side.

3. The Department has alleged that on or about October 14, 2015,
Respondent did not act in the best interest of a patient by
refusing to get assistance for a patient found on the floor.

4, That the foregoing allegations of the Department, described In
paragraph three (3) herein, if proven, constitute unprofessional
conduct pursuant to 23-17.9 and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5. That the Department and the Respondent mutually agree to
enter into this consent order in the matter of C75-1040 to resolve
this complaint to avoid a contested hearing.

6, That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Department
and hereby agrees to remain under the jurisdiction of the
Department.

7. That the Respondent has read this Consent Order and
understands this Consent Order shall become binding once
signed by the Respondent and the Department and/or Board.

8. That the Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent
Order ts solely to resolve the complaint of unprofessional
conduct alleged against the Respondent.

9, That the Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to have an Administrative Hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself/herself or be represented by an Attorney of
Respondent's own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent's
behalf:

d. The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by the Department;

a. The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically
contained herein;

f. The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order.

ACCORDINGLY AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

The Rhode {sland Department of Health and the Respondent hereby agree to the

1. That Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand,
