Pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health ("RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C23-0636AB (the “Complaint”)
charging SOLANGE FERNANDES Nursing Assistant license number NA 50822,
("Respondent") with violations of R.]. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute,

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of
Rhode Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 50822.

That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Bayberry
Commons, Burrillville, Rnode Island

On June 29, 2023, a video surveillance camera in a common area of
Bayberry Commons captured footage that shows Respondent entered
and sat down in the common area. A resident who had a walker next to
her chair was attempting to walk without her walker, for a period of one
minute and 23 seconds. The Respondent neglected to assist the resident
until the resident attempted to sit down in her chair, fell backwards, hit her
head on the chair and landed on the floor.
