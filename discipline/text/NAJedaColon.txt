CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health (“RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C23-0932C (the “Complaint")
charging JEDA COLON , Nursing Assistant license number NA 35291-1,
("Respondent") with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect fo the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute,

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent was licensed by the State of Rhode Island as a Nursing
Assistant with a 120 day permit from February 7, 2023 up to and including
June 7, 2023 under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 35296-T.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed as a nursing
assistant by Grace Barker Health, Warren, Rhode Island.

3. Onor about September 26, 2023, the Rhode Island Department of Health
determined that Respondent was employed by Grace Barker Health as a
nursing assistant after her temporary nursing assistant license had expired,
and was working as a nursing assistant without a license.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and
the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this

Complaint:

9, Respondent agrees to a finding that she engaged in unprofessional
conduct because she continued to work as a nursing assistant after the
expiration of her temporary license.

10.Respondent acknowledges that this Consent Order is an agreement
between Respondent and RIDOH.

11. This Consent Order shall become a permanent part of Respondent's

proceeding.
