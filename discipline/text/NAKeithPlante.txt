CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to 23-17.9 and the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under, the Rhode
Island Department of Health (hereinafter the “Department”), and the Nursing Assistant
Advisory Board (hereinafter the “Board”), has investigated a complaint charging KEITH A
PLANTE, NA11706 (hereinafter the “Respondent’) with a violation of RIGL 23-17.9.

After review and consideration by the Department and the Board with respect to the
allegations of unprofessional conduct of the Respondent, the following are and shall
constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed io practice in the
State of Rhode Island under Nursing AssistantLicense Number,
NA11706.

2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by
Landmark Medica! Center, Woonsocket, RI.

3. That on or about February 22, 2015, Respondent used
inappropriate physical force while attempting to prevent an -
intoxicated, combative patient, who was an elopement risk, from
leaving the emergency department at Landmark Medical Center.

4. While the patient did not suffer any additional injury as a result,
the conduct described in paragraph (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to RIGL 23-17.9 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of the Department
and hereby agrees to remain under the jurisdiction of the
Departmeni.

6. That the Respondent has read this Consent Order and
understands this Consent Order shall become binding once
signed by the Respondent and the Department and/or Board.

7. That the Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent
Order is solely to resolve the complaint of unprofessional
conduct alleged against the Respondent.

8. That the Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to have an Administrative Hearing on this matter:

b. The right to represent himself/herself or be represented by an Attorney of
Respondent’s own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent's
behalf;

d. The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by the Department;

e. The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically
contained herein;

f. The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order.

ACCORDINGLY AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

The Rhode Isiand Department of Health and the Respondent hereby agree to the
following disposition of this complaint:

1. That Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand
and must provide proof of completion of a course in patient rights,
(go to, htto://www.rn.org/Courseinfo.php?course id+42 The name
of the course is, Legal: The Rights of Patients), and an anger

care/anger-management/ The name of the course is, Preventive
Care: Anger Management).

2. That this Order shall become part of a public record of this

proceeding.
