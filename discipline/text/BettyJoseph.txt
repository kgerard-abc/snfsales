IMMEDIATE COMPLIANCE ORDER TO CEASE AND DESIST OPERATIONS

Now comes the Director of Health (the “Director”) of the Rhode Island Department of

Health (“RIDOH”) who, pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §§ 5-34-1 et seq., § 23-1-21, and 216-RICR-

40-05-3.3, makes the following findings and enters this Order to Cease and Desist Operations (this

“Order”):

Findings of Fact and Conclusions of Law:

1.

That Betty Joseph (“Respondent”) is not licensed in the state of Rhode Island as a
professional nurse.

That on or about May 17, 2024, a complaint submitted to RIDOH stated that Respondent
worked unsupervised as a nurse at a skilled nursing facility located in Rhode Island on
May 4, 2024, into May 5, 2024.

. The Respondent made entries in thirty-four (34) residents’ electronic medical records

and documented that Respondent administered medications and/or treatments to five (5)
residents with a feeding tube or tracheostomy.

That R. I. Gen. Laws § 5- 34-2 requires any person who practices as a registered nurse in
Rhode Island to first obtain a license (i.e., to be a duly licensed registered nurse pursuant to
Rhode Island law).

That actions of Respondent described above. in: n: paragraphs 2 and 3 violate the laws cited
above in paragraph 4. . _

Accordingly, Based Upon the Foregoing:

A. Respondent shall immediately cease and desist from performing any activity that

(further) violates R. I. Gen. Laws §§ 5-34-1 e¢ seg., including, but not limited to,
engaging in the unlicensed practice of a registered nurse resulting in contravention of R.
I. Gen. Laws § 5- 34-2, and performing the duties of, and/or holding themselves out as a
registered nurse in the State of Rhode Island.

B. If Respondent fails to immediately cease and desist operations as directed by this Order,
RIDOH may take any additional enforcement action against Respondent not otherwise
precluded by law which could include fines and/or imprisonment pursuant to R. I. Gen.
Laws § 23-1-25,

C. Notwithstanding any further findings, actions, or sanctions by RIDOH, this Order shall
remain in full force and effect until further notice.
An administrative hearing may be requested within 15 days of service of this Immediate
Order, by writing to the Director pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §§ 23-1-1 et seg. If no hearing
request is made within 15 days of this Order, the Order shall not be reviewable by any court or

agency.
