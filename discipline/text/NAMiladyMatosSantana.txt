CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department
of Health has investigated this Complaint charging Respondent, Milady R.
Matos Santana, with a violation of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct, the following are and shall constitute,

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is licensed to practice in Rhode Island under
Nursing Assistant License NA54877.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by
Health Care Services, 400 Reservoir Avenue, Providence, RI 02907.

3. That on or about February 15, 2023, and April 14, 2023,
Respondent clocked in and out of her work shift when a client
assigned to her care was not at home, thereby committing fraud.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein
constitutes Unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-
17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated there
under,
5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby
agrees to remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands
this Consent Order shall become binding once signed by
Respondent dnd RIDOH.

7. That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order
is solely to resolve the Complaint.

8. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a) The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b) The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney
of Respondent's own choosing at said hearing;

c}) The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on
Respondent's behalf;

d} The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e} The right to further procedural steps except for those
specifically contained herein;

f) The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent
Order; and

g) Any objection that this Consent Order will be reported to the
National Practitioner Data Bank as well as posted on RIDOH's
public website.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING
RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this Complaint:

9, Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of Probation, starting the day
this Consent Order is last executed (the “Effective Date"),

10.Commencing with the Effective Date:

a} Respondent’s Nursing Assistant license will be subject to a two (2)
year period of probation and Respondent's license shall reflect the
status “Active Probation.”

b) Respondent must be employed at a hospital or nursing facility.

c} The probationary period will abate for the duration of any period in
which Respondent ceases to be employed as a Nursing Assistant.

d) Respondent will notify RIDOH of any lapse in employment and the
probation period will be extended until the required employment as
a Nursing Assistant is completed.
e) Respondent must provide RIDOH with the name and address of any
new employer(s) and the reason for said change.

f) Respondent shall provide his employer with a copy of this Consent
Order and a quarterly performance report form provided to him by
RIDOH.

g) When Respondent complies with the laws and regulations governing
the practice of Nursing Assistants during the period of probation and
with the requirements of this Consent Order, Respondent may apply
to RIDOH for an unrestricted license to practice as a Nursing Assistant.

11.Respondent acknowledges that:

a) this Consent Order is an agreement between Respondent and -
RIDOH;

b) RIDOH is relying upon Respondent’s compliance with these terms
and conditions as an inducement to enter into this Consent Order;
and

c) any violation or failure to adhere to the terms of this Consent Order
shall constitute unprofessional conduct and subject Respondent to
further disciplinary action at the discretion of RIDOH, including but
not limited to Suspension or Revocation.

12. This Consent Order shall become a permanent part of Respondent's

record maintained by RIDOH and a part of a public record of this
proceeding.
