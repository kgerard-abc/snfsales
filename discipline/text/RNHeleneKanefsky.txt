CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and the

regulations promulgated thereunder, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with the Board of

Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board’”), charging Helene Kanefsky (the

“Respondent”), a licensed registered nurse (RN license #36655), with a violation of Chapter 5-34
of the General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by an Investigating Committee of the Board with respect

to the allegations of unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the Board has made the following

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW:

. Respondent is licensed to practice as a registered nurse in the State of Rhode Island under

license RN36655.

. Respondent was employed as a nurse at Charlesgate Nursing Center, 100 Randall Street,

Providence, Rhode Island, on or about January 2018.

. Respondent was assigned to a patient on the evening of January 28, 2018 from 3:00PM —

11:00PM with diagnosis but not limited to diabetes.

. Respondent performed a blood sugar finger stick at 4:30PM; the results were 314 mg/dL.

. Patient presented as diaphoretic and lethargic; however, respondent omitted blood sugar

test at bedtime on January 28, 2018.

. The findings of fact described in paragraphs one (1) through five (5) herein constitutes

unprofessional conduct as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(6) which
includes (v) failure to maintain standards established by the nursing profession.
Specifically, the Standards of Practice, Standard 1: Assessment - Prioritizes data
collection based on the healthcare consumer’s immediate condition, or the anticipated

needs of the healthcare consumer or situation.
