CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to RI. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq, and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health ("RIDOH") has investigated Complaint No. C23-0243 (the “Complaint")
charging YURI NUNEZ DE TEJADA, Nursing Assistant license number NAS7417,
("Respondent") with violations of R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute,

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed fo practice in the State of
Rhode Island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA57417.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Resopondent was employed by Health
Care Services, 400 Reservoir Avenue, Providence, RI 02907.

3. On or about March 6, 2023, the Respondent clocked in and out of a work
shift knowing the client was out of the country, thereby committing fraud.

4, That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to Rl, Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and
the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
