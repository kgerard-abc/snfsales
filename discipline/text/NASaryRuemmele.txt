After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode
Iskand under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 31167 and as a Medication
Alde under license MAD 02099.

2, That on or about August 15, 2024, Respondent was scheduled to work at a nursing
facility as a medication aide.

3. That on or about August 15, 2024, Respondent arrived at the facility and was
informed that due to a staffing shortage she was required to work in her capacity
as a nursing assistant and not in her capacity as a medication aide. Instead of
working as a nursing assistant, Respondent left the facility without notifying
anyone, thus abandoning the residents of the facility who were in need of her
care.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq, and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated there under.
