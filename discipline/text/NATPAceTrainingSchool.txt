IN THE MATTER OF: ACE TRAINING SCHOOL, INC.!
License Number: NATP0000150

Complaint Number C25-0413

ORDER OF SUMMARY SUSPENSION
OF NURSING ASSISTANT TRAINING PROGRAM LICENSE

ACE TRAINING SCHOOL, INC., (ACE) was licensed as a nursing assistant training
program on April 21, 2022 by the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) pursuant to R.I.
Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and 216 RICR-40-05-22. This Order is issued pursuant to R.I.
Gen. Laws § 42-35-14.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. ACE is a nursing assistant training program licensed in Rhode Island under License Number
NATP00150 with a physical presence at 815 Reservoir Avenue, Cranston, Rhode Island.

2. Onor about April 22, 2025, RIDOH conducted an unannounced site visit at ACE in response
to a complaint that the program coordinator was a nursing assistant and that students were not
completing the required clinical hours. The site visit revealed the following:

a. 216-RICR-4005-22.7.1(E)(1) requires the program coordinator at a licensed nursing
assistant training program to be a registered nurse with a minimum of two years of nursing
experience, one of which must be in the provision of long term care services; and must
have documented evidence of teaching and/or appropriate supervisory experience. The
program coordinator at ACE was a nursing assistant and thus ACE is in violation of this
regulation.

b. 216-RICR-4005-22.7.1(H) requires a nursing assistant training program to have a
detailed course curriculum. ACE did not have a detailed course curriculum and thus ACE
is in violation of this regulation.

c. 216-RICR-40-05-22.12.2 requires students to complete minimum hours of practical
experience in a clinical setting. 216-RICR-40-05-22.7.1(G)(1)(d) requires a nursing
assistant training program to have service resources to provide the kinds of practical
learning experiences required. ACE could not demonstrate that it had the service resources
to provide its students with the minimal practical experience in a clinical setting and thus
ACE is in violation of this regulation

d. Students attending this nursing assistant training program training school are not
receiving the levels of training and education required in accordance with R.I. Gen. Laws
§ 23-17.9-1 et seq. and 216 RICR-40-05-22, and permitting ACE to continue to provide
substandard training to its students presents a risk of harm to the public.

3. After considering the above facts, the Director of the Rhode Island Department Health finds
that public health, safety, and welfare imperatively requires emergency action.

ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-35-14(c), Respondent’s nursing assistant training
program license is SUSPENDED until further order of the Rhode Island Department of Health.
Proceedings for revocation or other action shall be promptly instituted and determined.
