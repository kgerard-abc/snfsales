CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and §§ 40-05-3.14 and 10-05-4 of
volume 216 of the Rhode Island Code of Regulations, a.complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with
the Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Nicole Imbeault (the
“Respondent”), a licensed practical nurse (license LPN12216), with a violation of Chapter 5-34 of the
General Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by the Board with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of the Respondent, the following are and shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a practical nurse licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island under
License Number LPN12216.

2. That on or about June 25, 2024, Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) received a
complaint with allegations against the Respondent.

3. That on or about June 21, 2024, the Respondent was employed by a long-term care facility in
Rhode Island. On this date Respondent left at the end of their shift prior to being relieved by
another nurse leaving the patients without care. Additionally, the Respondent failed to perform a
proper narcotic count and left the narcotic keys in a unit desk drawer when they left the facility.

4, That on April 14, 2025, an Investigative Committee (IC) of the Board reviewed the allegations
and relevant evidence, including the Respondent’s response to the allegation. The IC made a
preliminary determination that the Respondent was guilty of unprofessional conduct by
abandoning patients without proper staff in place.

5. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) a herein constitutes unprofessional conduct as
defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(6)(i).
