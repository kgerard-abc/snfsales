CONSENT ORDER

VOLUNTARY SURRENDER OF NURSING ASSISTANT LICENSE

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 54294.

2. On November 12, 2021, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at an assisted

living facility in North Providence, Rhode Island.

3, On November 12, 2021, Respondent engaged in unprofessional conduct by striking a
patient in her care as depicted in a video clip recorded by a video camera installed at the
facility.

4, The conduct described in paragraph three constitutes conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8(3) and the Rules and Regulations

promulgated thereunder.

5. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this

complaint:
1, Respondent voluntarily surrenders her Nursing Assistant license.
2. This Consent Order shall become a public record.
