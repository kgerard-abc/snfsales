Pursuant to R.I. Gen, Laws § 23-17.9-1 ef seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (hereinafter “RIDOH”), and the

disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R.L. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration
by RIDOH and the Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall
constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode {sland under Nursing
Assistant License NA48798.

2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by Ocean State Nursing Services,

3. Respondent admits that on or about April 4, 2017, April 6, 2017, April 11, 2017, April
20, 2017, and April 25, 2017, she entered a patient's home when the patient was not
present, and fraudulently requested and received payment for services that she claimed
had been provided to the patient on those dates, when in fact no services had been

provided to the client on those dates.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
7. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a, The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent himself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree to the following disposition of this

complaint:

1, Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand on her Nursing
