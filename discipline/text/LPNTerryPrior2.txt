CONSENT ORDER.

Pursuant to § 5-34-24 of the Rhode Island General Laws and the Rules and Regulations

' promulgated there under, the Rhode Island Department of Health (the “Department”), and the ~~

Board of Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”) has investigated a complaint
charging Terry Prior, LPN09945 (“Respondent”) with a violation of Chapter 5-34 of the General
Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by an Investigating Committee of the Board, the Board made

the following Findings of Fact with respect to the professional conduct of Respondent:
. FINDINGS:
1. Respondent is a licensed practical nurse licensed to practice nursing in the State of Rhode

Island under practical nurse License Number LPN 09945.

2. At all pertinent times, Respondent was employed at Royal Westerly Nursing Home at 79
Beach Street in Westerly, Rhode Island.

3. On July 5, 2016, while on duty, Respondent took five (5) Benadryl tablets from Royal
Westerly Nursing Home for her own use.

4. The conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes unprofessional conduct as_
defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-24(6)(v) and the Rules and Regulations

promulgated there under.
