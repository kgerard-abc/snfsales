CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17,9-1 ef seq, and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health and the Nursing Assistant Advisory Board collectively, "RIDOH") have
investigated this Complaint charging Respondent, Carol Beckford, with a
violation of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct, the following are and shall constitute,

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1, That Respondent is licensed fo practice in Rhode Island: under
Nursing Assistant License NA36461.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by
Right At Home, 1444 Warwick Avenue, Warwick, RI 02888.

3. That during the course of her employment as nursing assistant
assigned to care for patient LL. in L.'s home, Respondent
committed fraud because she asked LL. to “clock” her in and
“clock" her out for the purpose of reporting to Respondent's
employer that Respondent had arrived or departed L.L.’s home,
when in fact, Respondent had not arrived or departed L.L.’s home,
including an occasion when L.L. clocked Respondent in and out
while Respondent was on vacation.

The Respondent denies the allegations described in paragraph three (3)
and does not admit to any wrongdoing in this matter.

That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and
the Rules and Regulations promulgated there under,

That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to
remain under the jurisdiction of RIDOH,
