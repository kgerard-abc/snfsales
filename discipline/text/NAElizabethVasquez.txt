CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated thereunder,
216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing Assistant Advisory
Board (collectively, “RIDOH”) have investigated Complaint No. C21-1746 (the “Complaint”)
charging Elizabeth Vasquez, Nursing Assistant license number NA 57202, (“Respondent”) with
violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9 -1 et seg.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional conduct
of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

. On or about October 12, 2021, Respondent was issued a 120-day temporary permit to work as

a nursing assistant pursuant to 216-RICR-40-05-22.5.4(D).

In December, 2021 Respondent was working as a nursing assistant at the home of E.K.

On December 28, 2021, Respondent admitted to being in possession of a debit card and
electronic benefits transfer card owned by E.K, and to have used the debit card to purchase
food for herself and was aware that her grandson had made purchases for himself with the debit
card.

The temporary license expired on February 12, 2022.

Respondent was issued nursing assistant license NA 57202 on July 11, 2022.

jurisdiction of RIDOH.

binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

Complaint.
