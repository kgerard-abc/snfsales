Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 etseg. and the Rules and Regulations

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. On or about May 19, 2025, Applicant applied to RIDOH to be licensed as a
nursing assistant.

2. RIDOH and Applicant agree to the following conditions regarding the
issuance of Applicant’s nursing assistant license.

3. Applicant admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

1. Respondent's Nursing Assistant license shall be subject to a two (2)-year
period of active probation and Respondent's license shall reflect the
status “Active Probation.”

2. Respondent must demonstrate two (2) years of employment as a nursing
assistant at a hospital or nursing facility with direct supervision. Respondent
may not work as a nursing assistant in a private home or anywhere else
without direct supervision.

3. If at any time Respondent is not employed as a nursing assistant, the
active probation period will be extended until the required two (2}-year
period of employment as a Nursing Assistant is completed.
