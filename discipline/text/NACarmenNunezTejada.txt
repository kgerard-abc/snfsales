CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health
(“RIDOH”) has investigated Complaint No. C25-1220 (the “Complaint”) charging CARMEN
NUNEZ TEJADA, nursing assistant license number NA 60022 (“Respondent”) with violations
of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Nursing Assistant License Number NA60022.

2. That on or about August 31, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant by a
staffing agency and was assigned to work at the homes of two patients.

3. That on or about August 31, 2025, Respondent failed to appear at the homes of the two patients
as scheduled, thus abandoning two patients who were in need of her care.

4. That on or about September 13, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant by a
staffing agency and was assigned to work at the home of a patient.

5. That on or about September 13, 2025, Respondent failed to appear at the home of a patient as
scheduled, thus abandoning a patient who was in need of her care.

6. That the conduct described in paragraphs three and five herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.
