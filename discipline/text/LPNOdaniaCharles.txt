CONSENT ORDER FOR VOLUNTARY SURRENDER

Charles Odania (“Respondent”) has been licensed as a Practical Nurse pursuant to R.I Gen. Laws
§5-34-11 and the Rules and Regulations for Licensing of Nurses 216-RICR-40-05-3 and holds
license numbers LPN121699 (the “License”).

The Respondent recognizes that allegations of professional misconduct have been submitted to the
Rhode Island Board of Nursing Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”) and hereby agrees
to voluntarily surrender their license effective immediately.

FINDINGS OF FACT
1, The Respondent holds a license (LPN12169) to practice as a practical nurse in the state of
Rhode Island.
2. That on or about July , 2025, the Respondent entered this voluntary surrender of their

license after failing or refusing to provide requested information relative to obtaining their
nursing school transcripts.

3. Respondent voluntarily submits this surrender of License to the Board of Nursing
Registration and Nursing Education and states that no promises or representations have
been made to her other than those terms and conditions stated herein.

4. That the Respondent has read and comprehended the Consent Order and understands
that this Consent Order shall be subject to final approval of the Board of Nursing
Registration and Nursing Education and is not binding on Respondent until final
ratification by the Board of Nursing Registration and Nursing Education.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

5. The Board of Nursing Registration and Nursing Education and Respondent hereby agree
