CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.Il, Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, "RIDOH") have investigated Complaint No, C22-0903
(the “Complaint") charging Hannah O. Langello, Temporary Nursing Assistant license
number NA33522-T, ("Respondent") with violations of R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Temporary Nursing Assistant licensed fo practice in the State of
Rhode Island under Temporary Nursing Assistant License Number NA33522-T.

2, On or about July 19, 2022, it was determined that the Respondent held herself out to be
a Nursing Assistant when responding to an on-line inquiry for such services, Af that time,
she held a Temporary Nursing Assistant license and was unable to represent herself as a
Nursing Assistant because she was not fully licensed.

3. That the conduct described in paragraph two (2) herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.l. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

4, That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
