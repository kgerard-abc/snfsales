CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of Health and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board (collectively, “RIDOH") have investigated Complaint No. C20-1166
(the “Complaint’) charging Talitha Lou Szpotanski, Nursing Assistant license number
NA41880, (“Respondent”) with violations of R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of unprofessional
conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rhode Island
under Nursing Assistant License Number NA41880.

2. That at all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed at Heatherwood Nursing
and Rehabilitation Center, 398 Bellevue Avenue, Newport, RI 02840.

3. That on or about August 9, 2020, Respondent admits that she used a gait belt to restrain
a resident to a chair.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3} herein constitutes unprofessional
conduct pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated there under.

5. That Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH and hereby agrees to remain under
the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. That Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

7, That Respondent understands the purpose of this Consent Order is solely to resolve the
Complaint.
