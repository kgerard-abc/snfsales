CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.J. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) and APRIL METTS (Respondent)
enter into this Consent Order.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Onor about December 20, 2018, Respondent applied to RIDOH to be licensed as a nursing

assistant.
2. On or about January 15, 2019, RIDOH denied the application.

3. Onor about February 19, 2019, Respondent requested an administrative hearing to appeal
‘the denial.

4. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
5. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
6. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:
a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;
b. The right to represent herself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s own
choosing at said hearing; |
c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf at
said hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.
ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED UPON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree as follows:

1. Upon signature and ratification of this Consent Order, RIDOH will issue a 120-day
temporary nursing assistant license to Respondent.

2. For the first 365 days that Respondent holds a 120-day temporary nursing assistant
license or a nursing assistant license, and not including any days on which Respondent
does not hold a 120-day temporary nursing assistant license or a nursing assistant license,
if any, Respondent will be required to work under direct supervision of a licensed nurse
in a facility and may not work under indirect supervision in a private home.

3. For the first 365 days that Respondent holds a 120-day temporary nursing assistant
license or a nursing assistant license, and not including any days on which Respondent
does not hold a 120-day temporary nursing assistant license or a nursing assistant license,
if any, Respondent will be on probation and shall ensure that her employer submits
quarterly Job Performance Progress Reports to RIDOH.

4. Failure to comply with the requirement of paragraphs 2 and 3 above may result in

additional disciplinary action.

This Consent Order shall become a public record.
