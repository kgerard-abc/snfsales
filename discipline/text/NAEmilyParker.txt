IN THE MATTER OF: EMILY PARKER

LICENSE NUMBER: NA18244
COMPLAINT NUMBER: C25-0889

CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated a complaint
alleging unprofessional conduct by EMILY PARKER (“Respondent”). After review and
consideration by RIDOH, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a nursing assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA18244.

2. On or about July 14, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at an assisted
living facility.

3. On or about July 14, 2025, Respondent spoke in a loud and disrespectful manner to a
resident sitting in the dining room, stating that that she would not pick up the resident’s
dish after the resident had completed her meal because the resident had previously
reported that she, the resident, did not want the Respondent around her, and the
Respondent told the resident that she did not want to be around the resident or she would
be fired.

4. The conduct described in paragraph three constitutes unprofessional conduct pursuant to
R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

5. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, effective the day this Consent
Order is last executed.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

complaint against her license.
