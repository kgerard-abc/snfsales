CONSENT ORDER FOR ACTIVE PROBATION

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (““RIDOH”), has investigated this complaint
regarding allegations of unprofessional conduct in violation of R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et
seq. against KELLY FANNING (“Respondent”), and after review and consideration by RIDOH
with respect to the allegations of unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and
shall constitute:

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 24510 (the “License”). Respondent was issued her License on
August 26, 1998.

2. On or about November 8, 1998, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH)
received a complaint (#2-0699A, B, the “Complaint”), alleging that, while Respondent
was employed as a nursing home assistant, she abused residents.

3. On or about April 28, 2000, Respondent and RIDOH entered into a consent order (“First
Consent Order’’) to resolve the Complaint against her License.

4. Under the terms of the First Consent Order, Respondent’s License was subject to a
suspension, to be stayed for a two (2) year period of probation. Under the terms of the First
Consent Order, non-compliance by Respondent with the terms of her probation would
subject her to immediate suspension of her License.

5. On or about March 15, 2002, Respondents License was indefinitely suspended due to
noncompliance with the terms of her probation.

6. Following the suspension of her License in 2002, Respondent was without an active
License for more than 23 years.
7. On or about January 10, 2024, Respondent completed a nursing assistant training program
and successfully passed the state examinations.

8. On May 15, 2025, Respondent appeared before the Nursing Assistant Advisory Board
(“Board”) to request that the Board reinstate her License.

9. Following its review, the Board granted Respondent’s request to reinstate her License,
subject to a six-month probation.

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

10. The conduct described in Paragraph 2 above (abuse of residents) constitutes
unprofessional conduct under Rhode Island Gen. Laws §§ 23-17.9-8(2) and 23-17.9-8(5)
and Section 22.6(A)(5) of the regulations governing nursing assistants, Nursing
Assistants, Medication Aides, and the Approval of Nursing Assistant.and Medication Aide
Training Programs, 216-RICR-40-05-22.

Whereas Respondent wishes to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing Assistant License NA
24510 and agrees to voluntarily enter into this Consent Order in order to resolve the Complaint
against her License, and whereas RIDOH finds the within disposition protective of the public
health, safety, and welfare, and for good cause shown, RIDOH and Respondent hereby agree to
the following disposition of the Complaint.

ACCORDINGLY, IT IS THEREFORE AGREED AND ORDERED:

11. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a six (6) month of Active Probation, effective
the day this Consent Order is last executed, under the terms and condition as follows:

a. Respondent must work as a nursing assistant at a hospital or nursing facility with
direct supervision for a period of six (6) months. Respondent may not work as a
nursing assistant in a private home or anywhere without direct supervision.

b. Respondent’s license status shall be listed as “Administrative Inactive” until
Respondent provides RIDOH with documentation from a hospital or nursing facility
stating that the hospital or facility employs or intends to employ Respondent as a
nursing assistant. Upon receipt of this documentation from Respondent or the
hospital or facility, Respondent’s licensing status will be changed to “Active
Probation.”

c. IfRespondent changes jobs during her probationary period, Respondent must
provide RIDOH with the name of the new hospital or nursing facility where she is
employed within two weeks of starting employment.
d. Respondent must complete, as part of their facility training, in-service hours on the
topics of abuse and neglect, professional conduct and resident’s rights.

e. Respondent must provide all hospitals and/or nursing facilities where she is employed
as a nursing assistant with a copy of this Consent Order and a Job Performance
Progress Report form to be provided to her by RIDOH.

f. Respondent will ensure that Respondent’s employer provides Job Performance
Progress Reports to RIDOH. The due dates of Respondent’s quarterly reports are as
follows:

1 Quarter Report (January —March) due by April 15th each year

2nd Quarter Report (April-June) due by July 15th each year

3rd Quarter Report (July-September) due by October 15th each year
4th Quarter Report (October-December) due by. January 15th each year

g. Respondent will permit RIDOH to communicate with Respondent’s employer(s) or
potential employers regarding her employment.

h. Respondent understands that if she fails to cause her employer to timely submit
Respondent’s Job Performance Progress Report to RIDOH, that is, within four (4)
weeks of the date it is due to RIDOH, RIDOH shall be entitled to presume that
Respondent is not employed as a nursing assistant and will change Respondent’s
license status to “Administrative Inactive” until proof of employment or future
employment is received by RIDOH.

12. After Respondent completes six (6) months of employment as a nursing assistant at a
hospital or nursing facility with the status of Active Probation, Respondent may apply to
RIDOH for her license status to be changed to “Active.”

13. Any violation of, or failure to adhere to, the terms of this Consent Order shall constitute
unprofessional conduct and subject Respondent to further disciplinary action at the
discretion of RIDOH, including but not limited to revocation of Respondent’s License.

14, Respondent understands and agrees that her formal discharge from this Consent Order
shall take place only upon her successful completion of the terms and conditions stated
herein and by approval of RIDOH.

15. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.

16. Respondent understands that the purpose of this Consent Order is to resolve the
Complaint against her License.
17. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

a. The right to have an administrative hearing on this matter;

b. The right to represent oneself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s
own choosing at said hearing;

c. The right to present testimony, evidence and witnesses on Respondent’s behalf;

d. The right to cross-examine witnesses presented by RIDOH;

e. The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained
herein;

f. The right to take an appeal from the terms of this Consent Order; and

g. Any objection that this Consent Order will (i) be reported to the National
Practitioner Data Bank, (ii) become a permanent part of Respondent’s record
maintained by RIDOH and a part of the public record of this proceeding, or (iii)
be posted on RIDOH’s public website.
