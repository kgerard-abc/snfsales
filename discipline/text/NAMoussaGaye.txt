CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH), and the Nursing
Assistant Advisory Board have investigated a complaint charging MOUSSA GAYE, NA39811
(hereinafter “Respondent”), with conduct subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R.I.
Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-8. After review and consideration by RIDOH and the Nursing Assistant
Advisory Board with respect to the Respondent’s conduct, the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA39811.

2. At all times relevant hereto, Respondent was employed by the Rhode Island Department
of Behavioral Healthcare, Developmental Disabilities and Hospitals, Eleanor Slater

Hospital, Zambarano Unit.

3. That on or about January 28, 2017, Respondent answered a telephone call on his personal

cell phone while a patient in his care was in a bathtub filled with water.

4, That the conduct described in paragraphs three of this Consent Order constitutes conduct
subject to disciplinary proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8 and the Rules
and Regulations promulgated thereunder.

5. That the Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.

7. That Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:

The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;

own choosing at said hearing;

The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf;
at a hearing;

The right to further procedural steps except for those specifically contained
herein; and

Any and all rights of appeal of this Consent Order.
