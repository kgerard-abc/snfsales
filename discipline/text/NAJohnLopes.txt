CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seq. and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder, 216-RICR-40-05-22, the Rhode Island Department of
Health (“RIDOH") has investigated a complaint charging JOHN LOPES,

Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq.

After review and consideration by RIDOH with respect to the allegations of
unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the following are and shall constitute

1. That Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in the State of Rnode
island under Nursing Assistant License Number NA 29409.

by a nursing facility.

patient in his care.

4. That the conduct described in paragraph three (3) herein constitutes
unprofessional conduct pursuant to R.|. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules
