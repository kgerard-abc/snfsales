CONSENT ORDER FOR REPRIMAND

Pursuant to R.[. Gen Laws §23-17.9-1 et seg. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (“RIDOH”) has investigated a complaint
alleging unprofessional conduct by JANE DAVIS (Respondent). After review and consideration
by RIDOH the following are and shall constitute

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a nursing assistant, licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing
Assistant License NA 05509.

2. On or about September 5, 2025, Respondent was employed as a nursing assistant at a
hospital facility.

3. On or about September 5, 2025, Respondent was struck by a patient and responded in a
manner that was inconsistent with safe management techniques on which she has been

trained, and as a result, Respondent comprised the patient’s dignity and safety.

4. The conduct described in paragraph three constitutes unprofessional conduct.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED ON THE FOREGOING

5. Respondent is hereby issued the sanction of a Reprimand, effective the day this Consent
Order is last executed.

6. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by Respondent and RIDOH.
7. Respondent understands that the purpose of this Consent Order is to resolve the
complaint against her license.
