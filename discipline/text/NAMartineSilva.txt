CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-17.9-1 ef seq. and the Rules and Regulations promulgated
thereunder, the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) and Martine Silva (Respondent)
enter into this Consent Order.

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Respondent is a Nursing Assistant licensed to practice in Rhode Island under Nursing

Assistant License NA 50361.

2. On or about December 18, 2018, Respondent entered a plea of nolo contendre in the
Providence County Superior Court to two counts of giving a false document to an agent,
employee, or public official in the case captioned P2-2018-0100(F), State of Rhode
Island v. Martine Silva.

3. The conduct described in paragraph 2 constitutes conduct subject to disciplinary
proceedings pursuant to R. I. Gen. Laws §23-17.9-8(3) and the Rules and Regulations
promulgated thereunder.

4. Respondent admits to the jurisdiction of RIDOH.
5. Respondent has read this Consent Order and understands that this Consent Order shall
become binding once signed by the Respondent and RIDOH.
6. Respondent hereby acknowledges and waives:
a. The right to an administrative hearing on this matter;
b. The right to represent herself or be represented by an attorney of Respondent’s own

choosing at said hearing;
c. The right to produce testimony, witnesses, and evidence on Respondent’s behalf at
said hearing; and

d. Any and all rights of appeal.

ACCORDINGLY, AND BASED UPON THE FOREGOING

RIDOH and the Respondent hereby agree as follows:

Respondent’s nursing assistant license is suspended for a period of ONE YEAR from the date of

ratification of this Consent Order

This Consent Order shall become a public record.
