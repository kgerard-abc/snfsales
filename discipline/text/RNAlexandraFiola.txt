CONSENT ORDER

Pursuant to Rhode Island Gen. Laws § 5-34-25 of the Rhode Island General Laws and the
regulations promulgated thereunder, a complaint (the “Complaint”) was filed with the Board of
Nurse Registration and Nursing Education (the “Board”), charging Alexandra Fiola (the
“Respondent”), a licensed registered nurse (license #57390), with a violation of Chapter 5-34 of
the Genera! Laws of the State of Rhode Island.

After review and consideration by an Investigating Committee of the Board with respect

to the allegations of unprofessional conduct of Respondent, the Board has made the following:

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW:

1. Respondent is licensed to practice as a registered nurse in the State of Rhode Island
under license RN 57390.

2. Respondent was a student in the Memorial Hospital of Rhode Island School of Nurse
Anesthesia Program at Kent County Hospital, Warwick, Rhode Island, on or about
March 14, 2018.

3. Respondent was functioning as a student registered nurse anesthetist in the operating
room and was present during the universal timeout process at the start of the right
partial nephrectomy.

4. Respondent acknowledges that she was attending to her patient duties as a student
registered nurse anesthetist for this patient and did not speak up during the time out
process for time out verification of the operative site immediately prior to the right
partial nephrectomy in question.

5. The conduct described in combined paragraphs three (3) through four (4) herein
constitutes unprofessional conduct as defined in Rhode Island Gen. Laws 5-34-
24(6)(v).
