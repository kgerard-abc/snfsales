Greenwood Center

Nursing home in Warwick, Rhode Island

Who has run this home, and what happened

Newest first. An owner’s time starts on the date it took over management, if a management agreement came before the sale, or otherwise the date it took over ownership. Star ratings are from the last CMS file before that date and the latest file, or the last file before it left. A Special Focus Facility counts as 0 stars. Immediate jeopardy is CMS’s most serious level: a problem that caused, or was likely to cause, serious injury, harm, impairment, or death. Actual harm is the level below it: a resident was hurt, but not at the immediate jeopardy level.

Mayflower

Mayflower took over ownership December 4, 2025. Still in charge as of October 2026 (0.8 years so far).

Overall stars (5 is best): 2 in the last CMS file before it took over ownership, 1 now (down 1 star).

While it ran the home: 2 immediate jeopardy citations, 2 actual harm citations, and 2 federal fines totaling $124,992.

Genesis Healthcare

Genesis Healthcare took over ownership November 30, 2012. Ran it until December 4, 2025, when Mayflower took over ownership.

Overall stars (5 is best): 2 when it left. The CMS files on this site have no rating from before it took over ownership.

While it ran the home: 1 immediate jeopardy citation, 2 actual harm citations, and 3 federal fines totaling $41,022.

Citations and fines counted from April 2016. The CMS files on this site start in April 2019 and list inspections from about 3 years before that.
