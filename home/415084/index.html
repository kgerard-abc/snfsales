Newest first. An owner’s time starts when it took over management, if a management agreement came before the sale, otherwise when it took over ownership. Marquis/Tryko

Marquis/Tryko took over ownership December 13, 2016. Still in charge as of October 2026 (9.8 years so far).

Overall stars (5 is best): 2 now. No rating from before it took over is in the CMS files on this site.

While it ran the home: 7 immediate jeopardy citations, 13 citations for harm to a resident, and 11 federal fines totaling $701,684.

CharterCARE / Prospect

CharterCARE / Prospect took over ownership July 17, 2009. Ran it until December 13, 2016, when Marquis/Tryko took over ownership.

While it ran the home: 0 immediate jeopardy citations, 0 citations for harm to a resident, and no federal fines.

Counted from April 2016, when the CMS files on this site begin.
