Who has run this home, and what happened

Newest first. An owner’s time starts on the date it took over management, if a management agreement came before the sale, or otherwise the date it took over ownership. Star ratings are from the last CMS file before that date and the latest file, or the last file before it left. A Special Focus Facility counts as 0 stars. Immediate jeopardy is CMS’s most serious level: a problem that caused, or was likely to cause, serious injury, harm, impairment, or death. Actual harm is the level below it: a resident was hurt, but not at the immediate jeopardy level.

AdviniaCare

AdviniaCare took over management February 1, 2023, and took over ownership when the sale was approved on February 1, 2025. Still in charge as of October 2026 (3.7 years so far).

Overall stars (5 is best): 1 in the last CMS file before it took over management, 2 now (up 1 star).

While it ran the home: 1 immediate jeopardy citation, 3 actual harm citations, and 3 federal fines totaling $33,151.

Centers Health Care

Centers Health Care took over ownership June 22, 2017. Ran it until February 1, 2023, when AdviniaCare took over management (5.6 years).

Overall stars (5 is best): 1 when it left. The CMS files on this site have no rating from before it took over ownership.

While it ran the home: 5 immediate jeopardy citations, 4 actual harm citations, and 6 federal fines totaling $114,414.

NSL Holdings

NSL Holdings took over ownership June 22, 2016. Ran it until June 22, 2017, when Centers Health Care took over ownership (1.0 years).

While it ran the home: no immediate jeopardy or actual harm citations and no federal fines.

Centers Health Care

Centers Health Care took over ownership August 1, 2014. Ran it until June 22, 2016, when NSL Holdings took over ownership.

While it ran the home: no immediate jeopardy or actual harm citations and no federal fines.

Citations and fines counted from April 2016. The CMS files on this site start in April 2019 and list inspections from about 3 years before that.
