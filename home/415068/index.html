Warren Center

Nursing home in Warren, Rhode Island

A sale to AdviniaCare is pending with the state (CEC-132). AdviniaCare has managed the home since July 1, 2026.

Who has run this home, and what happened

Newest first. An owner’s time starts on the date it took over management, if a management agreement came before the sale, or otherwise the date it took over ownership. Star ratings are from the last CMS file before that date and the latest file, or the last file before it left. A Special Focus Facility counts as 0 stars. Immediate jeopardy is CMS’s most serious level: a problem that caused, or was likely to cause, serious injury, harm, impairment, or death. Actual harm is the level below it: a resident was hurt, but not at the immediate jeopardy level.

AdviniaCare

AdviniaCare took over management July 1, 2026, while its purchase is pending with the state. Still in charge as of October 2026 (0.3 years so far).

Overall stars (5 is best): 4 in the last CMS file before it took over management, 4 now (no change).

While it ran the home: no immediate jeopardy or actual harm citations and no federal fines.

Mayflower

Mayflower took over management January 20, 2024, and took over ownership when the sale was approved on May 23, 2025. Ran it until July 1, 2026, when AdviniaCare took over management (2.4 years).

Overall stars (5 is best): 5 in the last CMS file before it took over management, 4 when it left (down 1 star).

While it ran the home: no immediate jeopardy or actual harm citations and no federal fines.

Genesis Healthcare

Genesis Healthcare took over ownership January 28, 2009. Ran it until January 20, 2024, when Mayflower took over management.

Overall stars (5 is best): 5 when it left. The CMS files on this site have no rating from before it took over ownership.

While it ran the home: 1 actual harm citation and 1 federal fine totaling $27,528.

Citations and fines counted from April 2016. The CMS files on this site start in April 2019 and list inspections from about 3 years before that.
