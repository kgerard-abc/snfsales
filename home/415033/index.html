Who has run this home, and what happened

Newest first. An owner’s time starts on the date it took over management, if a management agreement came before the sale, or otherwise the date it took over ownership. Star ratings are from the last CMS file before that date and the latest file, or the last file before it left. A Special Focus Facility counts as 0 stars.

AdviniaCare

AdviniaCare took over ownership November 25, 2025. Still in charge as of October 2026 (0.9 years so far).

Overall stars (5 is best): 2 in the last CMS file before it took over ownership, 1 now (down 1 star).

While it ran the home: no citations for harm to a resident or immediate jeopardy, and no federal fines.

Athena Health Care Systems

Athena Health Care Systems took over ownership April 25, 2014. Ran it until November 25, 2025, when AdviniaCare took over ownership.

Overall stars (5 is best): 2 when it left. The CMS files on this site have no rating from before it took over ownership.

While it ran the home: 2 citations for harm to a resident or immediate jeopardy, 1 of them immediate jeopardy, and 1 federal fine totaling $16,801.

Citations and fines counted from April 2016. The CMS files on this site start in April 2019 and list inspections from about 3 years before that.
