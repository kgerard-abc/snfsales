Newest first. An owner’s time starts when it took over management, if a management agreement came before the sale, otherwise when it took over ownership. AdviniaCare

AdviniaCare took over management February 1, 2023, before the sale was approved on February 1, 2025. Still in charge as of October 2026 (3.7 years so far).

Overall stars (5 is best): 1 when it took over, 2 now (up 1 star).

While it ran the home: 2 immediate jeopardy citations, 4 citations for harm to a resident, and 3 federal fines totaling $67,874.

Centers Health Care

Centers Health Care took over ownership November 23, 2015. Ran it until February 1, 2023, when AdviniaCare took over management.

Overall stars (5 is best): 1 when it left. No rating from before it took over is in the CMS files on this site.

While it ran the home: 4 immediate jeopardy citations, 9 citations for harm to a resident, and 7 federal fines totaling $472,777.

Counted from April 2016, when the CMS files on this site begin.
