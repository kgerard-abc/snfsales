Pawtucket Falls Healthcare Center

Nursing home in Pawtucket, Rhode Island

Who has run this home, and what happened

Newest first. An owner’s time starts on the date it took over management, if a management agreement came before the sale, or otherwise the date it took over ownership. Star ratings are from the last CMS file before that date and the latest file, or the last file before it left. A Special Focus Facility counts as 0 stars.

Cch Healthcare/Mayers

Cch Healthcare/Mayers took over ownership January 1, 2024. Still in charge as of October 2026 (2.8 years so far).

Overall stars (5 is best): 1 in the last CMS file before it took over ownership, 1 now (no change).

While it ran the home: 3 citations for harm to a resident or immediate jeopardy, 2 of them immediate jeopardy, and 1 federal fine totaling $193,160.

Starlight Healthcare

Starlight Healthcare took over ownership December 23, 2020. Ran it until January 1, 2024, when Cch Healthcare/Mayers took over ownership (3.0 years).

Overall stars (5 is best): 4 in the last CMS file before it took over ownership, 1 when it left (down 3 stars).

While it ran the home: 7 citations for harm to a resident or immediate jeopardy, 4 of them immediate jeopardy, and 8 federal fines totaling $191,506.

Genesis Healthcare

Genesis Healthcare took over ownership January 28, 2009. Ran it until December 23, 2020, when Starlight Healthcare took over ownership.

Overall stars (5 is best): 4 when it left. The CMS files on this site have no rating from before it took over ownership.

While it ran the home: 1 citation for harm to a resident, and 1 federal fine totaling $7,803.

Citations and fines counted from April 2016. The CMS files on this site start in April 2019 and list inspections from about 3 years before that.
